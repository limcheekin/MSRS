Farrell is the young, impulsive navigator of the Terran Reclamations crew aboard the ship *Marco Four*. As the team approaches Alphard Six, a planet once surveyed and seeded by humans but later invaded by the Hymenop aliens, Farrell's eagerness to land and explore clashes with the cautious warnings of Captain Stryker and engineer Gibson. Despite his impatience, he ultimately agrees to lead a reconnaissance mission, stepping into the helihopper to investigate the planet’s surface.

What unfolds is a dramatic and unsettling encounter: Farrell's ship is destroyed when it crashes into an electrically charged power line, and he is captured and imprisoned in the grounded spaceship. There, he discovers a hidden colony of humans who have lived in isolation for centuries, believing themselves to be descendants of a lost Terran expedition that failed during a voyage to Sirius. These people were conditioned by the Hymenop invaders, who used them as test subjects to study human behavior and survival in alien environments. The Hymenops had left the ship derelict, but the colonists grew up believing it was their ancestral home.

Farrell’s journey culminates in a daring escape, during which he realizes the true nature of the inhabitants: they are not aliens or descendants of the original crew, but humans who have been psychologically conditioned for generations to believe they are part of a failed interstellar mission. The story ends with Farrell understanding that the Bees' decision to leave them alone was part of a controlled experiment to observe human resilience and adaptability—an ironic reversal of the old saying that Terrans cannot understand alien cultures, as the Hymenops themselves never truly understood humanity.
