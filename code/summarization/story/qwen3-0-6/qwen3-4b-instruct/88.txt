The setting of the story following a young cadet preparing for a historic journey into space amidst personal and familial anxieties is the U.S. Academy of Interplanetary Flight in 1995, during the final days of the graduating class. The narrative unfolds as the cadets prepare to embark on their first deep-space missions, with the story centering on Ben, a young man who must confront both his personal feelings for Laura—the woman he loves—and the immense responsibilities and fears associated with space travel. The setting blends Earth-based preparation—such as physical exams, classroom instruction, and family reunions—with the looming reality of departure. The cadets gather at a New Mexican beach to celebrate their graduation, where they are surrounded by families and mentors like Robert Chandler, who has already achieved feats on Mars and Venus. This juxtaposition of human connection and interplanetary ambition underscores the emotional weight of the journey. The story emphasizes the personal anxieties of leaving Earth, the fear of failure or loss, and the tension between romantic love and the demands of pioneering space exploration. Thus, the setting is both a physical academy and a symbolic threshold between Earth-bound life and the unknown reaches of space.
