Mr. Michaelson is an archeologist from Earth who has traveled to Alpha Centaurus II to explore a mysterious, ancient city that dates back half a million years. He is portrayed as intelligent, curious, and deeply introspective, driven by a desire to understand the advanced technology and civilization that once thrived there. His personality is marked by a blend of rationality and wonder—while he is initially skeptical of supernatural claims, such as the spirits of the city or the talking book, he becomes increasingly captivated by the mysteries he encounters. Michaelson demonstrates perseverance, patience, and emotional depth, particularly in his dedication to studying the city’s artifacts, including the enigmatic "clock" and the books that speak to him. Though he begins with a sense of isolation—having lost his wife and being alone in a foreign, decaying world—he gradually develops a profound connection to the ancient people and their legacy. His actions reflect both scientific inquiry and spiritual sensitivity, as he ultimately realizes that death is not final, and that consciousness can transcend physical form. Michaelson is thus a thoughtful, resilient, and emotionally complex figure who embodies the tension between human rationality and the awe of encountering something beyond understanding.
