Captain Midas is a retired spaceman and the central figure in a tale of space exploration and the pursuit of treasure. Though he appears as an old, frail man in a hospital bed, his nickname—Captain Midas—derives from the mythological figure known for turning things into gold, symbolizing his obsession with wealth. In the story, Captain Midas once led a mission to recover a derelict spacecraft floating in space that contained vast amounts of gold, a treasure beyond imagination. However, he later reveals that the treasure is cursed and comes with immense danger and personal cost. His physical decline, gray hair, and deteriorating health reflect both the toll of age and the psychological burden of his past actions. The story emphasizes that the pursuit of treasure in space—like in ancient times—is driven by greed and a desire for power, even if it leads to moral and existential ruin. Captain Midas embodies the tragic irony of those who seek wealth in the void: they are rich in gold but poor in peace, and their legacy is one of sorrow, not triumph. His character serves as a cautionary tale about the dangers of unchecked ambition in the face of the unknown.
