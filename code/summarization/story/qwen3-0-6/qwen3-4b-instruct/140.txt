The story revolves around Kev Faraday, a character who struggles with a lack of psi abilities in a family renowned for their telepathic and psychic talents. Unlike his siblings and parents—who possess strong telekinetic, telepathic, and other advanced powers—Kev is a "psi-negative," unable to receive or project telepathic signals. This makes him feel like an outsider, both within his family and in society, where psi abilities are highly valued and considered essential for progress and survival.

As the story unfolds, Kev's isolation deepens when he learns of a starship returning from Alpha Centauri with reports of hostile alien life forms. The family, prepared for potential war, relies on their psychic powers for defense strategies, further emphasizing Kev’s inadequacy. However, the narrative takes a turn when Kev encounters a man claiming to be an alien from Wazzenazz XIII—a planet identical to Earth. This encounter, initially dismissed as a con, leads Kev to question his reality and his place in the world. His skepticism is challenged when he receives a letter from a "not-world" being, suggesting that Earth's own psychic powers may have been suppressed by human arrogance, and that beings like Kev might be key to understanding or countering alien threats.

Ultimately, the plot reveals that Kev’s lack of abilities is not a flaw but a unique perspective—one that allows him to see beyond the illusions of power and into deeper truths. His journey culminates in a confrontation with an alien race, where his inability to use psychic powers becomes a source of strength, leading to unexpected revelations about identity, humanity, and the nature of existence. The story suggests that true power lies not in psychic dominance, but in empathy, observation, and the courage to question established norms.
