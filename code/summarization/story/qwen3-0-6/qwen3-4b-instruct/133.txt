It is important to Jimmie that John continue playing with the band because John’s music is the emotional and musical heart of their performances. Despite John’s strange, otherworldly demeanor and his obsession with "holes" and time dimensions, his playing brings an unmatched depth, richness, and vibrancy to the band’s sound. As Jimmie observes, “The tones that surged from that monstrous bass were ecstatic... they held a depth and a volume and a richness that were astounding.” This music transforms the atmosphere of the club, turning customers into "weary warriors getting their first glimpse of Valhalla" and creating an experience that is both magical and unforgettable. Without John’s music, the band becomes “notes in a lost chord,” losing not only their sound but their connection with their audience. His presence, even if fleeting or unstable, is essential to their identity and success. For Jimmie, John’s music represents more than performance—it is art, hope, and a vital emotional anchor that sustains the band through their struggles. Thus, ensuring John stays is not just about securing a gig, but preserving the soul of their music.
