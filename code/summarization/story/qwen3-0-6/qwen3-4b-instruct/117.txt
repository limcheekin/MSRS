Captain Dylan is a veteran soldier and former outcast who has spent thirty years drifting through the outer edges of space, waiting for a war to begin. Though not a fighter by nature, he is appointed captain during a crisis in a remote colony on Lupus V, where an alien threat looms and the colony is dangerously unprepared. Dylan is tasked with evacuating the settlers, but discovers that a buried bomb wire—cut and reburied—has been tampered with, suggesting intelligent, possibly alien interference. He deduces that a being or entity aware of the colony’s vulnerability deliberately cut the wire to prevent destruction, indicating a deliberate act of protection rather than attack. However, the situation spirals when communication with the sister colony at Planet Three fails completely. Dylan learns that Lieutenant Bossio, who was supposed to assist in the evacuation, has not responded—and ultimately, it is revealed that Bossio is dead. This devastating loss underscores Dylan’s isolation and the futility of human efforts in the face of an unknown, possibly non-biological threat. In the end, Dylan chooses to stay behind to protect the colony, knowing he must sacrifice himself to save those he never truly knew. His journey from a cynical, disillusioned soldier to one who acts with quiet determination and sorrow highlights the cost of war and the fragile bonds between humans in the face of existential danger.
