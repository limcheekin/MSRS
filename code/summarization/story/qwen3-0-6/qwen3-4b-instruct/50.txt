The plot of the story involving a group of people stranded on a distant celestial body revolves around a team of Terran explorers who land on Alphard Six, a planet previously colonized and later abandoned by the Bees during their invasion. The crew—navigator Farrell, captain Stryker, and engineer Gibson—arrive on the planet as part of a Reclamations mission to locate lost human colonies. Upon arrival, they are immediately threatened when a scoutship explodes near their vessel, prompting suspicion and fear. A subsequent reconnaissance reveals a large, grounded spaceship at the center of a city, which appears to be an ancient Terran artifact powered by continuous atomic fission—technology that should not have existed in such a primitive form. The team debates whether the inhabitants are survivors of a lost colony, Hymenop experiment subjects, or an alien race from beyond Terran reach. As they investigate, they discover that the planet is home to a group of people who believe themselves to be descendants of a long-lost expedition that failed centuries ago. These individuals were conditioned by the Hymenop invaders to accept a false history and have lived in isolation for over a thousand years, unaware of their true origins. The plot culminates when Farrell, during a daring descent, is captured and imprisoned in the ship's lower levels. After escaping, he realizes that the group has been successfully preserved through psychological conditioning, and that the entire planet is a controlled experiment in human survival and adaptation. The story ultimately reveals that the people of Alphard Six are not aliens or survivors of a disaster, but a carefully maintained human society, demonstrating how isolation and belief systems can shape reality itself.
