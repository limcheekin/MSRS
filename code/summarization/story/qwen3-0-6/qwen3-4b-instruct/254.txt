The setting of the story involving a man intrigued by messages hidden in books and a mysterious girl he encounters in a library is primarily a modern, urban environment centered around a library and its surrounding streets. The narrative unfolds in a city where the protagonist, Quidley, observes the mysterious girl, Kay, slipping hidden messages into books at the library, particularly in the section housing Taine’s *History*. The setting transitions to streets and alleyways as Quidley follows her, eventually leading to a brick apartment building where she lives. The atmosphere blends the mundane with the secretive—libraries, convertibles, coffee bars, and residential neighborhoods—creating an environment of quiet suspense and hidden codes. The story's setting is both literal and symbolic: the library represents knowledge and secrecy, while the city streets symbolize pursuit and discovery. The urban backdrop emphasizes the contrast between public order and private intrigue, grounding the mysterious code-based plot in a realistic, everyday world. The final revelation that "her folks" are involved underscores the domestic and personal dimensions of the secret society, shifting the setting from an abstract mystery to a grounded, intimate space of family and identity.
