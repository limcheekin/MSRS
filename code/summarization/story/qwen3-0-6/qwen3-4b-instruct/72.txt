The setting of the story where a man battles a notorious space pirate while trapped on a desolate planet is the barren, lifeless planet Alpha Centauri III. This world is described as the most barren planet in space, featuring a flat, rocky surface with no vegetation or signs of life. The only notable features are scattered lakes, such as the tiny Centaurian lake where Starrett Blade hides after his ship crashes. These lakes are isolated and surrounded by desolate, unyielding terrain. The atmosphere is hostile and silent, with no wind, no sound beyond natural ripples, and a complete absence of life or movement. The planet has no moon, no running water, and no natural energy sources, contributing to its extreme desolation. The environment is harsh and unforgiving, emphasizing the isolation and danger faced by the lone survivor, Starrett Blade, who must rely solely on his wits and weapons to survive while evading the relentless pursuit of the feared space pirate, Devil Garrett. The setting amplifies the tension and solitude of the protagonist’s struggle for survival.
