The setting of the story revolves around the planet Alphard Six, a distant world located in a region beyond the former limits of Terran expansion. This planet is described as a barren, uninhabited world with a single moon, initially appearing as a "cool green disk" in contrast to the arid and swampy environments of earlier Terran worlds. The story unfolds aboard the *Marco Four*, a Reclamations ship on a mission to locate abandoned Terran colonies that were previously enslaved by the alien Hymenop invaders. Alphard Six, though never colonized before the Hymenop invasion, is now being explored due to its potential for hidden human survivors or remnants of a long-lost colony. The planet's surface reveals a large central city and twenty-seven agrarian hamlets, all powered by a primitive, atomic-fission-based ship that appears to have been grounded for centuries. The setting is both alien and mysterious, blending elements of sci-fi exploration with psychological tension. The world is not only physically unfamiliar but also culturally and historically enigmatic, as the inhabitants appear to be human descendants of a failed interstellar expedition—conditioned by the Hymenops to believe they were the rightful heirs of a lost voyage. This creates a paradoxical, eerie atmosphere where the setting serves as a crucible for themes of identity, truth, and the dangers of mistaken reality. The isolation of Alphard Six amplifies the sense of uncertainty, making it a place where danger is not just external but deeply embedded in the fabric of human history and perception.
