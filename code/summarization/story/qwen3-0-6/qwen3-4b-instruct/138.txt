The President's communications to the public are deliberately deceptive and emotionally manipulative, designed to preserve societal stability. Upon learning of Earth's devastation and the truth about violent death, he refuses to reveal the full reality to the people, fearing mass hysteria and psychological collapse. He alters the report from Michael and Mary, omitting the horrifying truth that violent death was once a terrifying possibility, and instead presents a sanitized version of events—emphasizing hope and the success of a future migration to another planet. This decision stems from a deep-seated belief that humanity’s survival depends on maintaining mental equilibrium. The President justifies this censorship by claiming that the people must be protected from "the sight of pain and death," which historically caused widespread insanity. He further isolates Michael and Mary, offering them comfort and medical care while denying them freedom to live openly on Earth. Ultimately, he suppresses their truth—particularly their decision to return to Earth and begin a natural life, including Mary’s unexpected pregnancy—believing that such revelations would destabilize the population. His actions reflect a tragic prioritization of collective sanity over individual truth and human dignity, revealing a society that has abandoned empathy in favor of control.
