The story revolves around Sol Becker, a weary hitchhiker who arrives in a small, isolated town and is taken in by a mysterious woman who appears harmless but harbors unsettling secrets. The plot unfolds as Sol, soaked from the rain, seeks shelter and is offered a room by the woman, who claims to be a mother with a busy schedule. However, her strange behavior—such as refusing to provide bedding, insisting on an early wake-up, and abruptly referencing a "palace" and an "execution"—creates an atmosphere of unease. As Sol settles in, the narrative suggests deeper, hidden dangers: the woman's references to court, the odd time references, and her sudden departure imply that she is part of a secretive or sinister network. The story builds tension through subtle clues—like the clock showing a distorted time and the woman’s cryptic tone—hinting at a town filled with strange, possibly supernatural characters. Sol's isolation and vulnerability make him susceptible to manipulation, and the environment itself seems to mirror psychological disorientation. The plot ultimately centers on a young man who finds himself in a peculiar town where appearances are deceptive and reality is fragile, revealing a world of hidden truths and unsettling occurrences.
