The story is set on Alphard Six, a peaceful, habitable planet in a distant region of space beyond the known Terran frontier. It is a world with a single, dimly lit moon and a landscape that includes a central city surrounded by cultivated fields and twenty-seven small agrarian hamlets. The planet appears to have been abandoned by its original colonists during the Hymenop invasion, which occurred in 3025, and remained unoccupied for over a century. However, the story reveals a hidden truth: the planet hosts a long-forgotten Terran colony that was stranded here during an interstellar expedition from Earth in the early 21st century. This colony, isolated and forgotten, had been deliberately grounded by the Hymenop invaders as part of a psychological experiment to study human adaptation under extreme conditions. Over centuries, the people of Alphard Six developed a primitive, self-sustaining society, believing their ship had made a successful planetfall and that they were the descendants of a lost expedition. The setting is thus both alien and deeply human—marked by technological backwardness and a profound sense of historical delusion. Despite the planet's outward peace, it harbors a hidden threat: the presence of an ancient, atomic-powered ship that was never meant to survive, now serving as a symbol of a forgotten past and a potential danger if discovered. The environment is described as peaceful on the surface, but beneath this calm lies a complex web of deception, survival, and interstellar mystery.
