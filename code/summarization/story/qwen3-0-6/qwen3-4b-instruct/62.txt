The Thinkers are a powerful, influential group in the dystopian future of the story who control key aspects of society through advanced mental science and a vast, artificial intelligence known as "Maizie." They are not conventional scientists or politicians but rather a secretive elite who claim to possess superior mental abilities and have built a self-sustaining system of thought-based governance. Their importance lies in their ability to manipulate reality through psychological and technological means—such as the Mind Bomb, Selective Microbe Slayer, and ESP research—giving them immense influence over global events and political power. They are portrayed as both visionary innovators and authoritarian figures, using mental technology to shape public perception and policy, often at the expense of transparency or democratic accountability. The Thinkers' rise to power is built on a combination of deception, strategic timing, and public belief in their "Martian wisdom" and mental superiority. Their presence creates tension with traditional scientists and military leaders, who view them as dangerously unaccountable. Ultimately, the Thinkers represent a dystopian ideal where intelligence and control are achieved not through open science or democratic processes, but through manipulation of thought and belief—making them central to the story’s exploration of power, truth, and the dangers of unchecked mental authority.
