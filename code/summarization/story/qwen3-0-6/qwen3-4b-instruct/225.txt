The story that revolves around a man's unsettling discovery in his town and the strange indifference of the people around him is *The Haunted Fountain*. The plot centers on a man who experiences a profound psychological disorientation after encountering an ocean-like scene at night, which he had never seen in his hometown. He becomes convinced that the land has been transformed—where once there were farms and houses, now there are vast stretches of water and shifting geography. As he investigates, he notices the town’s residents remain unaware or unbothered by these changes. People continue their daily lives, ignoring the man's growing panic and the evidence of a world that no longer aligns with reality. His confusion deepens as he realizes he may have forgotten basic geographical facts—such as whether town is north or south of his home—or even the presence of people like the Pangborns and Elvertons who once lived there. The townspeople’s apparent indifference to his distress underscores a deeper theme: a world that has shifted beyond human perception, where reality is disrupted, and those who live within it remain unshaken. This creates an eerie atmosphere of isolation and existential dread, as the man grapples with a town that no longer makes sense—yet refuses to acknowledge its transformation.
