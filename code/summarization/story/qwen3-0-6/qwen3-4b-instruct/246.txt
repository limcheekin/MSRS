Doctor Hamming is a mysterious and enigmatic figure in the story who represents both a medical authority and a symbol of a world undergoing radical transformation. He appears to Harry Burr as a doctor who administers diathermy treatments, ostensibly to restore his sanity after a moment of existential realization. His traits—calm, detached, and subtly authoritative—contrast with Harry’s growing disorientation. When Harry learns that "we're on an ark," it becomes clear that Doctor Hamming is not merely treating illness but guiding Harry through a psychological and societal collapse. His statement that Harry "passed the test" suggests that the world has changed in ways beyond perception, and that sanity is now defined by one’s ability to accept this altered reality. Doctor Hamming embodies the tension between individual perception and collective truth in a world where normalcy has dissolved. He does not provide answers but instead confirms Harry’s realization: the world is no longer as it once was, and surviving means accepting that truth—even if it shatters one’s sense of reality. His role is thus both clinical and metaphysical, embodying the struggle between denial and acceptance in a world where the foundations of reality have been erased.
