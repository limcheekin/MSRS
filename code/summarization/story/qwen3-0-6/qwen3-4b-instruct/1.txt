The setting of the story is a dense, mysterious jungle world known as Sekk, characterized by its fertile valleys, deep marshes, and the vast, misty Lake of Uzdon. This jungle is home to unique and dangerous life forms, including hairy half-man creatures, carnivorous farn beasts with long fangs, and enigmatic "Misty Ones" who are initially perceived as invisible or ghostly beings but are later revealed to be living, humanoid creatures with distinct physical traits. The landscape transitions from thick, leafy forests to open game trails and swampy lowlands, emphasizing the environment’s wild unpredictability. The world is not only physically alien but also culturally layered—marked by ancient conflicts between hairy men and golden-skinned valley dwellers, as well as hidden societies and secret knowledge. Central to the setting is the idea of a forgotten past, where characters like Noork carry mysterious origins tied to Earth or other worlds, suggesting that the jungle world may be a portal or refuge from a larger, cosmic reality. The jungle's oppressive atmosphere, rich with danger and cryptic lore, serves as both a physical and symbolic backdrop for a character’s journey of self-discovery and survival.
