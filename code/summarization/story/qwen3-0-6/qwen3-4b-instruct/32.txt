Mury handles himself with cold precision and calculated menace throughout the story, embodying a revolutionary figure driven by deep-seated opposition to Martian domination. Initially appearing as a mysterious, intimidating stranger, Mury gradually reveals his role as a leader of a clandestine movement—"We"—that seeks to undermine the corrupt system exploiting Earth’s labor for Martian benefit. He speaks with conviction and fury, exposing the truth that Earth is being sold as a labor pool, not a sovereign world. Mury’s actions are methodical and ruthless: he manipulates Ryd into joining him through a mix of psychological pressure and the promise of survival, ensuring compliance by threatening violence if resistance occurs. During the critical mission to steal the *Shahrazad*, Mury demonstrates tactical skill and decisive leadership, executing a swift, coordinated plan to disable guards, seize control of the ship, and evade detection. His calm demeanor masks an unrelenting determination, and his actions are guided not by personal gain but by a broader ideological mission. Even in moments of crisis—such as when the warship intercepts them—Mury remains composed, using the flame gun with surgical efficiency to assert dominance. Ultimately, Mury operates as both a strategist and a revolutionary agent, balancing fear, authority, and moral clarity to advance a cause rooted in resistance and liberation. His control over the situation is absolute, reflecting his role as a disciplined, almost fanatical force dedicated to subverting systemic oppression.
