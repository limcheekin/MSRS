The story explores the complexities of marriage through the lens of a man, George Faircloth, who seeks an escape from his deteriorating relationship with his wife, Marge. Frustrated by her constant complaints, emotional outbursts, and list of grievances, George becomes desperate and devises a plan to resolve the marital crisis by replacing himself with an android husband—an "Ego Prime." He believes this solution will allow him to escape the constraints of marriage and avoid the social and legal repercussions of divorce. However, the story reveals a deeper truth: Marge never truly wanted to leave him. When George attempts to run away with the android, she returns, revealing that she had known about his plan all along. Her affection for him remains unchanged, and she ultimately chooses to stay with him, embracing their reality. The plot underscores how marriage can be both a source of suffering and a foundation of genuine connection, showing that escape from domestic trouble often leads back to love and acceptance.
