Jon Karyl and the Steel-Blues encounter each other during a tense and unexpected situation on an asteroid when Jon, a Lone Watcher, is unprepared for the sudden appearance of six steel-blue alien creatures emerging from a peculiar ship. Initially unaware of their presence, Jon realizes the danger when he sees them approaching after fleeing in panic. He later learns that the Steel-Blues are not hostile but rather advanced robotic beings who have captured him for a prolonged psychological and physical "torture" involving a corrosive liquid that dissolves metals. Jon discovers they are metal-based life forms, vulnerable to acidic substances, and that their entire system is designed to eliminate organic life through this process. The relationship evolves from one of fear and survival to one of curiosity and mutual understanding, as Jon gains insight into their motives—seeking living space in the solar system—and ultimately outsmarts them by warning the Space Patrol using a radio signal. Jon's survival hinges on the fact that the Steel-Blues' own vulnerability to acid makes their invasion strategy flawed, and his knowledge becomes critical to Earth's defense. Thus, the relationship is one of adversarial encounter that transforms into a strategic alliance through shared understanding and mutual survival.
