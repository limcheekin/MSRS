Captain Linden and his lieutenant "Split" Campbell share a deeply trusted, almost symbiotic relationship forged through shared danger and mutual reliance on the alien world they explore. Campbell serves as both a loyal companion and a grounding presence for Captain Linden, especially during Linden’s periods of unconsciousness or delirium. In dreams, Campbell's voice consistently reassures Linden, reminding him of critical protocols—such as the EGGWE Code—demonstrating his role as a disciplined, vigilant, and mentally steadfast officer. Campbell is not only physically present in the caverns, guiding and tending to Linden’s well-being, but also acts as a bridge between Linden and the native Benzendella people, helping to interpret their language and customs. Their bond is marked by quiet loyalty, shared responsibility, and a sense of duty that transcends the alien environment. While Campbell does not dominate or control Linden, he provides emotional and operational stability, ensuring that the mission’s integrity and ethical guidelines—particularly regarding interactions with native inhabitants—are maintained. This relationship reflects a partnership built on mutual respect, trust, and survival, making Campbell an essential, almost familial figure in Captain Linden’s journey through the mysterious and potentially hostile world.
