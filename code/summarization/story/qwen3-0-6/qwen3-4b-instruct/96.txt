The United Universe introduced a variety of rules and taboos tailored to the unique cultural, biological, and societal norms of each planet, all while maintaining a unified system to prevent conflict. For instance, Zosma enacted a new public head-covering rule to avoid appearing bareheaded, which was considered immodest—a reflection of its own cultural sensitivity to modesty and appearance. Similarly, interplanetary social norms were strictly regulated: fraternization between planets was forbidden to preserve cultural integrity, as Earth sought to prevent "shotgun weddings" that could destabilize distinct planetary identities. These rules stemmed from a belief that diversity in customs, when managed through mutual respect and clear boundaries, prevented conflict and ensured harmony. The system also included the breakdown of rigid caste systems—such as on Zur—where intermarriage between Earthians and Zurians was encouraged to create a blended, equitable society. This shift demonstrated a move from hierarchical control to shared coexistence. However, such rules were not universally accepted; events like the violent crowd reaction against a Kagor revealed persistent taboos and prejudices that even the United Universe’s laws could not fully suppress. Thus, the rules were both a product of cultural understanding and a tool to manage the complexities of interplanetary diversity, balancing order with the inherent human (and non-human) need for identity and belonging.
