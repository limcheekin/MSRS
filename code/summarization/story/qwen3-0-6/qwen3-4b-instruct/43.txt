The history of POSAT (The Perpetual Order of Seekers After Truth) is revealed as an ancient secret society founded over four centuries ago by a super-genius physical scientist and mathematician during the Italian Renaissance. This individual, who lived at a time when scientific breakthroughs were rare, invented foundational principles in physics and mathematics—such as calculus, quantum theory, electromagnetic radiation, relativity, and nuclear binding energy—long before they were formally discovered. Recognizing the dangers of atomic energy in a politically unstable world, he founded POSAT to preserve these discoveries in secrecy and ensure they would only be revealed when humanity was ready to use them safely. The society maintained absolute secrecy, with its members selected through psychological testing designed to identify individuals whose personalities and motivations aligned with the organization’s goals. Over centuries, POSAT evolved into a highly advanced, self-sustaining entity that operates behind a veil of normalcy, using sophisticated technology—including a functioning atomic reactor and a powerful computing machine—to study human motivation and behavior. The society's ultimate purpose is not to dominate, but to use its scientific mastery to reform civilization and prevent human self-destruction. Its members are carefully vetted, with different types of applicants—such as desperate individuals, religious fanatics, or scientists—assigned specific roles to maintain secrecy and ensure the project’s long-term success. The story culminates in Don Alford’s realization that POSAT is not a hoax, but a real, centuries-old organization that has been quietly shaping human progress through science and psychological insight.
