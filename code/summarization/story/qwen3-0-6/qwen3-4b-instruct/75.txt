The story involves a group of astronauts living in Lunar City who discover a mysterious, alien-shaped object on the Moon's surface. Initially, they speculate about its origin and age, with mineralogist Miller concluding it is a durable, ancient alloy possibly thousands of years old. The group decides not to alert authorities, fearing interference or dismissal, and instead investigates the object on their own. Their exploration leads them into an alien spacecraft, where they are suddenly trapped. The ship reveals itself to be a "booby-trap" constructed by an extraterrestrial race from another galaxy that visited Earth millennia ago. These beings intended to observe human civilization by placing the ship on the Moon as a trap for future explorers. Once activated, the ship transports the group to another planet, where they become prisoners in a zoo-like setting. The narrative unfolds with growing horror and realization of their fate—being studied and held captive for six months. The plot centers on survival, discovery, and the terrifying implications of encountering intelligent alien life, emphasizing both scientific curiosity and existential dread.
