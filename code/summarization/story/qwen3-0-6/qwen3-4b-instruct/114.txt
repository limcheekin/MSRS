The meeting between Escher and MacDonald is prompted by a pressing colonization challenge: the severe gender imbalance on Earth, where women outnumber men in a 5-to-3 ratio due to the departure of men to colonize other planets. This imbalance has led to social unrest, including increased crimes committed by women, puritanical laws, and a decline in male interest in emigration. The Colonization Board has tasked Escher and MacDonald with devising a solution to encourage women to join the colonization effort. As MacDonald presents the problem and cites the statistics and societal consequences, he directly approaches Escher to discuss the matter, seeking a viable, immediate strategy. Their meeting occurs in the context of urgent governmental pressure—within a year, the situation will worsen, threatening social stability. Thus, the circumstances leading to their meeting are the Board's assignment of the problem and the need for a practical solution to reverse the gender imbalance and revitalize colonization efforts.
