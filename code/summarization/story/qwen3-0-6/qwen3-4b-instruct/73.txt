Garrett made the girl believe he was Starrett Blade by fabricating a convincing identity and using deception to manipulate her perception. He presented himself as "Mr. Devil Garrett," a notorious killer, and claimed to have been posing as Starrett Blade to gather information. To solidify the illusion, Garrett showed her faked papers and a three-dimensional image of himself that matched the description of Starrett Blade. These documents, which he had secretly obtained, convinced her that he was the real Starrett Blade. Additionally, Garrett used his authority and knowledge of the girl's past to manipulate her, emphasizing that she had already seen proof of his identity through the "Hinton secret patents" and descriptions of his inventions. By doing so, he ensured that the girl, Anne Hinton, would believe he was the real Starrett Blade, allowing him to frame and execute him as a pirate. This deception was central to Garrett's plan to eliminate Star Blade while maintaining the illusion that he was the true "Death Star" and the only one who could be executed. Ultimately, it was Garrett’s strategic use of forged documents, psychological manipulation, and controlled information that made the girl believe he was Starrett Blade.
