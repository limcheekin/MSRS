The story involves a man named Lewis Terry who, after being transported to a strange world, wakes up with no memory of who he is or how he got there. He finds himself on a remote planet, Venus, where he is cared for by a mysterious girl. As he struggles to piece together his identity, he begins to question his past and the reality of his current situation. The narrative reveals that Lewis was once a real person—a writer named Thig—whose life and memories were erased when he was transformed into a creature of a Horde's creation. Upon regaining consciousness, he is reborn as Lewis Terry, a man who must now live with the burden of remembering a life he no longer recognizes. The plot centers on his journey to recover his identity, confront the truth of his past, and come to terms with the loss of self. The strange creatures and conflicts in the world—such as the hostile forces of the Horde and the mysterious girl who tends to him—serve as metaphors for inner conflict and the struggle to reclaim one’s true self. Ultimately, the story explores themes of identity, memory, and the human need for belonging in a vast, indifferent universe.
