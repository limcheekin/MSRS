The setting of the story exploring a young man's experiences navigating a complex and diverse society filled with peculiar customs and beings is a fictional, alien world characterized by strange fauna, unusual social structures, and non-human environments. This world is primarily depicted through the journey of Martin, a space-scouter who lands on a barren planet where he discovers a towering, geometric city made of metal. The setting is both alien and strangely familiar, evoking a sense of unease and wonder as the characters encounter distorted human-like elements—such as low buildings, unusual gravity, and the possibility of parallel evolution with native beings. The environment is hostile yet ordered, with the city’s strict geometry suggesting a society governed by logic or an advanced, possibly non-human intelligence. Additionally, the presence of wind-like figures, unexplained phenomena, and the eerie silence of the landscape heightens the sense of otherworldliness. This setting combines elements of science fiction and myth, where physical surroundings mirror psychological disorientation, emphasizing the protagonist’s struggle to understand a society fundamentally different from his own. The story unfolds across diverse locations—ranging from a mysterious city to a rural Indian campsite and a futuristic utopia—each reinforcing the theme of cultural and societal alienation. Ultimately, the setting functions as a metaphor for encountering the unknown, where norms are challenged and perception is reshaped by unfamiliar customs and beings.
