Professor Venetti’s invention, known as the "Expendable," is a device designed to dispose of waste—particularly unwanted bodies—by disintegrating them in a manner that appears to vanish the matter entirely. It does not burn, dissolve, or crush the material, as traditional methods would. Instead, the machine operates through an unexplained process that may involve transporting the processed material into another dimension, the past, or the future. The exact destination or mechanism remains uncertain, and Venetti admits he does not fully understand how or where the material goes. The device is powered electrically and, once activated, appears to function with minimal ongoing energy input, allowing it to "coast" on its own generators. Though initially developed for government waste disposal (particularly radioactive waste), the invention is repurposed by Tony Carmen for criminal use—specifically, to eliminate bodies without leaving physical evidence. The device’s ability to seemingly erase matter from sight and location makes it both a scientific enigma and a dangerous tool, raising questions about its implications for reality, time, and justice.
