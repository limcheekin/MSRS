The Grannies, or Granitebacks, are a deadly and indestructible form of life found on the lunar outpost in the story. Known as "Granitebacks" due to their name being an abbreviation of "Granitebacks," they are described as the only mobile life form discovered on Earth's moon. Their exoskeleton is harder than steel, diamond, or battleplate, making them impervious to all known weapons—steel, flame, electronic waves, or even the lethal atomo-needle dispenser. This gives them a terrifying resilience and makes them an existential threat to the outpost's inhabitants. The story emphasizes that no specimen of a Grannie has ever been successfully brought to Earth for study, underscoring their biological mystery and danger. They are portrayed as a relentless, unyielding enemy, possibly sent by an external force to threaten the survival of the lunar settlers. Their presence is both mysterious and ominous, symbolizing a natural threat that outpaces human technology and scientific understanding. The fear of the Grannies reflects a deeper theme of vulnerability in isolated environments, where even the most advanced civilizations face forces beyond their control.
