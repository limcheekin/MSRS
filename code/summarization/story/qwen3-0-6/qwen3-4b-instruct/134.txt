John Smith is a peculiar and deeply mysterious character in the story, known for his quiet demeanor and intense focus on music and dimensions. He plays a rare and ancient instrument called the *Zloomph*, which he claims is over five thousand years old and the only one of its kind. Unlike a conventional fiddle, the *Zloomph* is described as a monstrous, black, hour-glass-like bass instrument, hinting at its otherworldly origin. John's fascination lies not just in music, but in the concept of "holes" — physical and dimensional — which he believes are gateways to parallel time dimensions. He claims that these holes allow access to the history of all time dimensions, and that his own body is now filled with such holes, making it impossible for him to return home. His journey begins when he falls through a hole while returning from the university to prove a theory about pre-tape music. He becomes obsessed with finding the "right" hole to return to his original dimension, fearing discreditation if he fails. Though initially reluctant to play with the band, John ultimately joins them, and his music—especially the deep, rich tones of the *Zloomph*—transforms the atmosphere, bringing ecstasy and awakening the audience. The story reveals that John’s instrument is not just a musical tool but a dimensional key, and his music becomes a bridge between reality and other dimensions, suggesting that true connection and survival lie in harmony with the universe's hidden structures.
