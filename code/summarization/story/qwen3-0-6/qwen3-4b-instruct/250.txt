The plot revolves around a man named Quidley who notices a mysterious code hidden in a library book, specifically in a volume by Taine titled *History*. He observes a woman, Kay, secretly placing a folded sheet of yellow paper between the pages of the book and returning it to the shelf. Intrigued, Quidley deciphers the coded message—initially unintelligible but later recognized as referencing "Cai" and the name "Yoolna"—and deduces that the woman is named Yoolna, distinct from a previous figure named Klio. He follows her actions, using a clever scheme called "Operation Spill-the-sugar" to track her movements. When she returns to a coffee bar, he follows her inside and continues monitoring her. Eventually, he learns that the code is part of a secret society, and the term "snoll doper" refers to a coded message or object to be delivered. The story unfolds with increasing suspense as Quidley realizes the woman is part of a covert network using hidden messages and coded communication, suggesting deeper societal or conspiratorial implications. Though initially mistaken for a Martian plot, the true nature of the code and its connection to a secret society reveals a more grounded, human-driven mystery involving intelligence, surveillance, and personal identity.
