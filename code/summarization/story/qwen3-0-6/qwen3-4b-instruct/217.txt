The setting of the story involving a lone watchman on an asteroid is a barren, rocky asteroid with a sparse, desolate environment. The world is characterized by its lack of natural features like trees or water, existing instead as a jagged, rocky terrain where the watchman, Jon Karyl, lives in a small plastic igloo near a rocket power plant. The atmosphere is cold and isolated, emphasizing his solitary existence. The asteroid's low gravity—only about one thirtieth of Earth’s—makes movement easier but also alters physical perception, such as making the alien inhabitants appear significantly stronger. The setting shifts from a quiet, tense solitude to a high-stakes confrontation when a hostile alien force, composed of six steel-blue creatures, appears unexpectedly and attacks. These invaders are not native to the asteroid but descend from a mysterious vessel, creating an atmosphere of sudden danger and survival. The terrain—rocky slopes, ravines, and dense brush—serves as both a physical obstacle and a refuge for Jon, who uses it to evade capture. The setting thus blends isolation, alien threat, and survival instinct, forming a tense, atmospheric backdrop where the lone watchman must rely on speed, ingenuity, and sheer will to survive against overwhelming odds.
