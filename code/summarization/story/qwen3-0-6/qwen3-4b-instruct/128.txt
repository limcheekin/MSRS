The setting of the story revolving around a man working in a distant outpost, facing challenges and unexpected events while longing for the comforts of home is a remote lunar outpost on Luna, specifically Lunar III. This frontier station functions as a rocket refueling station, teleradio transmission point, and meteorological base. The protagonist, Horatio Jones ("Isobar"), is confined to a sealed, airtight dome for six months, cut off from natural sunlight, fresh air, and the excitement of Earth life. The setting emphasizes isolation, monotony, and physical and emotional deprivation—highlighting the psychological toll of long-term space service. Despite the technological infrastructure, the environment feels barren and soulless, amplifying Isobar’s longing for home, family, and the simple joys of Earth. The story contrasts the sterile, mechanized outpost with the warmth and humanity of domestic life, underscoring the emotional challenges of being separated from loved ones in a distant, unforgiving frontier.
