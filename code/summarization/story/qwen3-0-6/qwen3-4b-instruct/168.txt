Ekalastron, or Element No. 97, in the context of this story, is a highly valuable and dangerous space element discovered on the moon Titania, a satellite of Uranus. It possesses extraordinary properties: it is extremely lightweight, yet incredibly strong, capable of deflecting meteors and crushing diamonds while maintaining resilience like rubber. This makes it a revolutionary material for space travel and spacecraft construction. However, its power also makes it a temptation to pirates and outlaws, who seek to exploit it for their own gain. The story warns that the discovery and transmission of its location—through a visiphoned message—risked being intercepted by the Lorelei, a siren-like entity that lures spacemen to their doom. In the narrative, the pirate crew, referred to as the "Lorelei's men," ambushes the protagonists after they reveal the mine’s location, emphasizing that ekalastron is not just a scientific breakthrough but a deadly lure. The element symbolizes both technological advancement and peril, as its allure draws not only explorers but also ruthless forces seeking to exploit it. Thus, ekalastron functions as a metaphor for the dangerous intersection of progress, greed, and fate in space exploration.
