In a society where conformity is paramount and individuals are identified by numeric designations, the State acts as an all-encompassing controller that enforces uniformity through systemic dehumanization. Individuals are stripped of personal identity—replaced by cold, standardized designations like "AAAA-111/111"—which erases personal names, histories, and individuality. The State maintains control not only through rigid bureaucratic procedures but also through psychological manipulation. For instance, the fear of being renumbered or psycho-scanned instills a deep sense of anxiety, making citizens avoid nonconformist behavior. The State further legitimizes its authority by framing renumbering as a necessary, efficient process for national progress and stability, thus justifying its intrusive mechanisms under the guise of rationality and efficiency. Moreover, the State monitors individual behavior, as seen when a person’s designation is altered to "DANGEROUS CRIMINAL TENDENCIES," exposing how personal autonomy is systematically punished and surveilled. This system ensures compliance by making deviation not only socially unacceptable but personally threatening. Ultimately, the State’s role is not merely administrative—it is ideological, enforcing a society where individuality is suppressed and obedience becomes the foundation of survival.
