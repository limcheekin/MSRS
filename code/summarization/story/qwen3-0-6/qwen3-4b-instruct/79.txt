Ed and Verana are not directly involved in the alien spacecraft adventure on the Moon. The story centers on Kane, Marie, and the narrator, who discover an alien spacecraft on the Moon. Kane explains that the ship was placed there as a "booby-trap" by an advanced alien race from another galaxy, intending to study humans when they eventually find it. Verana is present during the discovery and reacts with skepticism, particularly regarding the idea of human dissection, showing her interest in extra-sensory perception. However, there is no indication that Ed (from the Edith/Loyce narrative) or any other individual named Ed is involved in this lunar alien encounter. The only Ed mentioned in the chapters is Ed Loyce, who experiences a mind invasion by alien mimics in Pikeville and escapes to survive. Therefore, Ed and Verana are not connected to the Moon adventure involving the alien spacecraft.
