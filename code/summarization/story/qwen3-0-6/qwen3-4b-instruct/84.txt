The setting of the story involving a desperate journey through space filled with danger, as the main character seeks to return home amidst a looming threat, is a desolate, alien planet known as Tintera. This world, last contacted nearly 150 years prior, is abandoned and uncharted, presenting a hostile environment. The gravity is unnaturally wrong, causing physical discomfort and disorientation; the planet is filled with unsettling, crawling creatures and pervasive, unpleasant odors. The journey begins when a group of young colonists—selected at age 14—are dropped onto Tintera to survive for one month, with no guarantee of return. The environment is designed to be physically and emotionally taxing, emphasizing isolation, survival struggles, and the psychological toll of alien life. As the protagonist, Mia Havero, navigates this hostile terrain, she faces not only environmental dangers but also personal conflicts and emotional despair, underscoring the perilous nature of the journey. The setting functions as both a physical and psychological battlefield, where the threat is not just external but internal—fear, loneliness, and the fragility of human will in the face of an indifferent universe.
