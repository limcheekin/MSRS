Tobias Whiting is the former colony trader and wealthiest individual in the group, who once amassed significant fortune through his business dealings with the Kumaji. After the caravan faces dwindling supplies and increasing danger from the Kumaji, Whiting disappears during the fourth night, taking Steve’s unicopter to attempt to retrieve his lost wealth. His friends speculate that he believed he could reclaim his fortune by directly confronting the Kumaji, who would trade it for the caravan’s location. However, Whiting is later found injured and in pain, having been attacked by a Kumaji guard. Despite being severely wounded, he insists on not betraying the group to protect Mary, his daughter, from torture. He ultimately chooses to die quietly, telling Steve and Mary that he will ensure their safety by misleading the Kumaji about their direction—telling them to head due south. Though he initially intended to trade information for wealth, he changes his mind, realizing that sacrificing himself ensures the group’s survival. His final act of self-sacrifice and moral courage becomes a pivotal moment in the narrative, symbolizing redemption and the triumph of love and loyalty over greed.
