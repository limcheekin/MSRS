The members of the Explorer follow strict health and safety criteria when navigating the seemingly idyllic yet potentially dangerous environment of Minos. Recognizing the risk of contagion, they prioritize medical evaluation of local wildlife before any interaction. The crew’s doctors wear airtight suits to test animals for diseases, understanding that alien pathogens could be contagious and deadly—especially given past failures of colonies on plague-infected planets. They also maintain vigilance regarding environmental threats, such as the sudden movement of sponge-trees and hostile attacks by armed groups, which are treated as immediate dangers requiring defensive measures like the ship’s siren and weaponry. Additionally, they enforce a cautious approach to human contact, relying on established protocols to avoid exposure to unknown biological agents. The presence of unusual behaviors—like the logs seemingly lifting the crew or the farn beasts’ predatory nature—further underscores their awareness of potential biological hazards. Ultimately, the crew adheres to a precautionary principle: no unverified interaction with local life or environment occurs without medical clearance or direct observation, ensuring the safety of the team in an environment that appears peaceful but may harbor hidden dangers.
