The relationship between the Elliotts and Mr. Snader is one of curiosity, skepticism, and gradual trust. Initially, the Elliotts view Snader as a mysterious, possibly deceptive figure who offers them an extraordinary and dangerous opportunity—time travel—to escape their present struggles. Jeff and Ann are wary, especially of the financial and personal risks involved, and they question whether Snader is truly offering a legitimate path to change their lives or simply playing a prank. However, as they experience the mechanics of time travel firsthand—seeing Snader step into a screen and reappear from another time dimension—their skepticism gives way to fascination. Snader’s confidence, his knowledge of future technology (like chromatics), and the tangible evidence of time travel convince them that he is not just a fool but someone who operates within a system of time travel. Though he remains enigmatic, his actions suggest a deeper purpose: to help people like the Elliotts who are restless or in trouble find escape or redemption through time. Ultimately, their relationship evolves from suspicion to a cautiously hopeful partnership, as Snader invites them into a future where change is possible, even if it defies logic and time.
