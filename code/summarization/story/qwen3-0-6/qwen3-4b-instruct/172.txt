The melting sickness is a deadly disease that devastated the human colony on the planet Minos, killing nearly all of its inhabitants two years after the colony's arrival. According to Patrick Mead, a member of the surviving Mead family, the disease was so severe that it claimed the lives of all doctors and medical personnel who attempted to study or treat it. The only people who survived were members of the Mead family, who were immune to the illness. Patrick explains that the colony's people all resemble one another because they are genetically related—specifically, descendants of the original settlers. This shared genetic lineage likely contributed to their immunity. The disease is referred to as the "melting sickness" due to its grotesque and destructive effects on the human body, though no further details about its symptoms or cause are provided in the text. The colony’s ship, carrying medical supplies and books, was destroyed to escape the contagion, and its crew perished, leaving the colony with no means of medical care or technological support. Despite this, no further outbreaks have occurred, and the planet remains free of other diseases. The melting sickness thus serves as a tragic reminder of the vulnerability of human life in isolated colonies and the devastating consequences of uncontrolled disease.
