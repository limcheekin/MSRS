Tim is the family’s meteorologist and one of the few members who shows genuine empathy and concern for Kevin, the psi-deficient brother. Unlike the others, who often overlook or dismiss Kevin due to their own psychic abilities, Tim consistently recognizes something unique in him—something beyond measurable tests. While the family sees Kevin as a flaw or an anomaly, Tim believes he possesses an undetected, unquantified ability or emotional depth that remains hidden from standard evaluation. Tim’s role is both practical and emotional: he provides a rare sense of connection and validation within a household defined by psychic superiority and social distancing. His confidence in Kevin’s latent potential reflects a deeper understanding of human complexity, contrasting with the family’s rigid, analytical mindset. Tim’s presence offers hope and compassion, making him a stabilizing force in a home where Kevin feels unseen and unvalued. His role is not just professional but deeply personal—he sees beyond surface-level abilities and recognizes the emotional truth in Kevin’s quiet suffering.
