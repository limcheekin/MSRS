The setting of the story is a harsh, rapidly aging world where life spans are measured in days—specifically, eight days for a newborn before death. This world is the result of a catastrophic crash of metal seeds from a distant green planet, which resulted in humans being accelerated into rapid aging and short lifespans. The environment is unforgiving: extreme solar radiation, volcanic activity, and deadly avalanches shape daily life. Life is fleeting and intense, with people living, dying, and being reborn within a matter of days. The story unfolds in a rugged, mountainous valley where the inhabitants live in cliffside caves, enduring constant danger from natural forces like avalanches and heat. Despite the brevity of life, characters experience profound emotional depth—love, fear, friendship, and conflict emerge quickly and intensely. The world is both physically and emotionally brutal, emphasizing the urgency of human connection and survival in a universe where time itself is compressed. This setting underscores the central theme: in a world where life is so short, every moment must be lived fully, with meaning derived from fleeting relationships and deep awareness.
