The setting of the story is a distant planet, Alphard Six, located two hundred twenty-six light-years from Sol, in a region beyond the former limits of Terran expansion. The planet is a cool, green world with a single dun-colored moon, described as habitable and previously surveyed for colonization before the Hymenop alien invasion in 3025. After the Bees withdrew from the system over a century ago, Alphard Six remained uncolonized until now. The narrative unfolds primarily in orbit around the planet, with the Terran Reclamations crew—Farrell, Stryker, and Gibson—conducting reconnaissance. Their mission is to locate abandoned Terran colonies, but they encounter a surprise: a large, grounded, primitive spaceship of Terran design, powered by continuous atomic fission, located in a central city surrounded by agrarian hamlets. This discovery leads to a deeper exploration of the planet’s history, revealing that the ship was likely part of an ancient Terran expedition from the 2170s that failed during its journey. The Hymenop aliens had discovered and captured the ship, used it as a test subject for human conditioning, and left it stranded on Alphard Six. Over centuries, succeeding generations grew up believing they were the original crew and had never left Earth. The planet’s setting thus blends alien history, forgotten human technology, and psychological experimentation, forming a hauntingly realistic backdrop of isolation, survival, and the unintended consequences of interstellar conflict.
