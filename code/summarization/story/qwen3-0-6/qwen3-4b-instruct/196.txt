The setting of the story involves a young man named Kaiser who is stranded on the distant, rain-soaked planet Big Muddy after being dropped by his mother ship, the *Soscites II*. The planet is described as having a persistent rainy climate, with large drops of rain constantly thudding against the transparent walls of the scout ship. This environment contributes to Kaiser's sense of isolation and disorientation. The setting is both physically harsh and psychologically oppressive—Kaiser struggles with confusion, memory lapses, and the unsettling realization that he has been receiving baby-talk messages from his mother ship, which he initially fails to interpret. The planet itself is uninhabited and devoid of life, emphasizing the solitude and existential dread of being cut off from civilization. The atmosphere is one of quiet desperation, where survival depends on repairing the scout ship before running out of time or supplies. The setting underscores themes of isolation, identity, and communication breakdown, as Kaiser tries to understand his situation and reestablish contact with his mother ship.
