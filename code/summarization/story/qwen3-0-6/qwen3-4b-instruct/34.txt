Ryd is a former helio operator and a man of modest means who finds himself caught in a dangerous conspiracy involving the power struggles between Earth and Mars. Introduced as a down-on-his-luck former worker with no current employment, Ryd is manipulated by Mury, a mysterious and intensely ideological figure who claims to represent a hidden revolutionary force known as "We." Mury reveals that Ryd is part of a vast population exploited by a corrupt government that has sold Earth’s future to Mars in exchange for cheap labor—reducing humanity to a colony. The central threat lies in the planned arrival of the "power cylinder," a device meant to revive Earth’s industry, which Mury asserts is being sabotaged to prevent Earth from gaining independence. Ryd is drawn into this plot not through choice but through fear and coercion; he is offered fifty thousand credits to join Mury’s mission, but the real danger lies in the fact that refusal means certain death. His transformation from a passive, fearful man to an active participant in rebellion underscores his entrapment in a larger system of power and control. Ultimately, Ryd’s character embodies the struggle of the individual against overwhelming systemic forces—his journey reflects both personal vulnerability and a desperate hope for agency in a world where fate is shaped by unseen, dangerous hands.
