The setting of *Captain Midas* is a lone, derelict space vessel drifting in the vast, silent void of space, suspended against the velvet darkness of the cosmos. This isolated, abandoned craft, described as torn and lifeless, serves as the central location where Captain Midas discovers a treasure beyond imagination—gold, symbolizing the alluring but dangerous lure of space-based wealth. The story unfolds in a desolate, otherworldly environment where the absence of human presence amplifies the sense of isolation and existential dread. The setting reflects the theme of greed: while the treasure appears boundless, it is revealed to be cursed, hinting at the moral and physical cost of pursuing such riches in space. The bleakness of the void contrasts sharply with the captain’s once-enthusiastic dreams, emphasizing how the pursuit of material gain in space leads not to fulfillment, but to ruin. The environment, therefore, is both a physical and symbolic stage—empty, unforgiving, and reflective of the consequences of human ambition in the face of cosmic indifference.
