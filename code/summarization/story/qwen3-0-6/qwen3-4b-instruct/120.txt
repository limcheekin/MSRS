The story revolves around a man, Jeff Elliott, who is seeking a job and encounters a mysterious figure named Greet Snader, who claims to run a time travel bureau. Initially skeptical, Jeff is drawn into the idea when Snader offers a free, one-hour trial trip to another time. The encounter takes place in a seemingly ordinary neighborhood, but inside a hidden room with two moving screens representing time streams, Jeff and his wife Ann witness the reality of time travel—people appearing and disappearing as if materializing from different points in time. Snader demonstrates the mechanism by stepping into one screen and reappearing on the other, revealing that time travelers move through a "fourth dimension" where past and future coexist. The plot deepens when Jeff begins to suspect the implications of such technology, especially as he learns that time travelers are carefully monitored to prevent paradoxes. A key twist emerges when Jeff returns to find a client, Mr. Oyster, who has been sitting in the office for days—appearing out of nowhere and claiming to have been waiting for him. This creates a paradox: the man appears only after Jeff's trip, yet he was already present before. The story ultimately reveals that time travel is real but highly regulated, with travelers forbidden from altering history, ensuring the timeline remains stable. The plot blends sci-fi elements with psychological tension, showing how a man’s desire for escape and change leads him into a world where time is both a tool and a prison.
