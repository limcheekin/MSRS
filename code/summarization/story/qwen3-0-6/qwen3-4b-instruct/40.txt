The story revolves around a mysterious organization known as the Red Ankh Society, which promises transformative knowledge and spiritual enlightenment to a select few. This group markets itself as a source of "incredibly powerful semantics" derived from ancient alien wisdom, positioning itself as a secretive, elite society offering profound truths beyond conventional understanding. The narrative reveals that the organization operates through deceptive and manipulative means, exploiting human desire for mystical knowledge and transcendence. Characters like Matheny describe how the society generates substantial profits by selling this "mysterious knowledge," often under the guise of spiritual awakening or ancient alien secrets. The story critiques such organizations by illustrating their insincerity—highlighting that the supposed wisdom is not rooted in genuine enlightenment but in financial gain and psychological manipulation. The plot underscores a deeper theme: the danger of believing in false promises of transformation, especially when those promises are delivered through illusion, secrecy, and emotional manipulation. Ultimately, the story warns against being seduced by organizations that claim to offer profound personal transformation while operating on the basis of deception and profit.
