The plot centers on a scientist, Professor Venetti, who is reluctantly drawn into a dangerous alliance with a gangster, Tony Carmen, who requests his invention—a device capable of disintegrating matter—to eliminate corrupt figures like Harry Keno. The scientist initially resists, viewing the request as unethical and illegal. However, when he activates the machine, it unexpectedly causes a bizarre and irreversible consequence: one of the massive square structures on the horizon vanishes, and the machine itself is halved. Carmen reveals that the invention is a disintegrator ray, and its use was not a test but a direct, violent act to eliminate Keno and his associates. The scientist is horrified by the implications, realizing he has unknowingly created a tool of murder. The story explores themes of scientific responsibility, moral ambiguity, and the unintended consequences of technology when wielded outside ethical boundaries. The gangster's use of the device under the guise of "cleaning up" corruption highlights the dangers of allowing scientific innovation to be exploited by criminal forces.
