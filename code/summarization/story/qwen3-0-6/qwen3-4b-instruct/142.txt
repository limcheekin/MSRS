Kevin's unique healing abilities remained undiscovered until he was twenty-six because, in a world where psychic powers are common, his lack of typical psi-abilities—such as telepathy or telekinesis—made him appear ordinary and overlooked. As a "psi-deficient," he was constantly dismissed by family and society, labeled as a "bundle of no-talent," and treated with pity or indifference. His family, particularly his mother, believed he had no latent powers and actively discouraged expectations of anything extraordinary in him. Even when others noticed unusual traits—like his ability to heal wounds—these were not recognized as psychic potential due to the prevailing belief that psi-powers were the only meaningful abilities. Additionally, societal structures and scientific testing focused exclusively on known psychic faculties, failing to include healing as a measurable or testable power. It wasn’t until the outbreak of war and the resulting surge in injuries that the world’s need for medical solutions became urgent. Only then did the government test individuals for healing potential, and Kevin’s unmatched effectiveness revealed his unique talent. Thus, his abilities remained hidden not due to secrecy, but because they were neither expected nor recognized within the established framework of psi-power evaluation until a critical need arose.
