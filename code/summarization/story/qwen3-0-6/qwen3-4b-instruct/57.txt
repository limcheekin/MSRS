Cassal is on his way to Tunney 21 because he has been assigned a critical mission by Neuronics, Inc., to secure the expertise of a relatively obscure researcher on that planet. This researcher has developed partial studies on instantaneous radio technology—specifically, a device known as Dimanche—which could revolutionize galactic communication by eliminating time lag. If perfected, such a technology would grant its creator or owner complete control over all interstellar communication, transport, and trade, establishing a galactic monopoly. Cassal’s role is not to study the research directly but to persuade the scientist to come to Earth and join Neuronics’ staff. To succeed, he must first guess the researcher’s price—before the scientist knows it—while also convincing him that Earth’s scientists are superior to the Tunnesians, particularly by highlighting the existence of Dimanche. Thus, Cassal’s journey is a high-stakes commercial operation driven by the potential for immense financial gain through technological dominance in interstellar commerce.
