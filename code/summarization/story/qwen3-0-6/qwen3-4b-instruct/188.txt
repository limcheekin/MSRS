Mom is the woman who lives in the strange, peculiar town and serves as a central, enigmatic figure in the narrative. Described as plump, apple-cheeked, and white-haired, she wears an old-fashioned nightgown and appears harmless at first glance—reminiscent of a political cartoon character. Her behavior is both warm and oddly rigid: she insists on hospitality without charge, offers no bedding, and maintains a strict routine, such as waking early for breakfast and missing court due to her "palace" duties. Her references to "the execution" and her sharp, dismissive tone ("Tush, nobody's asking you to pay") suggest a life steeped in unexplained rituals or local traditions. The name "Mom" is likely symbolic, evoking both familial warmth and the unsettling nature of the town’s peculiarities. In the context of the strange town filled with unusual happenings, Mom embodies the contrast between domestic simplicity and mysterious, perhaps supernatural, order. She acts as a grounding presence, yet her oddities—like her fixed schedule, strange comments, and unexplained references to events like an execution—hint at deeper, inexplicable forces at work in the town, making her a key figure in the story’s eerie atmosphere.
