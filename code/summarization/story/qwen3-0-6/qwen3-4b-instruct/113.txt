The setting of the story that revolves around the challenges faced by men and women from different solar systems as they navigate their complex desires for companionship and family is a future Earth where a severe gender imbalance has developed due to men's migration to other planets. On Earth, the ratio of women to men is 5:3, creating a society marked by social unrest, increased crime, and strict moral codes enforced by women-dominated communities. This imbalance drives the central conflict: how to address the scarcity of men and provide women with viable family and companionship options. The narrative explores solutions such as encouraging women to colonize other planets, where they can form families in environments with balanced gender ratios. The story unfolds across various settings—Earth’s cities, frontier colonies, and interplanetary travel points—highlighting cultural differences, societal pressures, and the emotional struggles of individuals trying to find love and stability. These settings reflect a world where traditional family structures are challenged by space colonization, and where desire for companionship is pitted against practical realities of gender distribution and planetary development. The core setting thus combines speculative futurism with social commentary on gender, migration, and human relationships.
