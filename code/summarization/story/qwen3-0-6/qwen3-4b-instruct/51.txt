The members aboard the life skiff with Malcolm after being marooned on a distant world are: Gregory Malcolm, Ralph Breadon, Sparks Hannigan, Tommy O'Doul (the cabin boy), J. Foster Andrews (the employer), Enid Andrews (his wife), Crystal Andrews (his daughter), Maud Andrews (his sister), and 'Tina Laney (the maid). They survive the crash of the space yacht *Carefree* and are stranded on what is later identified as Titan, a moon of Saturn. The group is initially disorganized and fearful, but gradually comes together to assess their situation and plan for survival. Despite initial arguments and confusion, they form a makeshift community to face the dangers of their isolated existence in an unknown and potentially hostile environment.
