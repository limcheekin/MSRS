The plot of the story about a young man's ambition to achieve something extraordinary, despite facing skepticism from those around him, centers on Ben, who dreams of piloting the first manned rocket to the Moon. Unlike others who see space exploration as a distant or impractical goal, Ben is driven by an intense personal desire to achieve this historic feat. His ambition is rooted in both a deep fascination with space and a longing for meaning beyond routine Earth life. Despite being labeled as merely "old stuff" by others—such as the Moon Patrol offer—he remains steadfast in his pursuit of deep space travel. The narrative highlights his internal struggle, showing how his dream is met with disbelief and dismissiveness from society, especially when compared to more conventional or immediate opportunities. Yet, Ben’s resolve persists, and he chooses to stay grounded on Earth, valuing human connection—particularly with Laura—and the emotional richness of everyday life. This decision underscores the theme that true ambition is not just about technological achievement, but also about personal integrity and emotional depth. The story ultimately portrays a young man who, despite skepticism, remains committed to an extraordinary goal, blending idealism with emotional realism.
