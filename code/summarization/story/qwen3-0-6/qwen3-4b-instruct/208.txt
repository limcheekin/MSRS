Mackenzie is a crew member and a former astrogator who plays a pivotal role in the story about a man's ambition to reach the moon. He is deeply concerned about the psychological toll of space travel on individuals, especially those who become overly focused on their success as rocket pilots. Mackenzie intervenes by hypnotizing Ish, a talented but isolated pilot, to erase his hallucination of having previously visited the Moon. This hallucination, which had made the actual mission feel mundane and unexciting, was a result of Ish’s emotional detachment from life beyond space travel—lacking family, friends, and personal interests. Mackenzie believes that such emotional isolation makes a pilot vulnerable to losing their sense of self and possibly failing to return from space. After the hypnosis, Ish no longer remembers his supposed prior Moon journey and becomes emotionally grounded. However, he ultimately dies on the space station, never entering a rocket again, as he is unable to reconcile his life with Earth’s emotional realities. Mackenzie's actions underscore the story’s central theme: that true courage in space travel requires not just technical skill, but emotional connection and human resilience.
