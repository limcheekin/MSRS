The setting of the story involving the mysterious organization POSAT is a modern, slightly surreal world where a secret society claims to offer ultimate knowledge and solutions to life’s problems. The narrative unfolds in a contemporary urban environment, with key scenes taking place in ordinary locations such as bus seats, mailrooms, offices, and homes. The atmosphere is one of quiet intrigue, blending the mundane with the mysterious. The organization, POSAT (Perpetual Order of Seekers After Truth), operates through advertisements in magazines and newspapers, reaching people through familiar channels like *Your Life and Psychology* or *The Bulletin of Physical Research*. The story’s setting emphasizes the contrast between public skepticism and personal fascination—Bill Evans, Miss Arnable, and Donald Alford each respond to the ad differently, reflecting a society where individuals navigate doubt, curiosity, and hope. The setting also subtly hints at deeper layers of secrecy and surveillance, as the organization’s responses become increasingly personal and intrusive, culminating in a mysterious invitation for a personal interview. This blend of everyday life and hidden danger creates a tense, thought-provoking backdrop that underscores the story's central theme: the allure and peril of promises of absolute knowledge.
