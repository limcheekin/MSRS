The story follows Ben, a young cadet in the U.S. Academy of Interplanetary Flight, as he faces the emotional and existential challenges of being chosen for a historic journey into space. The plot centers on Ben's internal struggle between his dream of becoming a spaceman and the desire to stay with his loved ones, particularly Laura, whom he loves deeply. As he prepares for launch, he grapples with fear, doubt, and the harsh realities of space travel—such as the potential dangers of long-term missions, the isolation of deep space, and the tragic consequences of past accidents like the *Cyclops* incident, where radiation exposure led to a monstrous child. Ben is torn between accepting a position on the *Odyssey*, which would take him deep into space toward planets like Mars, Jupiter, and beyond, and staying behind to teach and build a life with Laura, raising a family. The story explores themes of love, sacrifice, duty, and the human cost of exploration. Ultimately, Ben is overwhelmed by the weight of his decision, realizing that choosing space means potentially sacrificing personal happiness and family. Yet, the journey into space remains a symbol of human aspiration, and the emotional depth of his conflict underscores the profound challenge of balancing dreams with real-life commitments.
