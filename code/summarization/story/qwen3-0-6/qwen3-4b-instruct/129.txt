Isobar Jones is a lunar outpost technician stationed at Lunar III, a remote outpost serving as a rocket refueling station and meteorological base. He is described as mild-tempered but deeply homesick, yearning for the natural world—particularly grass, flowers, and trees—that he has been denied access to due to strict rules prohibiting outside excursions. His only source of solace is his bagpipes, which he plays in secret, despite being ordered to cease by Dome Commander Eagan due to the noise disturbing the station’s operations.

Throughout the narrative, Isobar’s emotional state deteriorates as he becomes increasingly isolated and disillusioned. When Patrolmen Roberts and Brown go outside for emergency repairs, Isobar secretly ventures beyond the Dome to join them, driven by a desperate need to experience the natural world. He discovers that the Grannies—deadly, unyielding lunar creatures—are threatening the group. In a moment of courage, Isobar helps the injured Patrolmen escape by guiding them to a tree for shelter, saving their lives despite being unarmed and unprepared.

His actions demonstrate a transformation from passive resignation to active bravery, revealing that his deep emotional connection to Earth’s natural beauty is not just nostalgia, but a fundamental human need. Ultimately, Isobar’s journey—from frustration and rebellion to self-sacrificial heroism—shows that true resilience lies not in compliance, but in the courage to defy limits when necessary.
