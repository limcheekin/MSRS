Tony Carmen is a Mafia racketeer who seeks out a scientist, Professor Venetti, to help him dispose of bodies left at his club by criminal associates. Unable to legally eliminate the corpses, Carmen turns to Venetti’s government-funded invention—a device capable of disintegrating waste—despite Venetti’s initial reluctance and scientific concerns. Carmen claims the machine works by emitting a disintegrator ray that vanishes matter, and he believes it can eliminate bodies without leaving physical evidence. He insists the device doesn’t burn or chemically destroy materials, suggesting instead that the matter is transported into another dimension or the future. However, Carmen’s deeper motive goes beyond disposal—he fears bodies appearing again in the future, which could lead to detection or legal consequences. When Venetti demonstrates the machine, it unexpectedly destroys a large square structure, confirming its destructive power. Carmen then uses the invention to eliminate Harry Keno and his associates in their hideout, asserting that no *corpus delecti* (body of the crime) remains. This act marks a pivotal shift from mere disposal to active, violent criminal intervention, positioning Tony Carmen as both a dangerous criminal and a pragmatic user of science to achieve his goals.
