The plot of *Captain Midas* revolves around Captain Ben Menthlo, a retired spaceman who discovers a derelict space vessel floating in the void containing vast quantities of gold. The discovery sparks greed among the crew, particularly in his Third Officer, Spinelli, who is immediately drawn to the treasure and secretly plans to seize it. Captain Menthlo, aware of the danger and the moral corruption it may unleash, carefully hides the truth about the gold from the rest of the crew, assigning only trusted personnel to handle the derelict. However, the temptation proves too strong, and tensions rise as suspicions grow between the crew members. Ultimately, the captain is punished by the Foundation for his role in the incident, and he is barred from future missions. He is left with a pension and a hospital bed, haunted by the consequences of his fateful discovery. The story illustrates how greed corrupts even the most disciplined individuals, and how the pursuit of treasure leads to betrayal, suffering, and personal downfall—making it a cautionary tale about the price of ambition in space.
