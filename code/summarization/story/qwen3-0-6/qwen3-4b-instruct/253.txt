The "snoll doper" is a secret, coded object used by a group of women—Kay, Klio, Yoolna, and Jilka—who belong to an amateur, quixotic secret society. It functions as a disguised weapon or device, specifically a long, tubular metallic object resembling a shotgun, which Kay reveals to the protagonist after he asks what it is. The term "snoll doper" is not a literal object but a code word within their society, used to conceal their activities. Its purpose is both symbolic and practical: it represents a form of covert communication and physical tool for enacting their mission, which involves selecting and transporting individuals who reject conventional social norms—particularly regarding sexuality and conformity. The society operates with humor and irony, using coded messages hidden in books like Taine’s *History of English Literature*, indicating that they are not involved in any Martian or interplanetary threat, but rather in a playful, self-contained ritual. The "snoll doper" thus serves as a key element in the story’s exploration of identity, rebellion, and the subversion of societal expectations through secrecy and shared understanding among women.
