In the story set in a futuristic setting, a flitterboat is a small, personal spacecraft used for short-distance travel within the asteroid belt. It is equipped with a single gravitoinertial engine and contains minimal life support—air, water, and limited food—making it more economical than full-sized spaceships. Flitterboats are not suited for long journeys due to the physical limitations of wearing a vacuum suit for extended periods; instead, travelers must hop between blinking navigation beacons, which slows their average velocity. The flitterboat is used when a character, such as the protagonist Daniel Oak, needs to travel from one beacon to another—like moving from Raven’s Rest toward Ceres—to navigate the Belt efficiently. These vessels serve as essential personal transportation in a setting where large ships are impractical due to cost and logistical constraints. The flitterboat thus becomes a symbol of the gritty, human-scale challenges of interplanetary travel in a vast, unforgiving system.
