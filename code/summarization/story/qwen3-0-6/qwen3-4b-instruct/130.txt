The story centers on John Smith, a unique musician with a fiddle called the Zloomph, who struggles to find a place in the world despite his exceptional talent. The group of performers—featuring a bassist, clarinetist, pianist, and others—faces the challenge of keeping their gig alive, especially after they discover that John is not just a skilled musician but someone deeply entangled with a mysterious and dangerous "hole" in reality. John claims he fell through a hole in space-time while trying to prove a theory about ancient history and time dimensions, and now he must return to his own time dimension before he is discredited. While the group initially tries to persuade him to join their ensemble, John is hesitant, as he is consumed by his obsession with finding a specific hole—his lost time dimension. Though he agrees to play with them, his real motivation lies in solving a scientific and existential puzzle. The plot unfolds as a blend of whimsy and mystery, where music becomes a bridge between the physical world and the metaphysical, and the performers' survival depends on John's return—not just as a musician, but as a man navigating dimensions and time. Ultimately, the story explores how art and personal identity intersect with the universe’s hidden truths.
