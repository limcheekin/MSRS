The setting of the story involving a crew exploring an ancient, seemingly desolate planet and uncovering remnants of a forgotten civilization is a barren, arid world known as Tintera. This planet has no atmosphere suitable for human life, lacks vegetation, and is devoid of any signs of recent habitation. The landscape is dominated by vast, shattered cities composed of dark, cloud-like glass fragments, with large circular holes at their centers—indicating massive destruction. The planet's surface is cold and desolate, and the environment is hostile, with radiation levels rising as the crew descends. The only life forms observed are robotic remnants, such as a black, gliding robot moving across the terrain, suggesting a long-dead, advanced civilization that may have once thrived here. The planet’s isolation and extreme conditions—combined with the absence of any signs of life or activity—emphasize its desolation, while the ruins imply that a once-advanced society collapsed violently, possibly due to war or environmental collapse. The crew's exploration reveals that the planet is not just abandoned but was the site of a forgotten civilization whose remnants remain frozen in time, offering a haunting glimpse into a lost technological and societal past.
