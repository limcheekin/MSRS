The plot centers on Eric North, a man who arrives at a mysterious, enchanting city that appears to be on Mars. Initially believing it to be a fantasy, he is drawn by legends of beauty and music. However, as he approaches the city, he begins to experience vivid illusions—first of a utopian, jewel-laden world, then a horrifyingly dark, twisted version where the people hate him with red eyes. The city reveals a dark secret: it is not a physical place but a psychic manifestation that recognizes and targets individuals whose names match ancient legends. When Eric identifies himself as "Eric the Bronze," a legendary figure from a previous generation, the city reacts violently, attacking his mind and body. The story unfolds as Eric realizes he is being manipulated by the city's illusions, which feed on his identity. Using a metal helmet designed to block psychic waves, he briefly resists but ultimately discovers it fails when the city adapts. He escapes, returns to his ship, and plans to destroy the city, realizing that it is not just a place of fantasy but a dangerous entity tied to his own suppressed identity. The narrative ultimately explores themes of self-identity, psychological manipulation, and the peril of confronting one’s hidden past.
