Daniel Oak is not involved in the development or operation of advanced robotics or interstellar travel in the story. Instead, his role is that of a consultant and investigator working within a corporate security context on Ceres. His involvement centers around thwarting espionage and sabotage efforts by rival companies—specifically Baedecker Metals & Mining Corporation and Thurston’s agents—aimed at ousting Jack Ravenhurst from managerial control of Viking. Oak is approached by Brock, a Security Guard officer, to assist in uncovering and countering these threats, despite being under Ravenhurst’s employment and having previously been criticized for revealing sensitive information. While Oak does not operate time machines or advanced robotics, he uses his investigative skills and access to intelligence to help protect the company’s interests. His job, therefore, is that of a security consultant and problem-solver in a corporate espionage scenario, not related to interstellar travel or robotics.
