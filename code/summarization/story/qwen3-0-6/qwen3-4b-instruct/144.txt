Kevin and his mother share a complex, strained relationship marked by both deep concern and subtle resentment. His mother, a psychiatrist, is deeply aware of Kevin’s unique lack of psi abilities, which she sees as a personal and familial flaw. Despite her efforts to support him, she fails to truly understand his inner world, which is open and raw due to the absence of a mental shield. This lack of empathy underscores the emotional distance between them. Kevin, in turn, feels seen only as a problem or a burden, and his attempts at connection—like forming a bond with Lucy or expressing romantic hope—are met with skepticism or dismissal. Their relationship reflects the broader family dynamic: one of control, judgment, and unacknowledged pain, where Kevin’s difference is both a source of shame and a barrier to genuine emotional intimacy.
