The cylinder is a mysterious, sentient device hidden behind Michaelson’s ear that serves as a portal to a sacred, ancient city existing half a million years ago. It is significant because it enables direct mental communication with the inhabitants of this lost civilization, particularly with Maota, a being who embodies the collective consciousness of the city. The cylinder functions as a gateway to a state of non-local existence—where time, space, and physical form dissolve—allowing the user to perceive the entire world, including Earth, from a timeless, omniscient perspective. Its significance lies not only in its technological or scientific oddity but in its role as a bridge between the present and a forgotten, spiritually advanced past. The cylinder's power is tied to consciousness and memory; when activated, it transports Michaelson into a realm of pure awareness where he can see the city and experience its timeless presence. This suggests that the cylinder is not merely a machine but a living artifact of a lost civilization that preserved its consciousness through thought and shared memory, making it central to understanding both the mystery of the sacred city and the nature of human consciousness beyond time.
