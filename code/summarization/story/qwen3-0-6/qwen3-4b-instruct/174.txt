The setting of the story involving explorers encountering a seemingly idyllic yet dangerous planet inhabited by a small, isolated colony is the planet Minos. This planet, located in a distant part of space, has been settled by a small colony of people who are descendants of a group that survived a plague known as the "melting sickness." The colonists, all related through descent from the Mead family, have developed a unique cultural identity, appearing physically similar to one another due to their shared lineage. The planet is described as having breathable air, a natural environment reminiscent of Earth, and a society that has existed in relative isolation for generations. However, the setting is fraught with danger, as the colonists are unaware of the arrival of the explorers, and their primitive technology and lack of medical knowledge make them vulnerable to external threats. The story unfolds as the explorers, including June Walton and her companions, arrive on Minos, initially mistaking the planet for a peaceful, uncolonized world, only to discover its hidden dangers and the deep-rooted societal structure shaped by survival and isolation. The setting thus blends a sense of wonder with underlying peril, highlighting the tension between perceived idyll and the reality of danger in a remote, isolated colony.
