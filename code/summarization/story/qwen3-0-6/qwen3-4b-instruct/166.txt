In the story, the Lorelei is not a mythical figure but a dangerous, femalevolent entity who operates in space, specifically among the asteroids. She is a space-based villain who uses her beauty and allure to lure space travelers—particularly those carrying the valuable substance ekalastron—into deadly traps. Her "rock" is an asteroid in the Belt, where she resides and exerts her influence. She does not sing but calls out to victims, using a voice that booms through their ship's systems and induces panic and disorientation. The story reveals that she is both physically and psychologically formidable, capable of manipulating crew members and even turning them into mindless, fused monstrosities. Her true form is shown in the laboratory of Lorelei Cooper, where she is revealed to be a human woman, but her actions suggest a transformation or possession by an alien force. The narrative presents her as a siren-like figure of space horror, combining mythic allure with real-world danger, making her a central antagonist in the conflict involving space travel, cargo theft, and extraterrestrial invasion.
