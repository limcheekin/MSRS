George Faircloth and Marge Faircloth are married, but their relationship is strained and marked by deep resentment and emotional manipulation. George, fed up with Marge's constant complaints, jealousy, and emotional demands, contemplates using a robotic husband—George Prime—to replace him at home. He believes this will provide him with relief from the stress of marriage, allowing him to pursue romantic relationships with other women like Jeree, Sybil, or Dorothy. However, he soon discovers that Marge is not only aware of George Prime’s existence but has been actively using the robot as a tool to manipulate and control the household dynamics. She employs psychological tactics—such as feigning affection, creating false impressions, and subtly monitoring George Prime’s behavior—to maintain her own power and emotional dominance. In truth, Marge never intended to run away with the robot; instead, she orchestrated George Prime's presence as a means of asserting control over George’s life and ensuring his emotional dependence on her. Thus, their relationship is not one of betrayal or infidelity, but of calculated manipulation—Marge, the more intelligent and strategic partner, uses George Prime as a psychological weapon to keep George emotionally bound to her, while George remains unaware of the depth of her control until it’s too late.
