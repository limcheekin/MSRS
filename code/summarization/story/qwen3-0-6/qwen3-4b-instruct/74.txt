Star possesses a jet weapon and an electron knife as his primary equipment throughout his struggles against space pirates. The jet weapon, a compact jet-blast capsule housed in a small, pen-like device, is used for rapid, decisive attacks, such as when Star fires it to kill Devil Garrett. The electron knife, which he uses with precision and speed, serves as a close-range weapon, allowing him to disarm or incapacitate enemies effectively. These tools are central to his combat effectiveness, enabling him to outmaneuver and defeat opponents despite being outnumbered or disarmed. Star’s use of the jet and electron knife demonstrates both his tactical awareness and physical prowess, contributing to his reputation as "Death Star" Blade. The equipment is not only functional but also symbolic—representing his resourcefulness and ability to fight back against overwhelming odds. Through these weapons, Star maintains control in high-stakes confrontations, ultimately triumphing over the pirate Devil Garrett.
