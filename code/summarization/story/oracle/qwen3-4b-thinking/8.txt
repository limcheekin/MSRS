The Ghost Ship in "Galactic Ghost" serves as a profound symbol of the inescapable psychological and existential trauma caused by space isolation. For Willard, it initially represents the haunting memory of Earth—a phantom manifestation of his deepest longing for home, triggered by his friend Dobbin’s dying wish. When Willard later sees the Ghost Ship again, it becomes a literal embodiment of his own despair: a spectral ship that mirrors the ship he’s been stranded on for decades. Crucially, the story resolves this by revealing the Ghost Ship is not supernatural but a scientific reality—fragments of matter from the *Mary Lou* itself, now reduced to a "vague outline" due to cosmic decay. As the captain explains, all objects in space gradually lose mass and energy until they become "mere shells," existing only as relative phenomena for other ships
