The significance of time in this story lies in its fundamental redefinition as a dimension rather than a linear sequence of events. Through the character of Mr. Snader and subsequent explanations, the narrative challenges conventional perceptions by revealing that time is not a fixed stream where past events are immutable. When Jeff realizes he's been transported to a future where the United States no longer exists (Costa, West Goodland in the Continental Federation), he confronts the concept that his "past" has already been altered.

The story's most critical insight emerges when the lawyer explains that time is a dimension where specific points don't correspond to historical events
