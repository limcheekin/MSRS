The story follows Jeff Elliott, a color engineer who needs a job after losing his lease on a building he planned to use for a television company. At a restaurant, he meets Snader, a man who claims to be from "another time" and offers to show them a time travel opportunity. After some hesitation, Jeff and his wife Ann accept Snader's invitation to take a "free trial trip" to a different time period.

Snader takes them to what appears to be a time travel station where they experience a brief journey through time. They end up in the future—specifically in "Costa, West Goodland," a city in the Continental Federation where the United States no longer exists as a recognized country.
