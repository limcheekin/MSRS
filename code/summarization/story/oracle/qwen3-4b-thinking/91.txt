The setting of this story is a secluded, rocky island off the coast of Maine with a quiet, almost timeless quality. Jack Barr discovers the island after landing in a cove that appears untouched by time—described as "as glassy as if the Annie O. had always been there." The island is small (only a quarter mile wide) with a rocky spine connecting it to a second, smaller island that Jack initially misses due to the cove's natural concealment.
