The story takes place in a rural American farming community that has been dramatically transformed following an unspecified global catastrophe. Harry Burr lives on what was once his farm, now surrounded by fallow fields and new regulations that govern nearly every aspect of life. The setting is characterized by a stark contrast between Harry's memories of the past and the altered present: he recalls working with livestock and crops, but now only maintains a vegetable patch due to new wheat and corn restrictions.
