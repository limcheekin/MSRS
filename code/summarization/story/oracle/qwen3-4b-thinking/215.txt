The story follows Jon Karyl, a lone "Lone Watcher" stationed on an asteroid who becomes the target of mysterious alien encounters. While performing routine maintenance on a rocket engine, Jon is unexpectedly discovered by six steel-blue robotic creatures who land nearby. Fleeing through the asteroid's rocky terrain, he reaches an underground service station but is soon pursued again by the aliens.

Inside the station, Jon learns the Steel-Blues have been conducting a 21-day "liquid test" on him.
