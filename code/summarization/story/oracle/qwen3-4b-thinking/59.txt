The story is set on Godolph, a weather-controlled planet in the center of the galaxy (inside the third ring), where Cassal finds himself stranded after his ship to Tunney 21 is delayed. Godolph's city features a unique transportation system called the "transport tide" - a network of water channels that flow at different levels throughout the city, allowing inhabitants to enter and be carried silently to their destinations. This system creates a dangerous environment for humans, as Cassal describes it as "Venice revised, brought up to date in a faster-than-light age."

The setting is particularly perilous for an Earthman like Cassal due to Godolph's physical and social characteristics. The planet's weather is controlled but presents challenges for humans - rain falls constantly, and water channels create hazards where humans could easily be swept away if they attempt to use them. The city's narrow walkways border the transport tide, creating a situation where human pedestrians have a definite disadvantage compared to native Godolphians who are accustomed to this environment.
