The story explores a cosmic revelation where Earth itself is a camouflaged spaceship from an ancient, advanced civilization. Initially, the narrative focuses on the unsettling disappearance of Mars' two moons, Phobos and Deimos, which sparks concern among the Wolver family who are investigating strange astronomical events.
