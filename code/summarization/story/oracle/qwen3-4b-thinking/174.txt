The setting of the story is the planet Minos, a seemingly idyllic world with vibrant, colorful forests featuring green, copper, purple, and fiery red leaves that create an enchanting yet alien landscape. The explorers from the spaceship Explorer encounter this planet after traveling 36 light years, finding it appears "like an Earth forest in the fall" but not actually in fall season. Minos hosts a small, isolated colony of 150 people who have lived there for three generations, having survived a devastating disease called "melting sickness" that killed most of the original settlers except the Mead family.

The planet presents both beauty and danger: its lush environment appears perfectly healthy to the explorers, but it harbors biological differences that make human survival challenging. The colonists have undergone a unique genetic adaptation process through "test-tube evolution" where human cells were modified to digest Minos's food, which has different chemical properties than Earth food.
