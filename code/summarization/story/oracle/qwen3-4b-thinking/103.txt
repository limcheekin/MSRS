The setting of the story is a mysterious, artificial city on Mars that appears as a beautiful, captivating place but has sinister psychological effects. The narrative begins with Eric North discovering this city through a canal system on the Martian surface. The city is described as "strange, fantastic, beautiful" with "spires and minarets that twinkled in the distance," yet it simultaneously exudes menace as it lures Eric into its grasp with alluring music and imagery.

As the story unfolds, Eric learns this city is not natural but rather a massive machine built by ancient Martians to protect a small colony. The city functions through thought-translation technology that converts imagined objects and concepts into physical reality. It's described as "the heart of it lies underneath our feet, in caverns beneath this building" and operates by capturing thoughts, transmuting Martian red dust into materials, and assembling them according to recorded mental patterns.
