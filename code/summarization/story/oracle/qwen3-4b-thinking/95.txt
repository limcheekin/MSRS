The story follows Michael Frey, a young man who leaves his secluded Angeleno Brotherhood to enter the diverse society of Portyork in the United Universe—a cosmic community where all planets have adopted universal customs and tabus to prevent conflict. Michael, having studied the "Universal Laws" before leaving, quickly realizes that understanding these rules is far more complex than he anticipated. He encounters a wide array of alien species with unique cultural norms: Meropians who consider the word "history" offensive, Theemimians who prohibit speaking about eating in public, and Talithans who view marriage as slavery. His guide, Pierce B. Carpenter, helps him navigate these customs but also reveals the society's contradictions—such as the ban on marriage despite Michael's desire for a relationship.
