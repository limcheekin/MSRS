The melting sickness is a fatal disease that affected the human colony on Minos two years after their arrival, killing nearly all inhabitants except the Mead families who were immune. According to Pat Mead, the disease was caused by fundamental biochemical differences between Earth and Minos—specifically, the amino acid structures in Minos food were incompatible with human digestion. People who hadn't adapted to Minos' environment would consume food that their bodies couldn't process, causing their cells to begin breaking down.

The disease's true nature was revealed through Pat's explanation of his people's unique adaptation process. Alexander P. Mead, a plant geneticist, had engineered a solution by taking human cells and transforming them into phagocytes that could evolve to digest Minos food. These cells underwent thousands of generations of selective pressure—living through hunger and hardship with alien food—over just six months. Once adapted, the cells were reintroduced into the Mead family members, allowing them to hunt and farm while others remained dependent on hydroponic food.
