The story centers on Jack Barr, a biology student who sails to a secluded island off Maine, where he encounters Mary Alice Pope, a young woman who appears to be from 1933 rather than 1953. Mary lives with her aunts on the island, receiving mysterious "boxes" containing poems, books, and notes signed "Your Lover" every day. Jack discovers that Mary has been living in this time loop for 18 years, believing it's 1933 when it's actually 1953.
