Dimanche is not a person but an electronic device embedded within Cassal's body that functions as his personal security and analytical system. It's a specialized instrument capable of scanning nervous systems, collecting and interpreting physiological data, and monitoring environmental threats. Unlike a human companion, Dimanche operates through subvocalized communication that Cassal can hear only in his ear, providing real-time assessments of potential dangers.
