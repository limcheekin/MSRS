The setting of the story is Portyork, the largest spaceport in the United Universe, described as the most cosmopolitan city in a future where all planets have adopted universal customs and tabus to prevent conflict. This world has existed in peace for 500 years due to the "system of universal kinship" established by the "Wise Ones," which ensures that all planetary customs and tabus are standardized across the galaxy.
