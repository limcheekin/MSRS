The mysterious hanged figure serves as deliberate "bait" in the story, designed by the alien invaders to identify those who had escaped their control. When Ed Loyce discovers the body hanging from the lamppost in Pikeville, he initially believes it represents some kind of local tragedy or crime. However, later in the narrative, he learns that the hanged man was specifically placed there as part of the aliens' strategy to test the town's population.

As revealed by the Commissioner at the end of the story, "To draw you out. Make you declare yourself. So they'd know who was under control—and who had escaped." This explains why the figure was hanging in such an obvious, unsettling way—because it was meant to be seen and reacted to.
