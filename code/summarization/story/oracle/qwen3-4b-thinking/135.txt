The story follows Michael and Mary, a couple who return to Earth after a 2,000-year space expedition. They discover Earth has been "burned to a cinder" with no habitable planets left in the galaxy, as the people they were sent to save had already destroyed their home world through atomic warfare and greed. Upon landing, they learn that humanity has been living in a controlled environment for centuries because they can't tolerate the sight of violent death—people have been conditioned to avoid any knowledge of natural mortality after a historical event where seeing a violent death caused widespread insanity.

Michael and Mary confront the council who have been hiding the truth about Earth's condition, showing them a projection of violent deaths in space that would cause panic. The council decides to isolate them and change their report to protect public sanity. However, Michael and Mary choose to defy this by requesting permission to leave the city and live on Earth's surface.
