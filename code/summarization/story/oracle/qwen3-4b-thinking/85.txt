The story follows Cadet Marshall Farnsworth, a young man selected as the first human to travel into space. Initially overwhelmed by anxiety after waking from a nightmare about explosions, Marshall grapples with his fear of being too young for such a historic journey while his family struggles to maintain composure. His parents show visible distress but attempt to project confidence, knowing the high risk of failure in space travel. 

After rigorous training, Marshall boards the rocket at Skyharbor, where he experiences extreme forces as the vehicle accelerates through the atmosphere. During flight, he views Earth from 350 miles above, describing the planet's beauty and the vastness of space. However, his zero-gravity training fails to prevent a sudden panic when he nearly loses control outside the ship, forcing him to use quick reflexes to avoid a dangerous collision.

The mission culminates in a challenging re-entry phase where Marshall must manually control the rocket's descent through the atmosphere, navigating braking maneuvers to ensure a safe landing. After successfully completing the historic flight and returning to Earth, Marshall is greeted by crowds of onlookers who celebrate his achievement as the first person to journey into space.
