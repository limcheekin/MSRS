The story "Home Is Where You Left It" follows Steve Cantwell, a young man who returns to his childhood desert village on Sirius' second planet after the Earth colony has been forced to evacuate. The village has been abandoned with its well poisoned, and Steve learns from an elderly Kumaji man that the Earth colonists had to flee because their water source was contaminated and the Kumaji were hunting them.

Steve decides to help the surviving colonists make their perilous journey toward Oasis City, a settlement 500 miles away. During this time, he encounters Tobias Whiting, a former successful trader with the Kumaji who has become bitter after losing his wealth due to the colony's sudden abandonment. Whiting steals Steve's unicopter at night to try to trade with the Kumaji for his lost money, believing he can regain his fortune through betrayal.
