The setting of the story is an ancient, mysterious city on Alpha Centaurus II that has existed for half a million years, remarkably preserved despite its isolation from modern civilization. The city presents a stunning blend of organic and geometric architecture—square buildings, ellipsoid structures, and spheroid forms connected by delicate, dream-like bridges that still swing in the wind after eons. Though partially buried in centuries of sand, the city remains visually striking with ebony surfaces catching the late afternoon sun, while desert plants grow from the roofs of smaller buildings.

The city appears both dead and alive, its remnants showing signs of advanced civilization that has long since vanished. The ruins are covered in layers of time—sand has filled doorways, and weathered surfaces display ancient inscriptions that hint at a sophisticated culture. The atmosphere is palpable with a sense of history and mystery, where the city seems to breathe with spirits that the native guardian, Maota, believes inhabit it.
