The story follows four medical explorers from the ship *Explorer* who discover a hidden colony on the seemingly idyllic planet Minos. Initially mistaken for a peaceful frontier settlement, the explorers quickly learn that the Minos inhabitants are descendants of a small group who arrived three generations ago and have undergone a remarkable biological adaptation process. The colonists explain that their ancestors, led by Alexander P. Mead, deliberately engineered human cells to survive on Minos through "test-tube evolution" - a process where white blood cells were subjected to thousands of generations of selective pressure to develop the ability to digest the planet's unique food chemistry
