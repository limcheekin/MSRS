The story follows Kaiser, a space explorer stranded on the distant planet Big Muddy after his scout ship crashes. Initially believing he's simply recovering from an illness, Kaiser soon discovers he's been invaded by a symbiote—an organism that has taken up residence in his body and is causing significant changes to his physical and mental state. The unusual communications he receives from the mother ship, Soscites II, reveal the symbiote's influence: messages begin appearing in baby talk, with Kaiser himself sending phrases like "OO IS SICK, SMOKY. DO TO BEDDY-BY. KEEP UM WARM" that he can no longer comprehend.

As Kaiser investigates, he learns the symbiote is attempting to help him adapt to his new environment by altering his physiology and psychology. It causes him to change color, have difficulty eating, and speak in baby-like language—traits he initially mistakes for illness.
