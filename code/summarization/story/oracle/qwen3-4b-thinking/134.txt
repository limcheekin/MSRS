John Smith is a peculiar character described as a "little guy, an animated matchstick with a flat, broad face" who has "sand-colored mop of hair" resembling dry grass. He wears clothing "something off a park bench" and has "watery, pale blue eyes." Despite his small stature, he possesses extraordinary musical talent that transforms the story's setting. His unusual instrument is a Zloomph—a five-thousand-year-old, midnight-black hour-glass-shaped object described as "more like a monstrous, midnight-black hour-glass than a bass." 

John explains the Zloomph is the only one of its kind in existence, and it has a special "hole" that must be found to return to his original time dimension. He's searching for this hole after accidentally falling through one while returning to the University to prove his theory about ancient musical instruments existing before recordings.
