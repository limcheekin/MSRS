Barry Barr undergoes profound physical and existential transformations during his time on Venus. Initially a human Earthman assigned to Venus Colony as a structural engineer, he experiences dramatic changes after being exposed to Sigma radiation while performing a critical task outside the ship. The radiation causes him to develop membranous webs between his fingers and toes, pronounced swellings in his neck, and dark parallel lines across his body. These early signs indicate an ongoing metamorphosis.

The most significant change occurs when Barry's body develops rudimentary gills, allowing him to breathe underwater—a transformation that fundamentally separates him from his human identity. Initially struggling with this new ability, he experiences severe respiratory distress as he attempts to adapt. Dr. Jensen, the colony doctor, confirms that Barry is undergoing "certain radical changes" and has developed "rudimentary gills" that provide relief from his breathing difficulties.
