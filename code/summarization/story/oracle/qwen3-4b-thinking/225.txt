The story follows Ed Loyce, a practical man who discovers a dead body hanging from a lamppost in his town's square. As he becomes increasingly distressed about the body, he notices the unsettling indifference of his neighbors—they walk past it without acknowledgment, treating it as normal. This prompts him to investigate further, leading to a terrifying realization: his entire town has been taken over by alien entities capable of mimicking humans and controlling minds. These "insects" (as Loyce describes them) have infiltrated from the top down, starting with authorities and spreading through the community. 

Loyce's discovery is significant because he was working in his basement digging foundations when the invasion occurred, placing him outside the aliens' control field. This accidental immunity allows him to escape the town while others remain under their influence
