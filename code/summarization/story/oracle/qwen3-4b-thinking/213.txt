The story's setting takes place on Alphard Six, a cool green planet described as "an airy jewel of a world" that contrasts sharply with the arid environments humans have previously encountered. This distant world features a single dun-colored moon and contains a central city with a square where a grounded spaceship—approximately ten times larger than the explorers' ship, Marco Four—rests in disrepair. The planet also has twenty-seven small agrarian hamlets surrounded by cultivated fields, creating a distinct agricultural society.
