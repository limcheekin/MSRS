Sergeant Andy McCloud begins the story as a frustrated noncom (non-commissioned officer) who has been "entombed" in Washington for two years, feeling ignored by military brass who suddenly discover his office deep within the Pentagon during an alarming epidemic. Despite being placed in charge of a mysterious illness that affects writers, poets, office workers in small offices, and college students but not government workers or doctors, Andy faces immediate challenges from impatient officers who attempt to relieve him of duty. He works tirelessly with Corporal Bettijean to analyze reports, identify patterns, and understand the epidemic's nature while battling bureaucratic inertia.

Through meticulous investigation, Andy eventually discovers the epidemic is caused by contaminated four-cent stamps—people who lick stamps (common among writers, artists, and those in small offices) ingest the poison.
