The story follows a group of lunar explorers who discover a massive, alien-shaped object on the Moon's Mare Serenitatis. After investigating, they enter what turns out to be a spaceship from another galaxy that had been left as a "booby trap" by an advanced civilization. This extraterrestrial race had anticipated human space travel and placed the ship on the Moon to observe humanity when it developed spaceflight capabilities.

The group becomes trapped inside the ship, which they learn will transport them to their makers' planet for six months of study. The ship's electronic brain controls everything and communicates with them through a mechanical voice. Despite their efforts to find ways to escape or alter the ship's course, they discover there's no way to damage the ship or reach the control systems.

The tension escalates when Kane, the group's most determined member, becomes convinced they must find a solution to their predicament. After drinking heavily and becoming increasingly agitated, he ties them all up and threatens to kill them if the ship doesn't return them to the Moon alive.
