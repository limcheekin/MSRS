Captain Midas is the protagonist of this science fiction story, who serves as the captain of the spacecraft "Martian Maid" during a mission to the outer solar system. In the narrative, he recounts his discovery of a massive derelict ship made of an alien metal that transformed into gold upon contact with human bodies.

Captain Midas is characterized by his advanced age and physical deterioration - his hair is gray, his face is described as "a mask" with "flesh hanging on my bones like a yellow cloth on a rickety frame." He's also portrayed as a man who has become disillusioned with space exploration, having been part of the early space pioneers who were "not seeking the spaceways for the advancement of civilization" but rather for personal gain
