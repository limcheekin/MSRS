The story "Galactic Ghost" follows Willard, a lone space traveler who experiences profound loneliness after his best friend Dobbin dies aboard their ship, the Mary Lou. Willard spends years in space, haunted by the memory of Earth and his lost companions, while struggling with the psychological toll of isolation. The central plot element is the recurring appearance of a ghostly ship—known as the Ghost Ship—that Willard initially believes is coming for him as a supernatural phenomenon.
