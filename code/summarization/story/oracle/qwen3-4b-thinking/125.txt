The story follows Isobar Jones, a man stationed at a remote lunar outpost (Lunar III) on the Moon for six months. Struggling with profound homesickness for Earth's natural beauty—he specifically misses grass, flowers, and trees—the protagonist finds solace in playing his bagpipes, despite being prohibited by Dome Commander Eagan due to the noise disrupting the station's operations.
