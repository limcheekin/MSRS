The trip back in the story represents Michael Frey's fundamental realization that the complex, diverse society of Portyork is not a place where he belongs, and his return to the Brotherhood signifies his acceptance of a simpler, more authentic existence. After experiencing the overwhelming array of cultural taboos, social customs, and linguistic complexities of the United Universe's spaceport city, Michael becomes painfully aware that he cannot adapt to this intricate system of universal rules.
