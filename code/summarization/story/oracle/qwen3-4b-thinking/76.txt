The setting of the story is the Moon, specifically the Mare Serenitatis (the Sea of Serenity), a vast lunar plain described as having a smooth layer of pumice that resembles the surface of a calm sea. The narrative follows a group of individuals who live in Lunar City—a lunar base where humans have resided for over a year—as they explore the Moon's surface during what they initially consider a simple walk.
