The story is set against the stark backdrop of space travel during a desperate journey toward Earth, occurring primarily aboard the damaged spacecraft *Stardust*. The narrative begins at Q City Spaceport on Mercury, where Shano boards the ship amid Mercurian night that "settled black and thick." The spaceport glows with infra-red illumination, revealing hangars, freighter catapults, and runways in "diamond-clear detail," while distant portholes of the *Stardust* sink slowly into view.
