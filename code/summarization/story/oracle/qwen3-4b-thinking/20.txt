The story "A Bottle of Old Wine" follows Herbert Hyrel, a man living in a bleak future where desperation to escape reality is widespread. Hyrel resents his wife for her money and the meager allowance he receives, feeling she has treated him as an irresponsible child. He uses a telporter suit to temporarily leave his physical body and enter a hidden world called the Riverside Club, where people can experience freedom from their mundane lives through self-telportation.
