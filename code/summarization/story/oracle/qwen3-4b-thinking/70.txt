The story follows Starrett Blade, known as "Death Star," who is fighting for his life against the notorious space pirate Devil Garrett while entangled with a mysterious girl named Anne Hinton. After being targeted by Garrett's Barden energy beam that crashed his ship into a lake on the barren planet Alpha Centauri III, Blade hides in the water and encounters Anne. She has been secretly working with her father, John Hinton—a manufacturer of advanced equipment—who has been supplying Garrett with critical technology.

Garrett has been posing as Starrett Blade to trick both of them into helping his scheme: he's developing a water electrolysis process that can generate hydrogen and oxygen for power on Alpha Centauri III. The planet has thousands of lakes but no life, and Garrett plans to build a massive power plant using this technology to transform the barren world into habitable territory. He wants to rule the planet after stealing the Hinton patents.
