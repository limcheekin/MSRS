The army's significance in this story is that it represents the necessary but unwelcome force that must intervene when a peaceful society's carefully cultivated tranquility is shattered by an external threat. The colony had been living for centuries without conflict, with deep anti-war conditioning that made them distrust and even despise soldiers—a direct contrast to their sudden dependence on military forces when aliens attack.

Captain Dylan, the lone soldier who arrives with an emergency message, embodies this tension. He's a "dregs of a society" who has spent thirty years waiting for something to happen, now thrust into leadership when the colony is under imminent danger.
