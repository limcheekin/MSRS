The story follows Herbert Quidley, a man who enjoys old books and young women, as he discovers a mysterious code hidden within a library book. While reading Hippolyte Adolphe Taine's History of English Literature, he finds a folded sheet of paper with coded messages that appear to be gibberish but contain hidden text. The messages reference "Cai," "Fieu Dayol," and "snoll doper," which Quidley eventually realizes is a code for interstellar communications.

Quidley becomes obsessed with deciphering the messages and follows a girl named Kay Smith who visits the library regularly to retrieve these coded messages.
