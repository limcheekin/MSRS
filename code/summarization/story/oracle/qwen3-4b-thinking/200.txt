The story follows Professor Venetti, a scientist working on government-funded research to dispose of radioactive waste, who becomes entangled with Tony Carmen, a Mafia-connected gangster. Carmen learns about Venetti's invention—the "Expendable"—a device that seemingly destroys matter by reducing it to zero mass without producing energy or matter (violating conservation laws). Intrigued, Carmen requests Venetti use the device to eliminate bodies being dumped in his club by associates like Harry Keno, who he claims are accidental "victims" of his operations.

Venetti initially creates the device with minimal safety precautions, unaware of its true implications. When he uses it to destroy a body, he realizes the destroyed matter may have been sent to another dimension or future time. Carmen quickly exploits this, using the device to kill people without leaving bodies (claiming there's no "corpus delecti"), and begins commercializing it as "Expendables"
