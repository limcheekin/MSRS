The story follows Kevin Faraday, a psi-deficient individual in a family of gifted telepaths and psychics who struggle with his lack of psychic abilities. While his siblings possess various psychic talents—his sister Sylvia writes advertising copy with telepathic precision, his brother Danny has mental control issues, and their youngest brother Tim is a meteorologist with prognostic abilities—Kevin can do nothing but sit at home, taking care of household machines and reading old books. He feels like an outcast, believing he's fundamentally unsuited for the world where psychic abilities are the norm.

The plot takes a dramatic turn when Kevin learns that Earth is under threat from hostile aliens who have discovered two inhabited planets in the Alpha Centauri system. As war preparations begin, Kevin's mother suggests he learn first-aid techniques, noting his lack of psychic sensitivity might make him better at healing without telepathic interference.
