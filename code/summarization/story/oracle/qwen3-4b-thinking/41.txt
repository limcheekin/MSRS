The setting of POSAT (Perpetual Order of Seekers After Truth) is a complex blend of modern and ancient elements that creates a striking contrast. Physically, it's located in an industrial area near a large warehouse, specifically at the end of a blind alley behind the building. The exterior appears ordinary—concrete construction with windows and loading platforms—but the entrance features the distinctive POSAT emblem. Inside, there's a stark contrast between the shabby reception room with dusty wallpaper and a luxurious waiting area adorned with Renaissance paintings (including Titians) that were supposedly created centuries ago.
