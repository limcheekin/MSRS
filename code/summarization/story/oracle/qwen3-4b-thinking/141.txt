In this family of individuals with various psychic abilities, Tim serves as the crucial emotional anchor for his psi-deficient brother Kevin. As a young meteorologist with exceptional prognosticating skills (the ability to predict future events), Tim stands out among his family members who possess more overt psychic talents. While Danny struggles with mental control, Sylvia is a telepathic copywriter, and their parents have advanced psychic abilities, Tim's unique strength lies in his ability to see potential where others see only deficiency.
