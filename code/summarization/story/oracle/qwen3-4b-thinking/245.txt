The story follows Harry Burr, a man experiencing severe confusion and unsettling memories in a post-apocalyptic world governed by mysterious emergency regulations. Harry gradually realizes his reality is distorted—he can't recall his son Davie, who he believes died, and his surroundings feel increasingly wrong: the barn layout changes, the house appears different, and he struggles to remember basic facts about his farm and family.
