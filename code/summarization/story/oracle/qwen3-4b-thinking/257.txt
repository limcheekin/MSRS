The story unfolds against a backdrop of early space exploration in the 1940s-1950s, when interplanetary travel was still primitive and dangerous. The primary setting is the Martian Maid, a 200-foot chemical-fueled spaceship operating in the outer solar system. Captain Midas describes the era as one where the asteroid belt was "the primary danger" to space travel, and hyperdrives hadn't yet been developed. The narrative takes place primarily in the vast emptiness of space beyond the Earth-Venus-Mars triangle, where the crew discovers a massive derelict ship that stretches a full thousand feet.

The setting is defined by technological limitations: the story mentions "spherical hyperdrives" as future technology, and the ships of this era were armed but lacked advanced capabilities. The crew travels through the "supra-solar void" toward the outer systems, where they find the derelict vessel that's "drifting... slowly spinning end over end as she swung about Sol in a hyperbolic orbit." Later, the action shifts to Callisto, Jupiter's moon, where Captain Midas is eventually confined after his ordeal.
