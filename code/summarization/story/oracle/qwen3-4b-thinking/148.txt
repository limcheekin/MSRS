The robots in this story are highly advanced mechanical beings created by an ancient civilization known as the Makers who have since disappeared. They appear humanoid but with distinctive features: a band of clear plastic around their heads that functions as all-direction vision, and a single round spot for the "eye" on top of their heads. Their bodies are constructed with "fantastic perfection" from metal, making them the most perfect mechanicals Steffens has ever seen.

These robots possess a collective consciousness where each is immediately aware of everything being said or thought by others. They communicate through a single voice (with occasional moments of apparent disappointment), and they have been designed to serve the Makers when they return. One robot
