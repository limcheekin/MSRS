The story follows Captain Midas, whose fateful discovery of a massive derelict spaceship in space leads to devastating consequences. While piloting the Martian Maid, Midas and his crew find a colossal alien vessel that appears to be made of an unknown metal. After testing samples, Midas discovers this metal has transformed into pure gold—revealing it's a "contra-terrene" substance that undergoes transmutation. The crew becomes obsessed with the treasure, but Midas soon realizes his Third Officer, Spinelli, has been secretly sharing the discovery with others.

As the ship is prepared for salvage, Midas discovers Spinelli has planned to seize control of the derelict. In a violent confrontation, Midas kills Spinelli to prevent mutiny. However, the true horror follows: the gold is not merely valuable—it's cursed. The alien metal drains life force from those exposed to it, rapidly aging them.
