In Ray Bradbury's "The Creatures That Time Forgot," Sim and Dark share a deeply protective sibling relationship that forms the foundation of their survival in a world where life is extremely brief. When Sim is born, he immediately recognizes his sister Dark, who is just days old, inside a stone crib. Their bond develops rapidly as they navigate the harsh reality of their existence—each human life lasts only eight days on this planet, with death occurring quickly and inevitably
