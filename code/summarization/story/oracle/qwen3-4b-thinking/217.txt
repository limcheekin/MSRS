The story is set on a remote, barren asteroid where Jon Karyl serves as a lone watchman for the Space Patrol. This desolate celestial body features rugged, rocky terrain with steep slopes that Jon navigates during his escape attempts from the alien invaders. The asteroid's surface contains natural features like ravines lined with dense bushes, rocky outcrops, and sparse sward (grass-like vegetation) that provide both cover and obstacles for the characters.

Jon's base consists of a small service station with an underground entrance and a plastic igloo that serves as his shelter. The setting emphasizes isolation and vulnerability, as the asteroid is completely devoid of life beyond Jon and the Steel-Blues invaders. The environment challenges the lone watchman through its harsh physical conditions and the constant threat from the alien presence.
