The story follows Captain Steffens of the Mapping Command who discovers remnants of an ancient civilization on Tyban IV, a desolate planet in the Coal Sack Nebula. While surveying the planet, he finds only a few stone buildings with no inscriptions, but geological analysis reveals they are at least 15,000 years old—a startling discovery as human exploration of space had only been ongoing for three hundred years. Steffens becomes intrigued when he learns about similar ruins on another planet that show evidence of war, leading him to investigate further.

His ship eventually discovers a dead planet with cities destroyed by massive circular craters, and it's here that he encounters the mysterious robotic beings. These robots, described as "the most perfect mechanicals" he has ever seen, are clearly descendants of the ancient civilization he found on Tyban IV.
