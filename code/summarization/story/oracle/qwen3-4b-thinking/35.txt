The story follows Captain Linden and Split Campbell from the EGGWE (Earth-Galaxy Good Will Expeditions) as they land on an alien planet to study a mysterious river-like entity called the Serpent River. This "rope of life" appears as a massive, silvery serpent-shaped structure crawling across the planet's surface, moving slowly over centuries. The explorers discover humanoid inhabitants who resemble Earth humans—wearing clothing and living in underground cities.
