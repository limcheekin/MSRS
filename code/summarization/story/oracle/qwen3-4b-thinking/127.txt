The Grannies, also known as Granitebacks, are the only mobile life forms discovered by space explorers on the Moon. They pose a significant threat to lunar outpost operations due to their nearly impenetrable exoskeletons, which are described as "harder than steel, diamond, or battleplate." No weapon known to humans—whether steel, flame, electronic waves, ionic radiation, or even the newly developed atomo-needle dispensers—can penetrate their carapace.
