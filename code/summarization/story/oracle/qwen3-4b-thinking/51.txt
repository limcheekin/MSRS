After being marooned on Titan following the crash of the ship *Carefree*, the members aboard the life skiff with Malcolm are: Gregory Malcolm himself, Ralph Breadon (who takes command after Malcolm steps down), Sparks Hannigan (the radioman), J. Foster Andrews (the employer), Enid Andrews (his wife), Maud Andrews (his sister), Crystal Andrews (his daughter), 'Tina Laney (the maid), and Tommy O'Doul (the cabin-boy).
