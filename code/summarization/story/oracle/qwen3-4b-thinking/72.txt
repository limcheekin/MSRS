The story is set on Alpha Centauri III, described as "the most barren planet in space." This desolate world features a flat, rocky surface with no significant natural features beyond scattered lakes. These lakes are small—only about two to three hundred feet across but potentially thousands of feet deep—and serve as the planet's primary water sources. Crucially, Alpha Centauri III has no moon, eliminating tidal power, and lacks running water entirely. The environment is starkly monotonous, with the lakes being the only elements that break the barren monotony.

The protagonist, Starrett Blade ("Death Star"), finds himself trapped on this planet after his ship crashes into one of these lakes following a Barden energy-beam attack from Devil Garrett, the notorious space pirate. The setting plays a critical role in the story's plot, as later discoveries reveal that the lakes are key to a massive power plant system developed by Garrett.
