The story is set on Sekk, a small second moon orbiting beyond Earth's moon Luna. This world, less than 500 miles in diameter, possesses a breathable atmosphere and supports life centered around an oval lake in a sunken star-shaped cavity. The landscape consists of eleven green valleys radiating from this central lake, filled with dense jungle growth and towering trees. 

The setting features two distinct populations: the hairy Vasads who inhabit the jungle valleys, and the golden-skinned people who live in valley cities. The world also contains unique life forms like the Misty Ones—semi-invisible beings who dwell on an island in the lake and are described as having physical characteristics similar to humans but with distinctive features like swollen scars and a tendency to be invisible.
