The theme of global warming is explored through the unintended environmental consequences of Professor Venetti's Expendable device, which destroys matter without creating energy. The Atomic Energy Commission (AEC) agent reveals that while the device appears to violate conservation of energy principles, it actually "adds the energy it created in destroying matter to the energy potential of the planet in the form of heat." This discovery shows how the invention's widespread use would cause planetary heating.
