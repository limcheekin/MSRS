The story follows Captain Dylan, a soldier who arrives at a peaceful space colony that has lived in isolation for decades without conflict. The colony, which had been carefully taught to despise soldiers and war, receives urgent news from Earth that aliens have destroyed another colony (Lupus V) - killing 31 people and leaving the rest missing. Dylan brings a message about this threat and a bomb that was supposed to protect the colony from alien attacks.
