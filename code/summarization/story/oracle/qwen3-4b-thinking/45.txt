The story follows a conformist named "I" living in Northem, a tightly controlled society where citizens have standardized identity designations. The protagonist has an unspeakable four-letter designation that causes social embarrassment and isolation—he's placed on Non-Productive status after attempting to change his name. His life is defined by conformity: he cannot mate due to his designation, is excluded from social activities, and experiences profound loneliness.
