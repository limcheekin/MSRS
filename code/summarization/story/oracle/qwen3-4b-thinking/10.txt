The story follows Terrestrial diplomat Retief as he navigates complex political dynamics on the alien planet Fust. Initially skeptical about the Cultural Attaché's request to sponsor the SCARS (Sexual, Cultural, and Athletic Recreational Society) youth group, Retief discovers that the Groaci Embassy has a hidden agenda involving this very group. 

Retief learns that the Groaci are secretly working with Slock, a leader of the SCARS movement, to install their own weapons systems on Fust's shipyards.
