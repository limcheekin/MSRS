The story follows a Terran Reclamations crew—navigator Farrell, captain Stryker, and linguist/engineer Gibson—as they land on Alphard Six, a planet previously surveyed but never colonized before the Hymenop invasion. Initially cautious due to the Handbook's warning that "any problem posed by one group of human beings can be resolved by any other group," they face immediate danger when their ship is nearly destroyed by an atomic projectile from an unknown source. After recovering, they discover a grounded spaceship on the planet's central city, which appears to be 1,100 years old with primitive atomic-powered technology that defies their understanding of interstellar travel.
