The story's primary setting involves a library on Earth, specifically where Herbert Quidley discovers hidden messages in books. The narrative begins with Quidley in the literature section of a library, where he finds folded sheets of yellow paper containing coded messages hidden within books like Hippolyte Adolphe Taine's History of English Literature. This is where he first encounters Kay Smith, a mysterious girl who appears to be part of an interstellar secret society. The library setting serves as the crucial location where Quidley becomes intrigued by these coded communications and begins his investigation into the messages.
