The story "The Creatures That Time Forgot" by Ray Bradbury depicts a planet where life is brutally short due to extreme environmental conditions. On this harsh world, humans have only eight days to live before dying from radiation and temperature extremes that make survival nearly impossible. The narrative follows Sim, a newborn who quickly learns the brutal reality of his existence—his parents are already aging rapidly, and he understands that the entire human population has been reduced to generations living in caves with just two hours of livable time each day.
