Martin Isherwood is a seventeen-year-old rocket pilot with an intense, singular ambition: to become the first human to pilot a manned rocket to the moon. In the futuristic setting of the story, he is characterized by his exceptional technical skills in navigation and mathematics, his ability to use specialized aviation terminology without reference to standard texts, and his extraordinary focus on achieving his goal.
