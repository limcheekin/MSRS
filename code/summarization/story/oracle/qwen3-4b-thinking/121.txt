The story takes place primarily in two distinct settings that contrast sharply with each other. The first setting is a noisy, crowded restaurant in 1957 where Jeff Elliott struggles with personal financial troubles—he has recently lost his lease on a building he was planning to develop into a television engineering company, and he's feeling desperate and discouraged. While eating, he encounters Mr. Snader, a mysterious man who claims to be from "another time" and offers him a time travel opportunity.
