The story unfolds in a desolate, harsh desert environment on Sirius' second planet, specifically a once-thriving but now abandoned Earth colony settlement. The setting is characterized by extreme heat that "sears, parches" and "draws all moisture out of [Steve's] body," with the sun "gleaming balefully" off the desert landscape. The village consists of only "shells of deserted mud-brick houses" where Steve returns after years away, finding no signs of life except the skeletal remains of his childhood home.

This hostile environment is further defined by the poisoned well that served as the community's sole water source—a critical detail that shows how quickly the colony was abandoned when "the well had been poisoned." The setting includes harsh desert features like "rippled dunes," "bare sun-blasted rock," and a vast wasteland where survival is difficult, with temperatures reaching "hundred-and-thirty-degree" levels.
