The story follows Chip Warren, a spaceman who discovers a deadly space threat known as the "Lorelei" - a siren-like entity that lures spacecraft into destruction. After finding a valuable new element called ekalastron on Uranus' moon Titania, Chip and his friends (Syd Palmer and Salvation Smith) accidentally transmit information about their cargo through etherwaves. Their friend Johnny Haldane warns them that this communication could have been intercepted by the Lorelei, who "begins exercising her vicious appeal" by luring ships to their deaths in the space belt.
