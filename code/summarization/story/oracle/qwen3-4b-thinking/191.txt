The "Prime" technology in this story serves as a critical metaphor for how people attempt to escape emotional problems through technological solutions rather than addressing underlying issues. George Faircloth's quest for an Ego Prime—a duplicate android that mimics his thoughts and behaviors—reflects his frustration with his marriage to Marge and his desire for an alternative to his wife. 

The significance lies in how the Prime ultimately reveals the true nature of their relationship. Initially, George believes the Prime will solve his marital problems by allowing him to be "away" while maintaining a facade of normalcy with Marge. However, the story shows that the Prime becomes a vehicle for Marge's hidden understanding: she had been secretly using her knowledge of the Prime to help George realize what he was missing in their marriage.
