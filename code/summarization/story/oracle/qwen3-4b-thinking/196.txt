The story is set on a distant planet named Big Muddy, a world characterized by persistent wet and humid conditions. Kaiser, the protagonist, finds himself stranded in a crashed scout ship after being dropped there by the mother ship Soscites II during its planet-mapping tour. The environment features constant rain or drizzle that thuds against the transparent walls of the scout ship, creating a damp atmosphere that permeates the setting.

The planet contains river systems where Kaiser encounters seal-people who live in round domes constructed from mud, river weed, and sand. These dwellings are built in haphazard rows along riverbanks, demonstrating a simple but effective architectural style adapted to the environment. The planet experiences radical weather cycles that shift from extreme moisture to extreme aridity during spring and fall seasons, as noted in communications from the mother ship.

Kaiser's primary setting is the scout ship itself, which has become his temporary home while he tries to repair damage caused by a crash. The ship is described as having a slight tilt when it crashed, requiring him to roll onto his stomach to reach the ground.
