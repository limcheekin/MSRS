The story is set on the planet Fust, a unique world inhabited by the long-lived Fustians who resemble turtles but move quickly when they choose. This planet features distinctive physical characteristics for its inhabitants—Fustians carry heavy carapaces (shells) weighing half a ton on their backs, which cause them to move slowly and wheeze under their burden. The setting includes a bustling shipyard where Fustians construct passenger liners like the Moss Rock, as well as diplomatic facilities housing the Terrestrial Embassy where Retief works with Ambassador Magnan.

The political landscape of Fust involves complex youth politics, with organizations like SCARS (Sexual, Cultural, and Athletic Recreational Society) emerging as significant forces.
