The story follows Roddie, a young boy living in a post-apocalyptic city after an invasion, who navigates a world dominated by robotic soldiers ("androids") that guard the ruins. Roddie is physically distinct from his mechanical friends—he wears diapers, experiences human needs like hunger and cold, and has a unique emotional capacity that makes him "non-mechanical." After fixing a damaged soldier with his screwdriver skills, Roddie encounters Ida, an "Invader" girl who has stowed away on a boat to escape the city. They form a tense but growing connection as they attempt to cross the supposedly impassable Golden Gate Bridge, which is broken and inaccessible to humans.
