The Steel-Blues, the mysterious blue beings encountered by Jon Karyl on the asteroid, possess distinct physical characteristics. They are approximately six feet tall and have egg-shaped heads that extend to their walking appendages, which are all steel-blue in color. Unlike humans, they have no legs but instead feature many-jointed tentacles that stretch and shrink independently while maintaining balance with their cylindrical bodies. Each Steel-Blue has four pairs of these flexible tentacles.

Their most notable feature is the elliptical lens covering half of their egg-shaped head, with converging ends curving around the sides. This serves as their visual organ, replacing traditional eyes. Jon Karyl discovers they have eyes on the back of their heads as well, a feature he describes as "quite an innovation."
