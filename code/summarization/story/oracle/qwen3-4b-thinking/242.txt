Rosalind is one of Theodor Wolver's wives in this cosmic science fiction story. She appears as a platinum-haired woman who becomes central to the unfolding planetary catastrophe when she discovers Ivan's briefcase with dirt embedded in it, bearing the message "Going down!" This discovery leads her to investigate the Earth's surface, where she unexpectedly begins sinking into the ground.
