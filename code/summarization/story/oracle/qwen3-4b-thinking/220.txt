The story follows Big Hogey Parker, a space traveler returning to Earth after nine months away from his family. Upon arrival, Hogey struggles with the physical effects of gravity—his "space legs" make it difficult to walk, causing him to stumble and collapse multiple times. He arrives at his childhood home in Great Plains country to find that his wife Marie has remarried (to someone named Hauptman) and has a daughter, not a son as he believed. This revelation creates emotional turmoil as Hogey confronts the reality that his wife has moved on with her life while he returns with the money he'd earned from space work.

Hogey's journey home becomes increasingly difficult as he grapples with his identity shift from a "tumbler" (someone who performed acrobatics in space)
