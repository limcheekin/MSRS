Johnny Haldane is a Space Patrol officer who serves as Chip Warren's former shipmate and friend. He's described as a devoutly religious man with an adventurous spirit who's "not one of those milk-and-water missionaries" but rather a missionary who carries the story of God to outland tribes, armed with both faith and skill in combat. Haldane is characterized as someone who can be both a serious moral guide ("the most pious man in space") and an exceptionally capable gunfighter.
