The story's setting centers on Tyban IV, the fourth planet of the star Tyban, a desolate world with no vegetation or free oxygen and a carbon dioxide atmosphere. Captain Steffens and Lieutenant Ball discover only a handful of weathered stone buildings built from native materials, which they determine are at least 15,000 years old. These structures show remarkable preservation despite the planet's harsh conditions, with no signs of inscriptions or organic life. 

The narrative then shifts to another dead planet where Steffens and his crew find evidence of a civilization destroyed by war—cities reduced to "shattered circles" with massive charred holes in their centers, indicating catastrophic destruction. This world is completely barren, with no living things surviving the radiation that would normally kill organic life.

The most significant setting element is the planet where the robots exist: a dead, ancient world that has been uninhabited for millennia. The robots live on this planet in a radiation environment that would be lethal to humans, yet they thrive without needing oxygen or food. They have no natural predators and are completely immune to the planet's radiation. The setting emphasizes profound desolation
