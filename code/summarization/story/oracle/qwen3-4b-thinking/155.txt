The story "The Lost Tribes of Venus" follows Earthmen participating in the Five Ship Plan, a joint government and private initiative sending five spacecraft to Venus. After a meteorite strike damages one ship (Number Four), Barry Barr—unassigned due to a broken ankle—volunteers to fix the damaged jet system and is sent to Venus. There, he encounters Sigma radiation, a previously unknown cosmic radiation that causes profound biological transformations. Barry develops gills, becoming a water-breather unable to breathe air, while his skin changes with membranous webs between fingers and toes.

The mission faces multiple challenges: Venus's hostile environment—hot, humid swamps teeming with dangerous alien life forms; the psychological strain of isolation; and internal conflict among the crew. Barry's transformation creates a critical divide, as he can no longer interact with Earthmen who remain air-breathers. The story reveals a complex social dynamic, particularly between Barry and Robson Hind, the wealthy jet chief who had previously been interested in Dorothy Voorhees, Barry's companion.
