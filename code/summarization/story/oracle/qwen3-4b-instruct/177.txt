Sim and his sister Dark are siblings in a world where life is extremely short and survival is constant. Born in the same cave on a planet ravaged by extreme weather and radiation, they share a bond formed through shared hardship and mutual survival. Sim, the younger brother, is born into a world where life lasts only eight days—each day a fleeting moment of existence. His sister Dark, though slightly older, grows up under the same oppressive conditions. Despite their different ages, they form a deep, intuitive connection, especially during moments of crisis or emotional intensity. Their relationship is defined by shared observation, survival instinct, and emotional awareness. Dark becomes a caretaker and protector, feeding Sim and guiding him through life’s brutal rhythms. When Sim first speaks, it marks a pivotal moment in their bond—showing that he has developed consciousness and empathy. Dark recognizes his emotional growth and warns him about the inevitability of conflict and enemies in such a short-lived world. Their relationship reflects the harsh reality of a world where familial bonds are forged not through time, but through shared suffering and survival. Sim’s awareness of life’s brevity and Dark’s protective role underscore their inseparable connection in a world that values only fleeting moments.
