Murra Foray is the first counselor at the Travelers Aid Bureau on Godolph, a key figure who provides Cassal with critical information about interstellar travel and the dangers of being stranded. She is significant because she reveals that ships bound for the center of the Galaxy, such as Tunney 21, require passengers to have valid identification tabs—preventing unauthorized travelers from boarding. This rule explains the deadly attack on Cassal: the assailant sought his identification tab, which he had taken during the confrontation. Murra Foray also reveals her hidden background as a Huntner, a sub-race from the far side of the Galaxy, indicating that Travelers Aid Bureau operates under secret protocols and is deeply involved in galactic security. Her presence and the bureau's unusual precautions suggest that interstellar travel is not just logistical but politically and socially controlled, with strict regulations to prevent chaos and unauthorized access. This adds a layer of danger and complexity to Cassal’s journey, emphasizing that the risks of interstellar travel extend beyond physical threats to systemic, bureaucratic dangers. Thus, Murra Foray serves as both a guide and a symbol of the hidden, perilous realities of galactic travel.
