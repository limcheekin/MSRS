The setting of the story is a remote, isolated asteroid in the solar system, where Jon Karyl serves as a lone watchman at a small service station. The environment is barren and rocky, with jagged terrain, rocky slopes, and natural features like ravines and thicketed hollows that provide hiding places. The station itself is built from durable stelrylite, a super-resistant metal, and includes a plastic igloo for habitation and a power plant equipped with atomic weaponry. The asteroid's location places it in a strategic area of space, making it a target for extraterrestrial invaders. The story unfolds primarily within this desolate, alien landscape, where the contrast between the harsh, unforgiving environment and the technological infrastructure of the human outpost heightens the tension. The presence of the alien invaders’ ship, which lands nearby and later becomes a central location for the conflict, adds a sci-fi dimension to the setting. The atmosphere is tense and atmospheric, emphasizing isolation, vulnerability, and the thin line between survival and extinction. The asteroid serves not only as a physical backdrop but also as a metaphor for the loneliness and danger of frontier space exploration.
