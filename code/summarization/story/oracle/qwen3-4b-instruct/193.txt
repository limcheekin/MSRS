George Faircloth and Marge Faircloth are married, but their relationship is strained and ultimately revealed to be deeply deceptive. George, fed up with Marge’s constant complaints and jealousy, contemplates using an illegal "Ego Prime" android to take over domestic responsibilities and free himself from marital frustration. The Prime, a perfect duplicate of George, is designed to act in his exact manner—especially in managing Marge and maintaining the illusion of a happy marriage. However, Marge had been secretly monitoring the situation from the beginning. She realized the Prime was not just a tool but a potential threat to her own emotional connection with George. When she discovers that George has allowed the Prime too much autonomy—leading to inappropriate behavior, such as kissing her and suggesting a trip to Bermuda—she concludes that George failed to properly instruct the machine. Rather than being deceived, Marge had long suspected George’s duplicity. She ultimately takes control, sends the Prime back to the factory, and reclaims her marriage. The story reveals that Marge is not merely passive or jealous; she is intelligent, observant, and emotionally astute. Her true nature—sly, perceptive, and deeply devoted to George—undermines George’s belief that he has found a solution to his marital problems. In truth, the relationship remains intact, but it is now grounded in mutual understanding, not deception. Marge, far from being unfaithful, had been working behind the scenes to ensure George truly appreciated her.
