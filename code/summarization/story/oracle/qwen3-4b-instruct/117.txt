Captain Dylan is a veteran soldier and former outcast who has spent thirty years drifting through space, waiting for a significant event. He is a captain by title, having been promoted after years of service, though he feels disconnected from the military’s purpose and societal expectations. When he arrives at a remote colony, he brings critical intelligence about an alien threat that has already attacked another colony, destroying it and cutting the bomb's detonation wire. Dylan realizes the aliens are highly intelligent and have deliberately cut the bomb wire to prevent the colony from being destroyed. Despite initial resistance and resentment from the colonists—who view soldiers as unnecessary and outdated—Dylan insists on immediate action to evacuate them. However, when contact with the sister colony at Planet Three fails, Dylan learns that Lieutenant Bossio, who was supposed to meet him, has also been killed. This devastating loss underscores the danger of the alien threat and the futility of peace-based society in the face of unknown, advanced intelligence. Ultimately, Dylan stays behind to ensure the colony’s survival, sacrificing himself to protect those he never fully knew, demonstrating that true strength lies not in peace, but in readiness and courage in the face of the unknown.
