In the story, ekalastron or No. 97 is a highly valuable and dangerous element discovered on the satellite Titania, far from Uranus. It is described as a revolutionary metal—light enough to coat a battleship with ease yet strong enough to deflect meteors and rebound like rubber. Its unique properties make it immensely valuable for space travel, especially in shielding spacecraft. However, its value also makes it a prime target for pirates and outlaws. The Lorelei, a siren-like pirate entity who lures spacemen to their doom, exploits this by targeting those who possess or advertise ekalastron. The story reveals that the element's discovery and public announcement via visiphone create a dangerous vulnerability, as it can be intercepted by the Lorelei, who then uses it to lure victims into a deadly trap. Ultimately, ekalastron serves as both a technological marvel and a catalyst for conflict, highlighting the dangers of unchecked scientific advancement and the peril of exposing powerful resources in the vastness of space.
