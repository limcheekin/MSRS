Captain Linden is the protagonist and lead explorer of the narrative, sent by the EGGWE (Earth-Galaxy Good Will Expeditions) to investigate a mysterious planet. He and his companion, Split Campbell, land on the planet’s summit to study a colossal, serpentine structure known as the "Serpent River" or "Kao-Wagwattl," a vast, slowly moving cylindrical rope-like formation that crawls across the surface. Initially guided by strict EGGWE protocols forbidding contact with native life, Captain Linden defies protocol when he recognizes the humanoid inhabitants—particularly the tall, dignified leader Tomboldo—who appear remarkably similar to humans. Despite Split’s caution and adherence to rigid scientific discipline, Captain Linden believes in peaceful engagement and decides to approach the people. Their encounter takes a dramatic turn when a hidden attack by armed warriors, concealed within moving "sponge-trees," threatens the native population. Captain Linden and Split use their ship’s siren and weapons to repel the attackers, though the mission ends with a serious injury to their guard, Gravgak, who is fatally wounded by a stone club. After recovering from his injuries, Captain Linden forms a close bond with Vauna, the daughter of Tomboldo, and learns about the planet's culture and reverence for the Serpent River. The narrative culminates in a decision by the native council to take Captain Linden and Campbell as guests on their journey along the Serpent River, symbolizing a fragile but hopeful alliance between Earth explorers and alien life. Throughout the story, Captain Linden evolves from a disciplined scientist to a compassionate observer who recognizes the humanity and vulnerability of the planet’s inhabitants.
