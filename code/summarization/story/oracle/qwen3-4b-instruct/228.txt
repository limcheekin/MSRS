The alien flies in the story are winged, insect-like beings from another dimension or universe that have invaded Earth and taken control of human minds and society. They appear as large, insectoid creatures with wings, capable of mimicking human forms through mimicry, allowing them to blend into human populations and assume human appearances. These entities enter through a "crack" in reality, such as the dark patch above the City Hall, and descend to infiltrate towns, starting from positions of authority like the police and city government. Their physical characteristics include a buzzing, humming sound, and they are described as having multi-lensed, cold, inhuman eyes. They are not merely insects but intelligent, invasive lifeforms that use psychological control to dominate society. The body hanging from the lamppost is not a real corpse but a deliberate trap—“bait”—designed to lure someone like Ed Loyce into exposure, revealing who has escaped their control and who remains under their influence. Thus, the alien flies are both physical invaders and psychological manipulators, symbolizing a larger existential threat to human autonomy and identity.
