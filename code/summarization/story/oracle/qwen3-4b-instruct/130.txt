The story follows Jimmie Stanley and his four-piece band, The Hottest Music This Side of Mercury, as they struggle to keep their gig at The Space Room on Mars. Their performance is threatened when their bassist, Ziggy, is injured, and their manager, Ke-teeli, refuses to hire a replacement, insisting only a "good" musician can perform. Just as the band faces financial and existential crisis, a mysterious, disheveled man named John Smith arrives with an ancient, otherworldly bass called a Zloomph. His music is extraordinary—rich, deep, and transcendent—transforming both the audience and the band’s morale. John’s music so moves Ke-teeli that he begins to genuinely enjoy the show, signaling a shift in their relationship. However, John is deeply obsessed with "holes" in the universe, believing they are portals through time and dimensions. He claims he fell through one while returning from a research mission and must find the right hole to return home. After a disastrous interview with a reporter, who mocks him, John becomes terrified and vanishes into a manhole, disappearing completely. The story ends with the band devastated, having lost not only their best musician but also the chance to understand or save him, leaving behind a haunting sense of loss and mystery about the nature of time, music, and human connection.
