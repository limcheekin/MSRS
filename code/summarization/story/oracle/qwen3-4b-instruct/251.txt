Kay Smith is a central figure in a mysterious secret society operating across interstellar space, specifically on the planet Fieu Dayol. She is a "ship's stock girl" and serves as a key intermediary in the society’s communication and matchmaking system. Her most notable characteristics include her striking physical appearance—tall, with hyacinth hair and liquid blue eyes—giving her a Helenesque beauty that draws attention and admiration. She speaks with a distinct accent, hinting at her non-terrestrial origins. Kay is intelligent, observant, and resourceful, using coded messages hidden in books like Taine’s *History of English Literature* to communicate with other members of the society. The messages, disguised as typewriter exercises, are actually requisitions for potential mates, with "snoll dopers" being individuals selected to marry due to their refusal to conform to their home planet’s sexual norms. Kay is both composed and enigmatic, maintaining control over the interactions while revealing only gradually that her society operates under strict interstellar laws forbidding public appearances and local communication. Her role as a liaison between the women of Fieu Dayol and potential male partners from other worlds makes her the pivotal figure in the story’s secret, elegant, and socially structured universe.
