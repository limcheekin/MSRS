Willie Dawes is a central character in the story set in the small, mysterious town where residents dream of a strange place called Armagon. He is a tall, rangy man with a lined face and a bobbing Adam's apple, who serves as a local authority figure and the primary guide to the town’s peculiar customs. Dawes is deeply involved in the town’s nightly rituals, particularly the dream world of Armagon, which he claims he has dreamed about since childhood. Though he appears practical and grounded during daytime interactions, he reveals that Armagon is a shared dream realm where the townspeople live out fantastical events, including an "execution" that occurs in their dreams. Dawes acts as a guardian of this secret, often dismissing outsiders' questions about the dream world as irrelevant or intrusive. He is skeptical of Sol Becker’s curiosity and warns him not to write about the town or its happenings, fearing it might be seen as absurd. Despite his gruff demeanor, Dawes holds a quiet sense of responsibility toward the community’s private dream life, and he eventually reveals that he is actually "King Dawes" in the dream world, indicating his deep integration into the supernatural realm. His character embodies the tension between reality and fantasy, making him both a skeptic and a participant in the town’s strange, dream-based existence.
