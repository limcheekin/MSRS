Gurn is the golden-skinned leader of the Vasad people and a central figure in the story. He serves as a guide, protector, and ally to Noork, the man with a forgotten past. Gurn is revealed to be an exile from the walled city of Grath, where he was labeled a traitor for opposing slavery. His leadership among the jungle inhabitants provides stability and wisdom, especially in their conflict with the oppressive Misty Ones of the Temple of Uzdon. Gurn plays a pivotal role in the narrative by delivering crucial information—such as the truth about the Misty Ones being human and vulnerable—through his loyal Vasad followers. Most importantly, Gurn is the one who ultimately reveals Noork’s true identity as Captain Stephen Dietrich, linking him to Earth’s past and resolving the mystery of Noork’s forgotten origin. His presence ensures that the struggle against oppression culminates in a successful rescue and a new beginning for Noork in the jungle world.
