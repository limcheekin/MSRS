Many Earthmen are desolate because they believe the arrival of the power cylinder will bring revival, only to discover that their hopes are manipulated by sinister forces. In the story, the promised salvation of Earth's industries is revealed to be a deception orchestrated by corrupt leaders who have sold Earth’s future to Mars for profit. Mury exposes the truth: the power cylinder is not a gift of renewal, but a tool of exploitation, turning Earth into a labor pool for Martian interests. Earthmen, like Ryd Randl, are caught in a cycle of hope and disillusionment, believing in progress while being systematically undermined by political and economic betrayal. Their desolation stems from the realization that the very forces they rely on—government, industry, and promised recovery—are part of a larger conspiracy. This makes their future uncertain and their hopes futile, as the "revival" is not genuine but a facade masking a deeper, more destructive exploitation. Thus, Earthmen are desolate not because of lack of opportunity, but because their dreams are undermined by the sinister, profit-driven realities behind the surface promises of renewal.
