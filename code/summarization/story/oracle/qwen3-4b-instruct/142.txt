Kevin's unique healing ability remained undiscovered until age twenty-six because, in a world where psychic powers were common, his lack of telepathic or telekinetic abilities made him appear as a mere "psi-deficient" or atavism. His family and society dismissed him as fundamentally incapable, focusing on his absence of known psi faculties rather than potential hidden talents. The story emphasizes that psi powers were widely tested, and Kevin was repeatedly deemed negative in all standard evaluations. Additionally, he lacked the mental shield common among psi-users, which made him vulnerable to emotional exposure and further isolated him from being taken seriously. His true power—healing—was not recognized because it did not fit into the established categories of psychic ability. Only when faced with the trauma of war and the urgent need to treat injuries did the latent healing ability manifest. This power was not detectable through conventional psi testing, and its nature as a physical, life-affirming gift rather than a mental or telekinetic one meant it was overlooked. Thus, the circumstances—social norms, lack of testing for non-traditional abilities, and societal dismissal of those without known powers—led to Kevin’s unique talent remaining hidden until he was twenty-six.
