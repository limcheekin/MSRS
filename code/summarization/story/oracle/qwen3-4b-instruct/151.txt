The setting of the story is an ancient, forgotten city on Alpha Centaurus II, half a million years old, buried beneath sand and time. It is a surreal, otherworldly landscape of crumbling yet remarkably preserved architecture—small homes, towering spires, spheroid and ellipsoidal buildings, and fragile bridges that still sway in the wind. The city is both beautiful and eerie, with ebony surfaces catching the late sunlight and desert plants growing from rooftops. Despite its physical decay, the setting exudes a sense of spiritual presence, as if the ancient civilization’s consciousness lingers. The ruins are not just relics; they are alive with unseen forces—spirits, voices, and thoughts that stir when touched or approached. The environment is still, yet deeply sentient, with the wind sighing through spires and the city seeming to "breathe" with the passage of time. This blend of desolation and mysticism creates a haunting atmosphere where the boundary between past and present, reality and spirit, is blurred. The city is not merely a backdrop—it is a living, breathing entity that resists intrusion, guarding its secrets and ancient wisdom from those who seek to possess or preserve it.
