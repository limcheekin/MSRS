A flitterboat is a small, personal transportation vehicle used in the Belt for short-distance travel between beacon stations. It features a single gravitoinertial engine and carries only essential supplies like air, water, and minimal food. Due to the low gravity environments of asteroids and the impracticality of using full-sized spaceships, flitterboats are vital for individual mobility. They are not designed for long journeys because a person in a vacuum suit cannot remain comfortable for extended periods, and travel requires frequent stops at beacon stations for acceleration and deceleration. In the story, Daniel Oak uses his flitterboat to travel from Raven's Rest to Ceres, the largest asteroid in the Belt, which serves as a major operations base. The flitterboat is central to the narrative as it enables Oak to reach the Viking Spacecraft facility where he must confront the robot ship McGuire and address the issues of robotic autonomy and control. The journey underscores the isolation and logistical challenges of space travel in a futuristic setting, highlighting the reliance on small, efficient vehicles for personal and operational movement.
