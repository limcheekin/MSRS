The setting of the story is a distant, desolate region of space beyond the Earth-Mars-Venus Triangle, specifically in the outer solar system and near the edge of the asteroid belt, where deep space and interstellar voids dominate. The narrative unfolds primarily aboard the Martian Maid, an early chemical-fueled spaceship that operates in the high, hazardous orbits of space, traveling through the supra-solar void. The central setting is the discovery of a massive, alien derelict ship—origin unknown—drifting in a hyperbolic orbit, seemingly from beyond the stars. This starship, described as a thousand feet long and made of a mysterious, unstable metal, floats in the silent darkness of space, illuminated only by distant stars and its own eerie, pulsing glow. The story contrasts the cold vacuum of space with the intimate, human environment of the ship's bridge and crew quarters, where greed, fear, and the slow transformation of the crew into aged, lifeless beings take place. The setting thus blends the vastness and horror of deep space with the psychological decay of human ambition, emphasizing the isolation, danger, and moral cost of seeking treasure in the cosmos.
