Ryd is a former helio-operator and skilled technician who has been unemployed for over a decade, marked by a life of marginalization and rejection from the power structures that once employed him. He is physically and emotionally worn down, embodying the disillusionment of a man who once contributed to Earth's technological advancement but was discarded when the industry shifted. His character is defined by quiet desperation, a deep sense of injustice, and a fragile hope for redemption. When he is drawn into a dangerous mission by Mury, a radical member of the clandestine group "We," Ryd is initially skeptical and fearful, but gradually becomes entangled in a conspiracy that threatens Earth's future. He is not a heroic figure by nature, but his decision to join Mury—driven by desperation and a sense of personal betrayal—reveals a surprising capacity for moral awakening. Ryd's journey from passive victim to active participant in a rebellion against Martian economic domination underscores the story's theme of individual agency in the face of systemic oppression. His entanglement in the power struggle highlights how personal suffering can be transformed into political resistance, making him a symbol of the overlooked masses caught in the web of global power dynamics.
