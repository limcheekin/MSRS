Mom is the central figure in the story, representing the everyday yet mysterious anchor of the peculiar town where residents dream of a place called Armagon. She is described as plump, apple-cheeked, and white-haired, wearing an old-fashioned nightgown, giving her a harmless, cartoonish appearance reminiscent of political figures. Her characteristics include warmth, practicality, and a strong sense of routine—she hosts strangers, manages household duties, and maintains a strict schedule, such as waking early for breakfast at seven. Despite her seemingly ordinary demeanor, Mom is deeply connected to the town’s surreal reality: she dreams of Armagon nightly, and her family members, including her husband (referred to as "Pa" or "Dawes") and daughter Sally, share the same dream world. Her actions—like rushing to the palace or calling about an execution—reveal that Armagon is a shared, subconscious realm. Mom embodies both domesticity and surrealism, serving as a bridge between the mundane and the fantastical, illustrating how the town's unusual happenings are woven into daily life through collective dreaming.
