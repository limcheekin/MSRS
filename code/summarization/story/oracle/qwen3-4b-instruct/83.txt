The equipment utilized throughout the narrative involving the frail protagonist Shano on his perilous journey through space includes polarized goggles, a lighter disk, and a series of mechanical and electronic devices aboard the ship. Shano uses his polarized goggles to navigate the spaceport and monitor the environment during takeoff. The lighter disk is essential for him to light a cigarette, a recurring act that symbolizes both his personal endurance and awareness of the dangers around him. Inside the ship, critical machinery such as the cosmic drive’s selector valves, fuel tubes, and valve rods are central to the plot. These components are operated manually by Shano when he enters the engine room, using his gum-clogged lungs and physical strength to lift and reposition the broken tungsten steel rods, thereby restoring the ship’s functionality. The ship’s alarm system, loud-speakers, and emergency lighting also play a key role in signaling danger and maintaining operational awareness. Ultimately, the story highlights how Shano’s survival hinges not on advanced technology, but on his ability to interact with and repair these mechanical systems, using his unique physiology and experience from labor in Pluto's mines.
