The plot revolves around a group of characters on a distant, hostile planet—Mars—who face life-threatening circumstances while attempting to escape in a small, unreliable surface rocket. The central conflict arises when a sick nurse, Judith, and another injured man, Gladney, must be evacuated to Earth for medical treatment, as no hospitals exist on the planet. The only means of escape is a tiny, unmodified ship designed for planetary landings, which is piloted by a mysterious Centaurian named Rat. Despite being dismissed as unqualified and marked by a shady past, Rat emerges as the sole capable pilot, driven by a desperate need to save lives. The crew faces numerous dangers: mechanical failures, extreme conditions, limited supplies, and the psychological toll of prolonged isolation. Rat's decision to take control of the ship—despite risking his own job and being labeled a criminal—demonstrates his moral resolve. As they journey through space, the characters endure physical and mental deterioration, including dehydration, illness, and growing fear. Ultimately, the story highlights themes of survival, human resilience, and the cost of redemption, culminating in a tense escape that hinges on trust, courage, and the fragile hope of reaching Earth.
