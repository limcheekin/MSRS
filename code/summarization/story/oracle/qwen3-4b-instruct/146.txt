The setting of the story is an ancient, desolate planet in the Coal Sack Nebula, specifically Tyban IV, a dead fourth planet orbiting a star called Tyban. The environment is barren and hostile—thin atmosphere, extreme winds, and a lack of vegetation or life. The landscape features isolated stone structures built from native materials, suggesting a long-dead civilization that predates human space exploration. The planet's age is estimated to be thousands, possibly tens of thousands of years, as determined by geological evidence such as wind erosion patterns. The environment is further characterized by its isolation, with no signs of life or activity on the surface. In contrast, the story later reveals a second planet with a dead, war-ravaged civilization, where ruins are strewn with charred cities and massive craters, indicating a catastrophic event. The setting combines alien desolation with deep historical implications, emphasizing the vast age of the civilizations that once inhabited these worlds. The barren, radiation-prone environment underscores the planet's inhumanity, while the remnants of architecture and the presence of advanced, silent robots suggest a forgotten, highly sophisticated society that endured for millennia before vanishing—leaving behind only traces of its existence and a legacy of mystery.
