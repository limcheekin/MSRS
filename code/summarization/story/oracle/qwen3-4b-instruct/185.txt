The story revolves around Sol Becker, a young man who is stranded in a strange, isolated town where everyone dreams of a mysterious place called Armagon. Initially believing he has merely stumbled into a bizarre local setting, Sol quickly realizes that the town's residents are deeply immersed in a shared dream world. The townspeople—such as Mom, Dawes, Charlie, and Sally—regularly speak of Armagon as if it were a real place, describing its palace, rituals, and events like an execution. Sol learns through conversations and observations that Armagon is a recurring dream realm where the inhabitants live out fantastical, often unsettling scenarios. When he questions the nature of the place, the townspeople become defensive, revealing that Armagon is a private, sacred dream world, not a physical location. The story takes a surreal turn when Sol, while asleep, is transported into Armagon itself, where he is confronted by the Knights and witnesses an execution, blurring the line between reality and dream. The plot ultimately explores themes of shared delusion, the power of imagination, and the isolation of a community living within a collective fantasy, suggesting that the town is not just strange—it is entirely constructed from dreams.
