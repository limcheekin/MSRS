The setting of the story is the Moon's Mare Serenitatis, a vast and serene expanse of smooth, pumice-like lunar dust covering approximately 340,000 square miles. The narrative begins with a group of individuals from Lunar City taking a walk on the Moon, where they stumble upon a massive, alien structure rising a quarter of a mile above the surface. This structure, described as a smooth, curving hulk of metal, is hidden and unremarked upon by previous lunar explorers due to its size and the Moon's vastness. The group investigates the structure, discovers a large, enclosed chamber with strange drawings and instruments, and eventually becomes trapped inside. The setting shifts from the open, tranquil lunar surface to the confined, alien interior of the ship, which is located on the Moon’s surface and functions as a booby-trap designed to capture early space-faring humans. The story unfolds entirely within this extraterrestrial environment, emphasizing the isolation and technological mystery of the alien ship, ultimately placing the characters in a situation of suspense, discovery, and psychological tension.
