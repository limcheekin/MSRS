John Smith is a peculiar and enigmatic character in the story, described as a ragged, matchstick-thin man with a flat, broad face and dry, grass-like hair. He wears a tight-fitting, black suit that seems out of place, and his pale blue, watery eyes suggest a deep, otherworldly awareness. He plays a bizarre, ancient instrument called a Zloomph—a massive, midnight-black bass fiddle resembling an hourglass—believed to be over five thousand years old. John is deeply obsessed with "holes" in the universe, which he claims are portals to different time dimensions and realities. His peculiar obsession stems from a traumatic fall through a hole while attempting to prove a theory about ancient music predating recorded tapes. He believes that if he doesn’t return to his own time dimension, he will be discredited and shamed. Though initially reluctant to join the band, John’s extraordinary music—rich, ecstatic, and otherworldly—transforms the atmosphere of The Space Room, turning customers into ecstatic listeners and even altering Goon-Face’s gruff demeanor into one of delight. Despite his quiet nature, his music is described as coming from "heaven," with a depth and richness unmatched. Ultimately, John vanishes after a reporter interrupts his conversation, revealing he fears his own body has become filled with holes, threatening his ability to return to his original dimension. His disappearance marks a tragic loss, leaving the band devastated and questioning the very nature of time, dimensions, and human connection.
