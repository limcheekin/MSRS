The plot revolves around Starrett Blade, a renowned fighter known as "Death Star," who is trapped and fighting for his life after his ship crashes into a lake on the barren planet Alpha Centauri III. He is pursued by the notorious pirate Devil Garrett, who has been hunting Blade for years. Trapped and weaponless, Blade finds himself in a dangerous situation when he encounters a mysterious girl named Anne Hinton, who has her own motives and knowledge of Garrett's true identity. Initially, she believes Blade is Garrett, and she is manipulated into thinking that the two are one and the same. However, through a series of revelations, including intercepted communications and evidence of Garrett’s secret power plant, it becomes clear that Garrett has been faking his identity to steal the Hinton company’s advanced electrolysis technology. This technology could power vast energy systems on the planet, transforming its barren landscape into a habitable world. In a climactic confrontation, Blade uses a hidden jet weapon to defeat Garrett, while Anne plays a crucial role by exposing the deception and helping them escape. Ultimately, Blade reveals that the power plant’s plans—though originally meant for exploitation—can be used to bring life to the planet's desolate regions. The story ends with Blade and Anne working together to ensure the planet is saved, and they humorously suggest renaming it "Garrett" as a warning against greed and corruption.
