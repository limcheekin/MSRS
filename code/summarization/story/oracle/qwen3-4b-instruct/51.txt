The members aboard the life skiff with Malcolm after being marooned on a distant world are: Gregory Malcolm (the secretary and initial leader), Hannigan (the radioman), Ralph Breadon (the pilot and eventual commander), J. Foster Andrews (the employer and head of the group), Enid Andrews (his wife), Maud Andrews (his sister), Crystal Andrews (his daughter), Bert Andrews (his son), 'Tina Laney (the maid), and Tommy O'Doul (the cabin boy). They are stranded on Titan, a moon of Saturn, after the spaceship *Carefree* is destroyed in a space-vortex and gravitational drag. The group survives by taking refuge in the life-skiff, which becomes their only vessel and shelter. Though initially disorganized and fearful, they gradually come together to assess their situation, establish a base, and plan for survival in the harsh environment of Titan.
