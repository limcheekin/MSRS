Daniel Oak’s job in the context of his involvement with the complex project involving advanced robotics and interstellar travel is that of a "Confidential Expediter." He is not a scientist, engineer, or pilot, but rather a problem-solver hired to help get things done—specifically, to navigate and resolve difficult situations involving technology, politics, and human interaction. In the story, Oak is tasked with going to Ceres to assist with the McGuire robot spaceship project, where he serves as a key intermediary between the robotocists at Viking Spacecraft and the political and security dynamics of the Belt. His role hinges on his ability to act as a neutral, efficient agent who can navigate bureaucratic, technical, and interpersonal challenges—such as the ethical dilemmas surrounding robotic autonomy and the human-robot relationship—without being tied to any one organization’s interests. Despite being hired by Shalimar Ravenhurst, Oak operates independently, using discretion, insight, and moral judgment to ensure the project's success while maintaining his integrity and avoiding conflict of interest. His expertise lies not in building robots, but in managing the human elements that make such advanced technology viable and safe.
