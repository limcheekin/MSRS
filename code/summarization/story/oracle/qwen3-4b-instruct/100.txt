The story follows Eric North, a man from Earth who stumbles upon a mysterious, enchanting city hidden beneath the Martian canals. As he approaches, the city's illusions grow powerful, drawing him in with music and visions of beauty, jewels, and exotic life. However, the city is a sentient machine that translates thoughts into reality, built by ancient Martians to preserve their civilization during a time of environmental collapse. The city’s people are superstitious and deeply attached to its illusions, believing that outsiders—especially those with certain physical traits—will destroy it. Eric, whose sun-tanned skin and copper-colored hat trigger the city’s recognition of him as "Eric the Bronze," is identified by the elders as the prophesied figure destined to dismantle the machine. Though initially terrified and confused, Eric learns that the city's power corrupts individuals who become consumed by their desires, leading to mindless, destructive behavior. The elders believe that only a man like Eric—the embodiment of a noble, ancient name—can trigger its self-destructive mechanism when contact with an outsider occurs. Ultimately, Eric is shown to be both a victim of the city's illusions and a key figure in its fate, as he must either fulfill the prophecy and destroy the city or remain safe within its protective structure, forever marked by the truth of his identity and purpose.
