The setting of *Raiders of the Second Moon* is the mysterious, jungle-covered moon Sekk, a small satellite orbiting beyond Luna that possesses a breathable atmosphere and life. Sekk is described as a world with a unique ecosystem centered around a sunken, star-shaped lake, surrounded by eleven radiating valleys filled with dense jungle growth. The environment is both lush and dangerous, featuring towering jungle giants, hidden pitfalls, and deadly creatures like the spotted narl. The story unfolds primarily in one of these green valleys, where the indigenous Vasads—hairy, forest-dwelling people—coexist with golden-skinned leaders like Gurn, who leads a resistance against the oppressive slave system of the valley cities. A central element of the setting is the enigmatic Lake of Uzdon, home to the "Misty Ones," invisible, humanoid beings believed to be demons or gods, who are actually revealed to be ordinary people living under a false belief. The jungle world is both a physical and symbolic backdrop, representing isolation, danger, and the struggle between freedom and oppression. The setting blends exotic fantasy with elements of survival, mystery, and cultural conflict, all anchored in the isolated, otherworldly terrain of Sekk.
