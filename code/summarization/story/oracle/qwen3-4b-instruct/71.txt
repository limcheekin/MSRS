Garrett is the notorious space pirate known as "Devil Garrett," who is later revealed to be a mastermind deceiving both Starrett Blade and the girl, Anne Hinton. Initially, Garrett appears as the enemy, having targeted Blade after a Barden energy-beam attack that crashes Blade’s ship into a lake on the desolate planet Alpha Centauri III. However, it is uncovered that Garrett has been faking his identity, posing as Starrett Blade to gain access to the Hinton family’s secret power processes—specifically, a method of electrolyzing water to produce hydrogen and oxygen for energy. He plans to build a vast power plant across the planet using the lakes’ water, enabling him to rule Alpha Centauri III and transform its barren landscape into a thriving world. Despite being captured and imprisoned, Garrett is ultimately defeated by Starrett Blade, who uses a hidden jet-blade weapon to kill him in a brief, decisive confrontation. The story reveals that Garrett’s true intentions were not merely criminal but also technological and colonial—aimed at exploiting the planet’s resources for personal control. In the end, Blade exposes Garrett’s deception, destroys his power plant plans, and proposes renaming the planet "Garrett" as a warning. Though defeated, Garrett’s legacy lives on in the story as a symbol of danger and the misuse of power.
