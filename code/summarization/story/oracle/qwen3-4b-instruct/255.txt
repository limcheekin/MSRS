The plot revolves around Captain Midas, the skipper of the Martian Maid, who discovers a massive, alien derelict ship drifting in space, seemingly abandoned and originating from beyond the stars. Believing it to be a treasure trove, the crew—driven by greed—seeks to claim it as salvage under Foundation regulations. Upon investigating, Captain Midas realizes the ship is made of a mysterious, contra-terrestrial metal that, when exposed to Earth conditions, undergoes transmutation into gold. This discovery fuels intense greed among the crew, especially Spinelli and Zaleski, who plot to seize the ship for themselves. As they prepare to bring it back to Callisto, strange events unfold: communications from the derelict grow garbled, and its systems begin malfunctioning. The crew members start exhibiting signs of aging and decay, revealing that the alien metal is not inert—it draws energy from living beings, slowly draining their youth and vitality. Ultimately, Captain Midas discovers that the ship has consumed his crew, turning them into aged, lifeless figures who now dwell within its walls. In a horrifying realization, he understands that the gold was never a prize—it was a curse. The story ends with Midas, himself visibly aged and broken, exiled from further service, forever haunted by the consequences of greed and the unnatural power of alien matter. The central theme is that the pursuit of treasure leads not to wealth, but to degradation, death, and spiritual ruin.
