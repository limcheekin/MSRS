The plot of *Saboteur of Space* centers on Ryd Randl, a former helio-operator who has been out of work for over a decade, becoming entangled in a dangerous conspiracy to sabotage the Martian power cylinder mission. The story unfolds when Ryd is approached by a mysterious man named Mury, who claims to be working against a corrupt system that has sold Earth's future to Mars for profit. Mury reveals that the so-called "power cylinder" meant to revive Earth’s industries is actually a scheme to enslave its population, turning Earth into a labor pool for Martian interests. To expose this deception, Mury recruits Ryd to participate in a daring mission: they must steal the towship *Shahrazad*, which is scheduled to carry the power shell into orbit. After a tense infiltration of the spaceport, they successfully board the ship and escape with the crew, including the young astrogator Yet Arliess, who ultimately joins their cause. As the ship accelerates into space, Mury reveals that the Martian warship *Alborak* is actively pursuing them, forcing a high-stakes journey through deep space. The narrative builds tension through Ryd’s growing fear and realization that he has been manipulated into becoming part of a secret, panclastic force—*We*—driven by revolutionary rebellion against colonial exploitation. Ultimately, the story is a warning about economic betrayal and the dangers of blind obedience to authoritarian regimes, with Ryd’s journey from disillusioned worker to reluctant revolutionary symbolizing resistance against planetary domination.
