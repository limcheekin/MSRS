Dimanche is an electronic instrument implanted in Denton Cassal’s body that functions as a personal security and analytical device. It monitors and interprets physiological data, such as heart rate, blood pressure, and neural activity, to assess threats and provide real-time warnings. In the narrative, Dimanche plays a crucial role in detecting the danger posed by a mysterious assailant who follows Cassal on Godolph, a planet with a dangerous urban environment for humans. Dimanche identifies the man’s intent—murder—and analyzes his nervous system responses, revealing that the threat is connected to Cassal’s inability to leave the planet due to a delayed ship. While Dimanche cannot read minds or access hidden information, it provides vital insights into the attacker’s behavior and motivation, such as detecting the man’s whispered justification: “Poor devil. I hate to do it. But it’s really his life or mine.” This suggests the assassin is driven by desperation, possibly tied to Cassal’s possession of a unique identification tab. Ultimately, Dimanche serves as Cassal’s only reliable ally in a hostile environment, enabling him to survive attacks and uncover the truth behind the threat—revealing that the attacker sought Cassal’s identification, which he had previously lost, and that the entire situation is rooted in galactic transportation rules that require identification for travel into the center of the Galaxy. Thus, Dimanche functions as both a survival tool and a key to understanding the narrative’s central conflict.
