The story is set on a remote, peaceful colony in a distant part of space, located in a quiet, isolated region of a planetary system far from Earth. The setting is described as serene and idyllic—characterized by cold, snowy weather, dense forests, and a sense of tranquility that has persisted for decades due to the colonists’ deep-rooted anti-war ideology and pacifist values. The colony is a self-sustaining frontier settlement, built into the natural landscape with plastic homes, automated farming systems, and a strong sense of community life. The environment is both beautiful and vulnerable: it is surrounded by vast, untouched wilderness and is exposed to the elements, making it physically isolated and susceptible to sudden threats. The atmosphere is one of quiet routine, broken only by the arrival of an unexpected alien force. This contrast between the peaceful, domestic setting and the imminent, terrifying threat from intelligent extraterrestrial life underscores the story’s central tension—how a society that has long rejected war and soldiers is now forced to confront the reality of survival in the face of a hostile, intelligent alien race. The setting emphasizes isolation, vulnerability, and the fragility of peace in a universe where danger can emerge without warning.
