The plot revolves around archaeologist Michaelson, who discovers a mysterious ancient city on Alpha Centaurus II. He is initially amused by the site's beauty and begins exploring it, collecting artifacts and marveling at its preservation. He encounters Maota, a native who claims to be the "keeper" of the city and insists that no one should enter or disturb it, warning that alien presence could awaken ancient spirits. Michaelson mocks the idea, but as their interactions deepen, he becomes intrigued by the city’s advanced technology and spiritual significance. A pivotal moment occurs when Michaelson finds a talking book that speaks in poetic language, revealing the inhabitants’ deep connection to thought and culture. When Maota threatens to kill him, Michaelson fights back, leading to a tense standoff. Ultimately, Maota reveals that the city’s people achieved immortality by mastering a device—a "clock" that allows minds to transcend physical space and time. After pressing the device’s button, Maota dies, while Michaelson experiences a mind-traveling sensation, gaining the ability to perceive the city from any point in space or time. The story concludes with Michaelson realizing that while he can now exist beyond physical limits, he cannot return to his original body or undo what has happened—highlighting the themes of human limitation versus transcendent consciousness.
