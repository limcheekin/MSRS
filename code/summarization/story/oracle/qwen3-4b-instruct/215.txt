The plot centers on Jon Karyl, a lone watcher stationed on an asteroid, who encounters a hostile alien race known as the Steel-Blues. These metallic, telepathic beings land on the asteroid and begin a 21-day torture experiment on Karyl, using a corrosive liquid that dissolves metal and causes severe physical degradation. Initially fearing for his life, Karyl remains curious and resists immediate death, realizing the liquid is not merely toxic but specifically designed to destroy metal—making it a fatal threat to the Steel-Blues themselves. As the days pass, Karyl grows stronger in will and learns the true nature of the alien beings’ vulnerability. On the 21st day, the Steel-Blues attempt to enslave him by offering him an undiluted dose of the liquid, which he refuses and instead drinks to warn Earth's Space Patrol (SP) ship. Using a portable radio, he sends a desperate signal that alerts the SP ship. The SP ship arrives just in time, and the alien vessel is destroyed when its metal structure is attacked by a water-filled projectile that oxidizes and melts the invaders' ship. The story reveals that the Steel-Blues are metal-based beings whose very biology makes them vulnerable to Earth’s common acid, and their invasion is ultimately foiled by Karyl’s survival and timely warning.
