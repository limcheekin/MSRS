The equipment used in the narrative involving the lone watcher's encounter with strange beings on an asteroid includes a stubray pistol, a power pack radio, an atomic cannon, a televisor, and various instruments such as an air pump and a cylinder with a glass-like tumbler containing a corrosive liquid. The Steel-Blues also use metal clubs, tentacles, and a force field to interact with the environment and Jon Karyl. Inside the torture chamber, a plastic igloo is constructed with a simulated atmosphere and a cylinder that sprouts tentacles to deliver the liquid acid. The liquid itself, described as a corrosive acid, is contained in a glass tumbler and administered via a cylinder mechanism. These tools and devices serve both as weapons and as instruments of psychological and physical torture, highlighting the technological sophistication and alien nature of the Steel-Blues.
