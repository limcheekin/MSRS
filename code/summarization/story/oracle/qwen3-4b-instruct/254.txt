The setting of the story is a combination of a modern library and an interstellar environment, primarily centered on Earth's urban landscape during the evening hours. The story begins in a quiet, book-filled library where Herbert Quidley, a man intrigued by hidden messages in books, discovers cryptic coded messages hidden in a copy of Hippolyte Adolphe Taine’s *History of English Literature*. This section takes place in a real-world setting, with specific details about the library, its shelves, and the movements of characters like Quidley and the mysterious girl Kay Smith. As the story progresses, the setting shifts dramatically to an alien world—Fieu Dayol, a distant planet. The narrative reveals that Kay and her companions are part of an interstellar society where women are in short supply and must seek mates ("wotnids") from other worlds. The final act unfolds at a hidden, spherical ship on Fieu Dayol, blending science fiction elements with the initial grounded library setting. Thus, the story’s setting is dual: a realistic, urban library atmosphere transitions into a futuristic, interstellar one, forming a cohesive backdrop for its mystery and romantic intrigue.
