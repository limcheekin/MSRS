The story is set on Alpha Centauri III, a desolate, barren planet known for its flat, rocky surface and thousands of isolated lakes. The environment is stark and lifeless, with no running water, no moon to provide tidal power, and no natural vegetation or climate to support life. The only features breaking the monotony are these vast, stagnant lakes, which serve both as a natural landmark and a crucial element in the plot—being the site where Starrett Blade crashes his ship and where he is forced to hide from Devil Garrett, the notorious space pirate. The planet lacks any significant energy sources or infrastructure, making it an ideal location for Garrett’s hidden operations. However, the story reveals that Garrett has secretly developed a power system using electrolysis of water from these lakes to generate electricity, which could one day transform the barren planet into a thriving world. Thus, the setting is both harsh and symbolic—representing isolation and potential for transformation, where a man’s struggle for survival becomes intertwined with the possibility of revolutionizing an entire planet’s future.
