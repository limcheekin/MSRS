The setting of the story is the planet Minos, a seemingly idyllic and Earth-like world with vibrant, colorful forests, copper and purple foliage, and a warm, living atmosphere. Located 36 light years from Earth, Minos is home to a small, isolated colony of about 150 people who have lived there for three generations. The planet features a unique ecological and biological environment where human life has evolved through adaptation—specifically, the Mead clan genetically modified themselves to survive on native food sources that are chemically different from Earth’s. Despite its peaceful, harmonious appearance, the planet harbors a dangerous disease known as the "melting sickness," which killed most of the original settlers but left the Mead family immune. The story unfolds as explorers from the spaceship *Explorer* arrive and encounter this colony, initially unaware of its existence, and must assess the risk of bringing the disease into their ship. The setting blends natural beauty with underlying danger, emphasizing themes of isolation, adaptation, and the fragility of life in alien environments.
