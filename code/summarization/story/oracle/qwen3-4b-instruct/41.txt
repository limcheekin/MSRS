The setting of the story is a blend of modern urban environments and a hidden, advanced secret society operating beneath the surface of everyday life. The narrative begins in contemporary America, with characters encountering the mysterious "Perpetual Order of Seekers After Truth" (POSAT) through advertisements in magazines and newspapers. These ads appear in ordinary publications like *Your Life and Psychology* and *The Bulletin of Physical Research*, placing the organization within a realistic, accessible context. The physical setting shifts dramatically when Don Alford visits POSAT’s headquarters—a secluded, warehouse-backed location with a shabby reception room contrasting sharply with a luxurious, opulent inner chamber. This juxtaposition symbolizes the duality of the society: outwardly mundane and commercially disguised, yet internally advanced and secretive. The true setting, however, is not just a physical space but a time-displaced one, as POSAT is revealed to be an ancient secret society founded over four centuries ago during the Italian Renaissance. Its headquarters functions as a high-tech laboratory housing a functioning atomic reactor and a massive computing machine dedicated to understanding human motivation. The setting thus blends the modern world with a speculative, time-traveling dimension, where scientific knowledge has been preserved and advanced over centuries, hidden from public view until now. This layered, surreal setting underscores the story’s themes of secrecy, scientific supremacy, and the manipulation of human behavior through psychological insight.
