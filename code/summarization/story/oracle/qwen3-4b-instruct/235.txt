The plot of the story revolves around Roddie, a young boy living in a post-apocalyptic world where robotic soldiers guard the ruins of a once-thriving city. Raised by a nurse named Molly, Roddie is emotionally isolated because his friends—both human and mechanical—are indifferent to pain, hunger, or cold, which he finds shameful. When he encounters Ida, an "Invader" who is actually a human woman living in the ruins, Roddie initially views her as a threat. However, through their journey together, they bond and share insights into their lives. Ida reveals that her people are human beings striving to survive, unlike the mechanical soldiers who serve only to protect the city. As they travel toward the ruined Golden Gate Bridge, Roddie is forced to confront his own identity and values. He learns that true purpose lies not in domination or survival through force, but in empathy and shared humanity. Ultimately, Roddie chooses not to kill Ida, recognizing their mutual humanity and the futility of violence. In doing so, he undergoes a profound transformation—realizing he is a man, not just a child, and that compassion and emotional connection define what it means to be human. The story concludes with Roddie embracing his identity as a person with feelings, marking a pivotal moment of self-discovery in a world dominated by machines.
