Patti Gray, the nurse, undergoes a physical and emotional ordeal throughout the story as she travels on a desperate escape from a perilous situation on a distant planet. Initially, she is driven by duty to save Judith, who suffers from a life-threatening appendix issue caused by atmospheric poisoning. When she learns that the only viable escape route is aboard a small, untrustworthy surface ship piloted by the mysterious and controversial Rat, she reluctantly agrees to the dangerous journey. During the eight-day flight, Patti endures extreme physical discomfort—hot, fuel-tainted water, cramped conditions, and constant motion—alongside growing mental strain from isolation, poor air quality, and the relentless rocket drumming. Her health deteriorates, marked by bleeding, pain, and exhaustion. Despite these hardships, she remains resolute in her mission. As the journey progresses, she begins to form a fragile connection with Rat, learning about his past and understanding his motivations. Though initially skeptical of him, she gradually respects his courage and determination. Ultimately, Patti's experience transforms her from a passive caregiver into a resilient survivor, gaining profound insight into human endurance, survival, and the value of trust in the face of overwhelming danger.
