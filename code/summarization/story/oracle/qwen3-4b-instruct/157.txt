Venus in the story is a hostile, mist-shrouded world dominated by a thick, humid swamp and a vast, steaming sea, creating an environment of extreme heat, oppressive humidity, and decaying organic stench. The inhabitants are not human but evolved, semi-aquatic, amphibious creatures resembling large, armored saurians or brontosauri—massive, vegetative swamp dwellers with thick hides and powerful limbs. These beings are dangerous due to their size and clumsy movements, often damaging structures by accidental collisions. In addition, the planet hosts a wide variety of bizarre, venomous life forms, including swarming worms that burrow into skin and deadly aquatic predators. Despite these dangers, some creatures appear intelligent and social, engaging in combat, using weapons like spears and crossbows, and exhibiting complex behaviors such as hunting and capturing prey. The story suggests the presence of a possible intelligent, non-human civilization—though unconfirmed—given reports of being watched, especially near open water. The environment itself seems to adapt to and even transform those exposed to it, as Barry Barr undergoes radical physical changes due to Sigma radiation, eventually developing gills and becoming a full water-breather, severing his connection to human life. Thus, Venus is not merely a planet of alien life but a dynamic, hostile, and transformative world where biological evolution and survival are constantly at odds with human biology.
