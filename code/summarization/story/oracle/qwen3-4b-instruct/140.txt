The story revolves around Kevin, a psi-deficient brother in a family of highly talented individuals who possess telepathic, telekinetic, and other psychic abilities. Unlike his siblings, Kevin lacks any special powers, leading to constant feelings of alienation, mockery, and being labeled as an "atavism." Despite this, he struggles with a deep sense of identity and purpose, believing himself to be fundamentally unsuited for the world he inhabits. His life is marked by isolation, menial tasks, and limited social connections. However, when news arrives of hostile alien life forms from Alpha Centauri, the family's anxiety over potential war triggers a shift in Kevin’s perspective. Amid the chaos, during a crisis at the hospital, he unexpectedly demonstrates a powerful healing ability—restoring injured people to full health instantly. This revelation transforms him from an overlooked outcast into the world’s only known human healer, proving that true power lies not in psychic abilities but in compassion and life-giving action. The story ultimately reveals that Kevin's "true" gift was never psychic, but his profound capacity to heal, challenging the family’s and society’s assumptions about what makes a person valuable.
