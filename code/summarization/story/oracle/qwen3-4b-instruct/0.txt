The story follows Noork, a man with a lost memory who is mysteriously transported to the remote moon Sekk, a small, habitable satellite orbiting the Moon. There, he finds himself in a jungle world populated by strange creatures, including the golden-skinned Vasads and the enigmatic "Misty Ones"—hooded, invisible beings who dwell on an island within a lake and serve a temple devoted to a bloodthirsty deity named Uzdon. Noork, initially mistaken for a threat due to his unfamiliar appearance and language, gradually gains trust with a golden-skinned girl named Sarna, who reveals she is a slave destined for sacrifice. Through encounters with the Misty Ones, Noork learns they are not supernatural demons but ordinary men, vulnerable to death and capable of being defeated. He uncovers their hidden rituals and the oppressive slavery system they enforce. In a climactic confrontation, Noork rescues Sarna from the temple's prison, defeats a priest who guards the sacrificial pit, and escapes with her and a slave named Rold. The story’s deeper twist reveals that Noork is actually Stephen Dietrich, a former pilot who had been searching for the Nazi scientist Doctor Karl Von Mark, whose experiments on invisibility technology had brought both men to Sekk. The final revelation confirms that the giant bird that carried Noork to Sekk originated from another world, and that the true conflict involves hidden scientific secrets and interplanetary warfare. Ultimately, the plot centers on identity, survival, and redemption in a hostile, alien environment where memory, truth, and power are intertwined.
