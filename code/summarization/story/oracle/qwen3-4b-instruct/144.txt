Kevin and his mother share a complex, strained relationship marked by mutual misunderstanding and emotional distance. From an early age, Kevin is viewed by his family as a burden—an atavism without psi powers—leading to a pattern of neglect, criticism, and emotional suppression. His mother, a psychiatrist, is acutely aware of his condition and constantly probes him, revealing her deep concern but also her inability to truly understand or accept him. She repeatedly dismisses his potential, insisting he is "just a psi-deficient," and expresses skepticism about his value, even as she privately recognizes his hidden strength. Despite this, the story reveals a quiet shift in their dynamic when Kevin demonstrates his healing ability during wartime. His mother’s reaction—her tears, her proud declaration that he has “the greatest gift of all”—marks a turning point. She acknowledges his worth not as a psi-being, but as a human with profound compassion and capability. This moment transforms their relationship from one of pity and exclusion to one of recognition and pride, showing that despite years of alienation, love and understanding can emerge through shared experience and the revelation of true potential.
