Hal is a young boy in the futuristic story *Time in the Round* who serves as a rational, mature, and intellectually grounded counterpoint to the protagonist, the Butcher (a nickname for the character known as "Butch" or "the Butcher"). Hal is portrayed as thoughtful and knowledgeable, possessing an understanding of complex scientific and philosophical concepts, such as time travel, the nature of the Time Bubble, and the difference between perception and reality. His role in the narrative is to provide balance and realism, challenging the Butcher’s extreme views on violence and warfare. While the Butcher is fixated on the idea of reintroducing violence in a future world where warfare is supposedly eliminated, Hal calmly explains that such ideas are based on outdated conditioning and that adults are naturally conditioned to reject violent solutions. He also demonstrates scientific insight about the Time Bubble's one-way nature and the impossibility of time travel. Hal’s presence underscores the story’s themes of growth, maturity, and the contrast between impulsive, pre-civilization thinking and rational, evolved perspectives. Through his calm reasoning and factual knowledge, Hal acts as a moral and intellectual guide, highlighting the importance of learning and development in shaping a peaceful future.
