The setting of the story is the United Universe, a vast, peaceful interplanetary society where all planets adhere to a unified system of customs, tabus, and laws established by the "Wise Ones" to prevent war. This setting is primarily depicted through the city of Portyork, the largest spaceport in the universe, which serves as a bustling, cosmopolitan hub filled with diverse extraterrestrial beings, each with unique physical traits, cultural norms, and social rules. The story unfolds as Michael Frey, a young man recently expelled from a primitive Brotherhood, navigates this complex society for the first time. The environment is richly detailed with vivid descriptions of architecture, clothing, public behaviors, and commercial advertisements, all governed by strict universal tabus—such as prohibitions on walking, eating in public, using certain words, or wearing specific colors. These customs are not arbitrary but are deeply rooted in a philosophy of mutual respect and tolerance, enforced by the universal principle of "Do unto others as you would be done by." The setting emphasizes both the beauty and absurdity of a society that has achieved peace through uniformity, while simultaneously highlighting the cultural shock and confusion experienced by newcomers like Michael. Ultimately, the story is set in a futuristic, interplanetary civilization where harmony is maintained through rigid adherence to shared social codes, making Portyork a microcosm of the entire United Universe.
