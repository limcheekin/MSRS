The story follows Barry Barr, an Earthman aboard a spacecraft destined for Venus, who faces extreme challenges after a meteorite strike damages the ship’s systems. Forced to enter the hostile Venusian environment, Barry is exposed to Sigma radiation, which causes profound and irreversible biological transformations. Initially suffering from respiratory distress due to a leak in his suit's shielding, Barry begins to develop gills and other aquatic adaptations, transforming him into a water-breather. This transformation isolates him from his fellow Earthmen, who remain grounded in terrestrial biology and culture. As he struggles with both physical changes and psychological turmoil—especially after Dorothy Voorhees reveals a fabricated letter implicating Robson Hind, a rival crew member—Barry’s identity fractures. Ultimately, he sinks into the Venusian swamp and becomes fully adapted to underwater life, surviving by breathing through gills and living among alien aquatic creatures. The plot explores themes of isolation, transformation, identity, and the dangers of unchecked ambition, as Barry is forever changed—both biologically and emotionally—by his encounter with a hostile, alien world.
