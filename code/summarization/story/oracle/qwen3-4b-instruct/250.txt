The plot revolves around Herbert Quidley, a man with a passion for old books and young women, who discovers a mysterious code hidden in a library book—specifically, a folded sheet of yellow paper containing cryptic messages encoded with the phrase "asdf ;lkj" and references to names like "Cai," "Klio," "Yoolna," and "Gorka." These messages appear to be part of a secret communication system. Quidley notices that a young woman named Kay Smith repeatedly visits the library, takes the same book (Taine’s *History of English Literature*), and surreptitiously inserts or removes the coded paper. He begins to deduce that the code is a disguised message from a secret society, and through analysis, realizes that the names correspond to different members of an interstellar group. The society, known as the "snoll doper" network, consists of women from the planet Fieu Dayol who seek male partners ("wotnids") on other worlds due to gender imbalances. The messages are not romantic but requisitions for potential mates. Kay reveals that she is a "stock girl" on a ship traveling to Fieu Dayol, and the coded messages are part of a covert system to find suitable partners under interstellar law. The story unfolds as Quidley follows Kay, learns about the society’s structure, and ultimately discovers that the entire encounter is a carefully orchestrated interstellar matchmaking operation, culminating in a dramatic reveal of the true nature of the "snoll doper" and the planet Fieu Dayol.
