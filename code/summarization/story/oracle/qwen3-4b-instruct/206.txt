Nan is a woman who represents love, emotional connection, and the human cost of ambition. She is Marty Isherwood’s girlfriend and a grounding force in his life, offering concern, affection, and reality checks. Throughout the story, she tries to understand and support Marty’s relentless pursuit of becoming a rocket pilot, expressing her fears about his obsession and the sacrifices it demands. When he dismisses the idea of marriage or personal fulfillment, she is deeply affected, revealing her emotional vulnerability. Her final appearance occurs after Marty's successful moon mission, where she confronts him, only to be met with coldness and detachment. She is ultimately left heartbroken and alone, symbolizing the tragic cost of ambition—when a person's dreams consume their humanity, they lose those who love them. Nan’s fate reflects the story’s central theme: in the face of relentless pursuit of dreams, emotional connection and personal balance are sacrificed, leaving behind a world of isolation and unfulfilled longing.
