Whonk is an elderly, heavily armored Fustian elder who serves as a wise, resilient, and fiercely protective figure in the story. He is not a political leader but a grounded, experienced member of the Fustian society who provides crucial insight into the youth's growing rebellion and the hidden dangers within the SCARS (Sexual, Cultural, and Athletic Recreational Society) group. Whonk’s relevance lies in his role as a key informant and ally to Retief, revealing the conspiracy orchestrated by the Groaci Embassy. He uncovers that the Groaci are secretly planning to destroy the VIP ship *Moss Rock*, which was intended to carry Slock, a youth leader, to a cruise, and that the ship is loaded with titanite—a volatile explosive. Whonk also discovers that the Groaci have been using deception, including disguising a shell-less Fustian (a man who has had his carapace removed) to infiltrate the group, showing their deep involvement in manipulating youth politics. His actions—such as confronting the youths, protecting the old Fustian from being harmed, and later orchestrating a symbolic revenge—highlight the tension between youthful rebellion and elder wisdom. Ultimately, Whonk’s knowledge and personal courage expose the political manipulation behind the SCARS sponsorship, making him central to the narrative’s exploration of power, youth, and deception in alien political dynamics.
