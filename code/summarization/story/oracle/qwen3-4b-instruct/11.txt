The Fustians are a long-lived species resembling turtles, with a slow, heavy gait due to their massive carapaces. Their life cycle is marked by delayed maturity; while they live for centuries, adolescence is a period of significant social unrest and rebellion, particularly among the youth. These young individuals—lacking carapaces—are physically and socially distinct from elders, exhibiting greater agility and a desire for independence. The SCARS (Sexual, Cultural, and Athletic Recreational Society) is a youth group that represents this emerging social dynamic, driven by youthful energy and defiance of elder authority. Despite being labeled as "merely teenagers," these youths are politically active and organized, showing leadership and planning—such as the conspiracy to sabotage the VIP vessel *Moss Rock* with titanite. This reveals a complex social structure where youth form a counterforce to traditional, authoritarian elder control. The Fustians' culture values tradition, physical strength, and ritual, including ceremonial vengeance, which is deeply tied to their identity. However, the story exposes a critical flaw: the youth are not truly adolescents, as some have been surgically de-carapaced, indicating that the social dynamics are not just developmental but involve manipulation and hidden agendas. The Fustians' culture thus balances ancient traditions with emerging rebellion, making their life cycle both a biological and social struggle between age, power, and freedom.
