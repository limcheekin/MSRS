Mr. Michaelson is an archeologist from Earth who has traveled to the mysterious ancient city on Alpha Centaurus II. He is portrayed as intelligent, curious, and passionate about discovery, showing a deep fascination with the ruins and artifacts he finds. Despite being referred to by the native Maota as "Mr. Earthgod," Michaelson firmly rejects that title, emphasizing his humanity—his emotions, vulnerabilities, and capacity for fear and empathy. He is emotionally grounded, capable of both humor and sorrow, and driven by a desire to preserve and understand the ancient civilization’s culture and technology. His personality contrasts sharply with Maota’s spiritual, superstitious, and protective stance toward the city. While Michaelson is initially dismissive of the idea of spirits or supernatural forces, he gradually becomes deeply affected by the living presence of the talking book and the warm, vibrating clock, leading to a profound realization about consciousness and existence beyond physical form. Ultimately, Michaelson embodies human curiosity and the struggle to reconcile science with the unknown, making him a complex, thoughtful, and emotionally resonant figure in the story.
