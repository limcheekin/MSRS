The plot revolves around a soldier, Captain Dylan, who arrives at a peaceful, isolated colony to deliver a dire warning: an intelligent alien threat is imminent and has already cut the bomb detonator wire in the colony’s center, preventing a defensive explosion. The colonists, deeply conditioned to peace and distrustful of soldiers, initially resist the soldier’s presence and question his authority. As Dylan explains the gravity of the situation, tension rises, fueled by resentment and fear. The colonists begin to suspect one of their own may have sabotaged the bomb wire, leading to suspicion and internal conflict. Dylan reveals that the wire was cut with precise knowledge of the colony’s layout and security measures, indicating an intelligent, non-human agent—likely alien—was responsible. The story unfolds as the colonists grow more fearful, and the alien, observing from a hidden location beneath a tree, adapts its plan to attack at dusk, knowing the humans are preparing to evacuate. Ultimately, Dylan realizes the colony is doomed: the ship cannot carry everyone, and the final evacuation fails when the ship cannot lift off. In the end, Dylan is left behind, haunted by grief over the loss of his only friend, Bossio, and the painful realization that peace, though noble, is fragile and often leads to isolation and despair. The story explores themes of fear, distrust, the cost of peace, and the inescapable presence of existential threats beyond human control.
