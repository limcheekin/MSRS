The story follows spaceman Chip Warren as he returns to the asteroid Belt after a successful voyage bringing ekalastron, a powerful new element, from a mine on Uranus. Upon landing on the asteroid Danae, Chip is approached by Space Patrolman Johnny Haldane, who warns him of a deadly threat known as the Lorelei—a beautiful, deadly lure in space who lures spacecraft into destruction by manipulating their navigation systems and murdering crews. Chip initially dismisses the warning, but when Haldane is suddenly murdered by a mysterious man with a scar, Chip realizes the danger is real. He and his friends, Syd Palmer and Salvation Smith, begin to suspect that Chip may be wrongly accused of the murder, especially after the bartender falsely claims Chip was seen committing the crime. As they investigate, they are drawn into a larger conspiracy, and through a series of events including a crash caused by a tractor-blast, they finally encounter the Lorelei’s lair. There, they discover she is not a supernatural entity but a pirate group who use the element ekalastron for profit. The real threat is not the Lorelei’s song or allure, but the greed and violence of those who seek to exploit space resources. Ultimately, Chip and his companions learn that their mission—to protect the element from falling into the wrong hands—is critical, and they must confront the pirates and prevent further destruction in the Belt.
