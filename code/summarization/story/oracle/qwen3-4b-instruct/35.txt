The plot of *The Serpent River* revolves around Earth explorers, Captain Linden and Split Campbell, who land on an alien planet to investigate a mysterious, river-like entity known as the "Serpent River" or "Kao-Wagwattl." This vast, serpentine structure moves slowly across the planet's surface, appearing as a glowing, silvery cable that has been shaping the landscape for centuries. The explorers are guided by advance data from roving cameras that confirm both the presence of humanoid inhabitants and the existence of the river. Upon landing, they observe the alien people—closely resembling humans in appearance—engaged in a ritual at sunset. However, their peaceful gathering is disrupted when mobile "sponge-trees" reveal themselves as a camouflage for a hidden war party that launches an attack. The explorers use a ship siren and fire weapons to repel the attackers, resulting in injury to their guard, Gravgak. As the story unfolds, the team forms a bond with the alien inhabitants, particularly with Tomboldo, the leader, and his daughter Vauna. The central mystery—the true nature of the Serpent River—is explored through cultural interaction, as the explorers learn that the river is viewed as a sacred, living force of life and movement. The narrative concludes with the decision to take the explorers back to the alien world's capital, where they will ride upon the Serpent River as honored guests, symbolizing a peaceful alliance between Earth and the alien civilization.
