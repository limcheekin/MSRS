The plot revolves around Kaiser, a lone explorer stranded on the distant planet Big Muddy after his scout ship crashes. Struggling with unusual communications, he begins receiving messages from his mother ship, *Soscites II*, that are in baby talk and filled with strange, incoherent language. Initially confused and disoriented, Kaiser gradually realizes he is being affected by a symbiote—a virus-like organism living in his bloodstream—that is attempting to adapt him to the planet’s environment. The symbiote causes physical changes, such as color shifts and increased strength, and mental shifts, including emotional responses and even mimicking baby talk, which reflects its attempt to restore a state of childhood happiness. As Kaiser interacts with more advanced seal-people, he observes that they too have symbiotic relationships with the same organism, leading him to suspect that the symbiote is evolving his mind and body to become more like them. The story reaches a tense climax when the mother ship warns that the symbiote may alter Kaiser’s consciousness entirely, threatening to transform him into a being more like the native tribes. In response, Kaiser chooses to defy orders and fully embrace the symbiotic change, stripping off his clothing and immersing himself in the river, symbolizing his acceptance of transformation and the loss of his former self. The narrative explores themes of identity, adaptation, and the blurred line between human and alien consciousness.
