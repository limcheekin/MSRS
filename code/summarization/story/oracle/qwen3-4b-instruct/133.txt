It is important to Jimmie for John to continue playing with the band because John’s music is transformative and deeply resonant, providing not only emotional and artistic value but also a vital lifeline for their survival in The Space Room. Without John, the band loses its musical soul and becomes merely a collection of notes without harmony—described as “notes in a lost chord.” His presence restores hope, shifts Goon-Face’s hostile attitude from suspicion to delight, and brings genuine joy to the patrons, who enter a state of “ecstatic hypnosis” with his playing. Moreover, John’s music is central to their livelihood; Goon-Face’s contract depends on a steady, reliable bass player, and without John, the band faces financial collapse. Jimmie recognizes that John’s talent is unmatched—his bass produces “ecstatic,” “unheard-of” tones that elevate every performance. Even more importantly, Jimmie understands that John’s emotional and psychological well-being is tied to music, and without it, John would lose his sense of purpose. Thus, keeping John in the band ensures both their financial stability and the continuation of a unique, meaningful artistic experience.
