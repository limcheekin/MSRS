Ida is a human female who emerges in the dystopian setting as an "Invader," a being from outside the city who has stowed away on a boat and seeks to survive by helping the wounded. She is physically described as short, lean, with bare feet and only two hands, dressed in a shapeless doeskin dress. Ida is warm-blooded and sensitive to heat, which allows her to be detected by the city's heat-sensitive android soldiers. Unlike the mechanized soldiers, Ida experiences emotions such as fear, sorrow, and affection, which contrast sharply with the logical, emotionless nature of the machines. She is driven by a sense of purpose—to help the wounded and assert humanity’s right to the city—despite being labeled an enemy. Ida challenges Roddie's worldview by asserting that true humanity lies in emotion, loyalty, and shared struggle, not in rationality or mechanical superiority. Her presence forces Roddie to confront his own identity and emotions, ultimately leading him to recognize that he, too, is a human being with feelings and purpose. Through their bond and her emotional depth, Ida becomes a symbol of human resilience and the value of empathy in a world dominated by machines.
