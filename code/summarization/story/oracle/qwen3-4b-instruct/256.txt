Captain Midas is the elderly former skipper of the Martian Maid, a pioneering spaceship in the early days of space exploration. In the story, he is a deeply flawed yet tragic figure driven by greed and a lust for treasure, embodying the corrupting influence of material wealth. Though he initially believes he has discovered a massive alien vessel filled with gold, his discovery reveals a horrifying truth: the derelict’s metal is not inert—it absorbs energy from living beings, slowly draining their youth and strength, turning them into aged, decaying figures. Captain Midas himself becomes a victim of this transmutation; his hands grow yellow, wrinkled, and aged, mirroring the transformation of his crew. The nickname "Captain Midas" is ironic, referencing the myth of King Midas, who turned everything he touched into gold—here, it symbolizes both the temptation and the destructive consequence of pursuing treasure. In the context of space exploration and the pursuit of treasure, Captain Midas represents the dangers of greed, the hubris of human ambition, and the ultimate price paid when humanity seeks wealth beyond the reach of reason or morality. His story warns that such pursuits do not bring fortune but bring decay, both physically and spiritually.
