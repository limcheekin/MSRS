Gravgak is a tall, muscular guard of the Benzendella people, distinguished by his green and black diamond-painted arms and legs. His significance lies in his role as a protector and enforcer, tasked with monitoring the dangerous, moving sponge-trees that pose a threat to the inhabitants. Gravgak is also a key figure in the social dynamics of the society, representing a potential source of conflict and tension. While he appears loyal and vigilant, his aggressive behavior—such as rushing into battle without orders and his abrupt demand for a private conversation with Vauna—suggests a deeper, possibly rebellious or possessive motivation. His actions, particularly his confrontation with Captain Linden after the attack and his insistence on speaking to Vauna alone, hint at a personal connection or unacknowledged power over her. This makes Gravgak more than just a guard; he embodies the complexity and hidden tensions within the society, serving as a symbol of both danger and emotional undercurrents. In the context of the strange new world, Gravgak reflects the duality of protection and potential betrayal, underscoring the story's themes of trust, loyalty, and the hidden depths beneath surface appearances.
