The Lorelei in the story is a dangerous, alluring female figure who inhabits an asteroid in the space belt and preys on space travelers. She does not sing like her mythological counterpart but instead calls out to spacecraft crews, luring them to their doom. Her method involves locking ship controls and setting vessels adrift, often resulting in crew deaths and cargo theft. The story reveals that she is not a physical being but a metaphorical or supernatural force tied to the dangers of space travel. The Lorelei's lair remains hidden, but her presence is confirmed through sightings and survivor accounts, including a cabin-boy who escaped her and described her appearance. Her identity is linked to the theft of ekalastron, a valuable new element, which makes her a threat to both spacefaring and space-faring commerce. Chip Warren, the protagonist, initially dismisses her as a myth, but after encountering her in the ship’s viewscreen and hearing her desperate plea for help, he realizes the danger she poses. Ultimately, the Lorelei is revealed to be a pirate force, operating with men who have stolen the ekalastron, and her true nature is a combination of myth and reality—a deadly, seductive force that exploits human vulnerability in the vastness of space.
