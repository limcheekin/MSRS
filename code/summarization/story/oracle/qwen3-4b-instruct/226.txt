Edward C. Loyce is a practical, observant, and initially anxious resident of Pikeville who becomes the central figure in a peculiar event involving an alien invasion. He is a TV salesman who, while digging in his basement, misses a strange, coordinated event in which the town is taken over by parasitic, insect-like beings that mimic humans and control minds. Loyce's key characteristic is his practicality—he tries to fix problems logically—and his heightened sense of awareness, which allows him to notice the anomaly of a body hanging from the lamppost. Unlike the other townspeople, who are either under the influence or unaware of the invasion, Loyce remains uncontrolled because he was in his basement at the time, thus escaping the psychic reach of the invaders. His intelligence and vigilance lead him to deduce that the town has been infiltrated by alien entities from another dimension, which use mimicry and mind control to dominate society. Loyce also realizes the hanging man is a deliberate "bait" meant to draw out those who are not under their influence, confirming the invaders' strategy of testing for escapees. Ultimately, Loyce becomes a symbol of resistance, using his insight and courage to escape and rally others against the alien threat.
