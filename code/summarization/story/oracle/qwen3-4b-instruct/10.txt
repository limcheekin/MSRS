The story follows Ambassador Retief, a Terrestrial diplomat on the alien planet Fust, as he navigates complex political tensions between youth movements and rival embassies. The central plot revolves around the SCARS (Sexual, Cultural, and Athletic Recreational Society), a youth group that the Terrestrial Embassy seeks to sponsor in an effort to strengthen diplomatic ties. However, Retief becomes suspicious of the group's true motives after discovering that the SCARS are being used as a cover for a covert plot orchestrated by the Groaci Embassy. The Groaci, who have long been interested in Fust’s advanced technology and strategic position, plan to sabotage a VIP passenger liner, the *Moss Rock*, which is scheduled to carry a secret payload of titanite—a highly explosive substance—intended for interplanetary warfare. Retief uncovers this scheme through his investigation of shipyard plans, the youth's violent behavior, and a hidden clue involving stolen drums of titanite. He also discovers that a key youth leader, Slock, is not actually a young Fustian but a de-carapaced former elder, indicating that the Groaci are using deception and manipulation to infiltrate Fustian society. In a climactic confrontation, Retief exposes the conspiracy, leading to the cancellation of the attack and a diplomatic shift. Ultimately, the story reveals that the Groaci are not just seeking military advantage but are attempting to exploit youth movements to destabilize Fustian society and claim dominance. Retief’s investigative approach and skepticism of official narratives allow him to uncover a deeper truth: the political struggle is not just about technology or diplomacy, but about control over youth and identity in a long-lived alien civilization.
