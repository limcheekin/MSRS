The plot of the story revolves around an elderly man named Shano who boards a perilous space journey aboard the ship *Stardust* on a suicide run to Earth, despite being warned of a deadly Uranian fleet that patrols the space lanes. The vessel receives a red signal indicating danger, and passengers are instructed to return or travel at their own risk. Shano, weary from a lifetime of labor in distant colonies like Pluto, is determined to go home to die. However, the ship's crew discovers he has boarded despite the red signal, and they warn him to stay in his cabin due to the imminent threat. When the ship is attacked by the Uranian fleet, the crew is forced into silence and emergency shutdown to avoid detection. During the crisis, Shano begins to suspect a traitor aboard—specifically, a fellow passenger, Rourke, whose notched jaw triggers memories of rumors about spies. In a moment of rage and desperation, Shano attacks and kills Rourke, believing him to be a traitor. Later, when the ship suffers a catastrophic failure in its engine room due to a toxic gas leak, Shano enters the sealed chamber with his gum-clogged lungs, a rare adaptation that allows him to survive in the toxic environment. Using his knowledge of labor conditions and survival in gas-filled mines, he manually repairs the broken fuel valves, reactivating the ship's cosmic drive. His actions save the vessel, allowing it to continue its journey. The story concludes with Shano driving the ship forward, not for a peaceful homecoming, but as a defiant act of resistance against the threat of capture and destruction—highlighting the theme of resilience, desperation, and the unexpected heroism of an ordinary man in the face of cosmic danger.
