The setting of the story is a mysterious, alien city on Mars, located along a canal in the red desert. This city is both alluring and menacing, constructed as a colossal machine powered by the collective imagination of its inhabitants. It appears as a fantastical metropolis with jeweled spires, minarets, and streets of fur, emitting a captivating, hypnotic music that draws people in. However, the city is not real in the conventional sense—it is a sentient construct that translates human thoughts into physical reality. The story unfolds primarily on the Martian surface, with key scenes taking place at the edge of the canal and within the city's walls, which are protected by a high, ornate gate. The city’s setting serves as both a physical and psychological landscape, embodying themes of illusion, desire, and the dangers of unchecked imagination. The red desert, the sloping canal, and the stark contrast between beauty and horror define the atmosphere, making the city a place of seductive danger that ultimately reveals itself as a machine built to preserve a stagnant civilization.
