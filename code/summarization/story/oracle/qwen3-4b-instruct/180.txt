The story explores themes of betrayal and survival in a harsh desert environment through the journey of Steve Cantwell, a former colony resident returning to his childhood village on Sirius II. After discovering that the village has been abandoned and its water source poisoned, Steve learns that the Earth colonists had fled due to a betrayal by Tobias Whiting, a wealthy former trader who secretly planned to sell their location to the hostile Kumaji tribesmen in exchange for his lost fortune. Despite initial anger, Steve chooses not to kill Whiting outright, recognizing the moral complexity of justice and survival. Instead, he helps Mary, Whiting’s daughter, confront her father's betrayal, ultimately allowing Whiting to die a peaceful death by sacrificing himself to mislead the Kumaji. Through this act, Steve ensures the group's survival by deceiving the tribesmen about their direction, enabling them to reach Oasis City safely. The narrative highlights how betrayal can be both personal and systemic, and how survival in extreme environments demands moral choices, empathy, and a redefinition of heroism.
