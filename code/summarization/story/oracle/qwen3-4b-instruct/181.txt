Tobias Whiting is the former colony trader and one of the wealthiest individuals in the desolate Sirius II settlement, who returns to the colony after its abandonment. Once a successful businessman with the Kumaji, he becomes bitter and disillusioned, believing he was cheated out of his fortune. When the colony's water is poisoned and the Earth colonists are forced to flee, Whiting decides to abandon the group and take Steve Cantwell’s unicopter to retrieve his lost wealth by selling information about the refugees’ location to the Kumaji. He disappears during the night, taking the craft, and is presumed to have reached the Kumaji base. However, when Steve and Mary find the unicopter, they discover Whiting has survived and is still in possession of the information. In a moment of moral conflict, Whiting confesses he did not tell the Kumaji yet, but insists on keeping his silence to protect Mary from torture. Realizing the danger, Steve ultimately intervenes by attacking Whiting and a guard to protect Mary, allowing Whiting to survive long enough to give crucial false information—directing the Kumaji away from the fleeing colonists. Though he initially seeks personal gain, Whiting’s final act of self-sacrifice—faking betrayal and dying with his mission misdirected—allows the refugees to escape safely. His actions, though driven by greed, ultimately serve a greater survival purpose.
