Star possesses and uses two primary pieces of equipment throughout his struggles against space pirates: an electron knife and a jet-gun. The electron knife, a sharp, high-velocity weapon, is used for close-quarters combat and is central to his identity as "Death Star." It is frequently deployed in deadly, split-second attacks, such as when he fatally wounds a pirate officer or uses it to strike Garrett during the final confrontation. The jet-gun, a more powerful ranged weapon, is also a key tool, allowing Star to fire explosive blasts at enemies. However, its use is limited by the need to reload, which he exploits strategically—such as hiding behind cover and waiting for the perfect moment to fire. Additionally, Star uses a small, concealed jet-blast capsule, which functions as his "ace card," enabling a surprise attack that proves decisive in defeating Devil Garrett. This compact weapon, resembling a fountain pen, is crucial in the final confrontation where it delivers a fatal strike to Garrett’s vital organs. These weapons, combined with Star’s exceptional reflexes and tactical awareness, enable him to overcome seemingly insurmountable odds.
