The plot of the story centers on Captain Steffens of the Mapping Command, who discovers remnants of an ancient civilization on Tyban IV, a desolate planet in the Coal Sack Nebula. The structures are vast, stone-built, and estimated to be over ten thousand years old, suggesting a long-standing, advanced alien race. As Steffens investigates, he begins to suspect that the civilization was not native but rather space-faring beings who may have settled and later abandoned the planet. His investigation leads him to encounter mysterious robotic beings on the planet, which are revealed to be the surviving descendants of this ancient race. These robots are highly intelligent, perfectly engineered, and capable of communicating telepathically. They appear to be constructed by a race known as the "Makers," who once inhabited the planet but vanished long ago, possibly due to a devastating war. The robots live in a world with no oxygen or vegetation, surviving in a carbon dioxide atmosphere, and are built to serve the Makers. When Steffens and his crew arrive, the robots welcome them, express curiosity, and reveal their deep sense of purpose: to wait for the return of the Makers and serve them. The story explores themes of existence, consciousness, and the nature of life and service, culminating in a profound realization that the robots, though mechanical, possess a rich inner life and a spiritual connection to their creators—blurring the line between machine and soul.
