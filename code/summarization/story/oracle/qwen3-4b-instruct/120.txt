The plot centers on Jeff Elliott, a struggling television engineer seeking a job after losing his lease and feeling disillusioned by financial setbacks. While dining with his wife Ann, he encounters a mysterious man named Greet Snader, who claims to operate a time-travel bureau. Despite skepticism, Jeff is intrigued and agrees to a "free trial" trip through time. Snader takes them to a futuristic, alternate timeline six years into the past, where they are transported to a society with advanced radiooptics technology but no color television. There, Jeff is recruited by the Continental Radioptic Combine to work on chromatic technology, which he resists. After a brief stay and a tense confrontation, Jeff and Ann are forced to leave, only to find themselves in a completely alien future where the United States is unknown and currency is unfamiliar. The authorities suspect them of fraud, leading to their imprisonment. The story ultimately reveals that time travel operates not as a linear journey but as movement through a dimension where events are fixed, and people in different times have no knowledge of past or future events. The complications arise from Jeff's confusion about reality, identity, and the nature of time, exposing both the illusion and the deeper philosophical implications of time travel.
