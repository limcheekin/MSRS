The story follows a young boy named the Butcher, who lives in a futuristic world filled with peculiar creatures like uninjes—mechanical dogs designed to be harmless and uninjurable. The Butcher grapples with the concept of violence and civilization, expressing a strong desire to return to a time when warfare and bloodshed were normal, unlike the pacified, emotionless society he now inhabits. He is deeply influenced by a fictional character, Huckleberry Finn, whose life of dirt and rebellion appeals to his rebellious spirit. The plot centers on the Butcher’s attempt to enter the mysterious Time Theater, a place that shows scenes from ancient times, including a barbaric warrior culture. Though barred from entry as a child, he tricks an adult into letting him in by fabricating a story about being older. Once inside, he becomes emotionally and mentally immersed in the violent scene, especially when the hooded sorcerer commands the warriors to attack. In a pivotal moment, the Butcher encourages the warriors to attack, leading to a real-time "anomaly" where the Time Bubble's safeguards are triggered, and the warriors physically emerge into the auditorium. The Butcher, using his uninjes, engages in a battle with the warriors, who are unable to harm him due to an invisible shield. This culminates in a symbolic triumph over civilization’s suppression of violence, as the Butcher embodies a pre-civilization mindset that values conflict and physical action. The story ultimately explores the tension between societal conditioning and innate human instincts, suggesting that true understanding of civilization requires confronting its violent roots.
