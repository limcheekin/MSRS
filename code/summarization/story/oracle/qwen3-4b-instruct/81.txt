Rourke is a chunky Earthman crew member on the space liner *Stardust* who has a noticeable nick in his jaw, a physical mark that becomes significant throughout the story. He is introduced as the lieutenant who warns Shano about the danger ahead, expressing concern about the red signal and the threat of a Uranian fleet. Rourke is portrayed as alert and skeptical, embodying a sense of caution and duty. His nicked jaw is tied to rumors among laborers that such a mark indicates a person has betrayed or sold out to the Uranians—though these rumors are dismissed by others, including the captain. Rourke’s role shifts from a mere observer to a central figure of suspicion, as he directly confronts Shano, challenging him to stay in his cabin. Ultimately, Rourke is revealed to be a traitor who has planted an electronic signal device to expose the ship's position to the Uranians. In a moment of rage and desperation, Shano attacks and kills Rourke in the engine room, proving that the man was indeed working against the mission. Rourke’s traits—his physical marking, his vigilance, and his betrayal—serve as a foil to Shano, highlighting the theme of loyalty, suspicion, and the dangers of espionage in a hostile space environment.
