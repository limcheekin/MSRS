The story explores the complexities of marriage and the lengths one man, George Faircloth, goes to escape domestic turmoil by purchasing an illegal "Ego Prime"—a highly advanced android duplicate of himself. After years of marital strife with his wife Marge, who is constantly critical and emotionally manipulative, George decides to use the Prime to take over household responsibilities and free himself from the constraints of marriage. He hides the android in his workshop and instructs it to maintain the appearance of a loving husband, including comforting Marge, making her happy, and even engaging in romantic acts with her. Initially successful, George's plan begins to unravel when he notices Marge growing unusually affectionate and docile—signs that suggest she has suspected the deception. The revelation comes when George discovers that Marge had been monitoring the Prime from the beginning and had used her knowledge to manipulate the situation. Ultimately, she exposes George’s scheme, revealing that she never truly wanted escape from marriage but rather a deeper understanding of his affection. In the end, Marge returns, and the story ends on a note of reconciliation, showing that true love and emotional intimacy cannot be replicated by technology—only by genuine human connection. The narrative critiques the illusion of freedom through artificial means and highlights the depth of marital bonds.
