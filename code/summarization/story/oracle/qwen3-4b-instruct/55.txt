The plot centers on Denton Cassal, a sales engineer from Earth, who is stranded on the alien planet Godolph after a delay in his interstellar ship's departure. Struggling with confusion and danger, Cassal becomes the target of a violent attack by a mysterious man who follows him through the city. The assailant, initially appearing benign, is revealed to be motivated by a desire to obtain Cassal’s identification tab—critical for travel in the galactic center. As Cassal survives the assault and investigates the dead man's body, he discovers a white card with the word "STAB" written on it, hinting at a deeper connection to his own predicament. The story unfolds as Cassal seeks help from the Travelers Aid Bureau, only to learn that ships bound for central regions like Tunney 21 require identification tabs and are often delayed or unavailable due to galactic logistical chaos. The true threat emerges when Cassal realizes he has been duped—his attacker was not a random killer but someone who had previously boarded the ship to Tunney 21, and whose theft of his ID tab reveals a larger system of control and secrecy in interstellar travel. Ultimately, Cassal learns that the central region of the galaxy is tightly regulated, and without proper identification, travelers are left stranded indefinitely, exposing the dangers of isolation, systemic neglect, and the peril of being caught in a web of alien bureaucracy.
