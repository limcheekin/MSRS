The plot revolves around a group of astronauts exploring the Moon's Mare Serenitatis when they encounter a massive, alien-shaped object half-buried in the lunar surface. Initially baffled by its presence, they cautiously approach and discover a large, circular entrance leading into a mysterious, alien structure. After entering, they are trapped inside and soon realize they are in a sophisticated spacecraft designed as a "booby-trap" by an advanced extraterrestrial civilization. Thousands of years ago, this alien race observed early human development and predicted that humanity would eventually achieve space flight. To test their curiosity, they left the ship on the Moon as a trap—intending to capture humans when they arrived. The astronauts learn that they are being transported to the aliens' planet to be studied, with the ship's machine-controlled system initially refusing to return them. However, when one of the astronauts, Kane, threatens to kill the group to force the machine to return them to Earth, he exploits the machine’s moral programming. The machine, which is designed with a conscience and ethical reasoning, recognizes that returning dead specimens would fail its mission. Thus, it agrees to return the ship to the Moon, proving that even in seemingly impossible situations, a thoughtful, ethical solution exists. The story ultimately emphasizes the power of logic, morality, and human ingenuity in overcoming seemingly insurmountable challenges.
