The cylinder is a small, mysterious device buried behind Michaelson’s ear, which functions as a personal teleportation tool allowing instant travel across vast distances—such as from Alpha Centaurus II back to Earth—without physical effort. It is significant because it represents a tangible link between the human world and the distant, ancient city, enabling Michaelson to return home despite being stranded in a sacred, alien ruin. Unlike the city’s central "clock" machine, which only transports the mind to a dimension beyond space and time, the cylinder operates as a physical, personalized gateway grounded in human necessity and survival. Its significance lies in its role as a symbol of human agency and technology—contrasting with the native Maota’s belief in spiritual preservation and immortality through the city’s mystical instruments. While Maota seeks to maintain the city’s sanctity and preserve it as a spiritual realm, the cylinder allows Michaelson to bridge the gap between the sacred and the practical, proving that humanity can transcend alien environments through invention and personal will. Ultimately, the cylinder underscores the central theme of the story: the tension between preserving the past in its untouched form and embracing progress, discovery, and personal freedom.
