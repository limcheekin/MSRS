Jon Karyl and the Steel-Blues first encounter each other during a tense and unexpected situation on an asteroid when Jon, a Lone Watcher, is fleeing from six steel-blue creatures that emerge from a blue spaceship. Initially, Jon believes the Steel-Blues to be hostile, reacting with fear and urgency. However, as the story progresses, their relationship evolves from one of suspicion and danger to one of mutual curiosity and understanding. The Steel-Blues, who are revealed to be advanced robotic beings made of metal and capable of telepathic communication, do not intend to harm Jon but instead subject him to a slow, corrosive torture using a liquid that dissolves metals. Jon, though initially terrified, remains alive due to the psychological and physical effects of the acid, which gradually weakens his body. His resilience in enduring the torture and ultimately warning the Space Patrol reveals a bond rooted in survival and shared intelligence. Ultimately, Jon comes to understand that the Steel-Blues are not enemies but beings from another world facing similar challenges—needing living space and vulnerable to the very substances they use to survive. Thus, their relationship is one of cautious coexistence, built on mutual awareness and the realization that both species are products of a larger cosmic struggle for survival.
