The history of POSAT, the Perpetual Order of Seekers After Truth, is revealed to be an ancient secret society founded over 400 years ago by a super-genius physical scientist and mathematician during the Italian Renaissance. This individual, who predated modern scientific discoveries such as calculus, quantum theory, relativity, and nuclear binding energy, recognized the destructive potential of atomic energy and chose to hide his groundbreaking knowledge to prevent misuse. He established POSAT to preserve these discoveries and ensure they would be safely revealed only when humanity was ready to use them responsibly. The society has operated in secret for centuries, maintaining its knowledge through careful selection of members who are drawn by curiosity or personal desperation. POSAT uses psychological profiling—via questionnaires fed into a vast computing machine—to assess potential members' behaviors and motivations, ensuring only those who can be effectively influenced by its principles join. While the society has achieved remarkable technical advancements, including a functioning atomic reactor and advanced computing, it has not succeeded in changing human nature, as evidenced by the continued development of nuclear weapons. POSAT’s true mission is to understand and influence human motivation through science, with the ultimate goal of guiding civilization away from self-destruction. Its history, thus, is one of hidden progress, strategic secrecy, and a desperate attempt to shape a more rational, peaceful world through deep psychological and scientific insight.
