The Grannies, also known as Granitebacks, are a terrifying and mysterious form of life discovered on the Moon. They are described as mobile, gaunt, and extremely durable, with exoskeletons harder than steel, diamond, or any known weapon-resistant material. These creatures are completely indestructible—no known weapon, including steel, flame, or electronic waves, can harm them. Their presence is a significant threat to lunar outposts, as they are aggressive, relentless, and capable of overwhelming human forces. Despite their physical invulnerability, the story reveals a surprising vulnerability: the Grannies respond to sound, particularly music. When Isobar Jones plays his bagpipes, the creatures are stunned and fall into a state of peaceful stillness, listening raptly as if drawn to the music. This unexpected reaction proves that the Grannies possess a sensory capacity for sound and can be psychologically affected. Their apparent "death" when the music stops—confirmed by Sparks upon arrival—suggests that the music has a paralyzing effect on them, rendering them inert. Thus, while the Grannies remain a formidable and enigmatic danger, the story reveals that their susceptibility to musical stimuli offers a unique and unexpected method of defense in a lunar outpost threatened by these unstoppable, stone-like beings.
