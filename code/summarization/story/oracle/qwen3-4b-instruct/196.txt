The setting of the story is a distant, alien planet named Big Muddy, characterized by its wet, humid, and warm climate, with constant rain and a dense, muddy environment. The planet features a river system inhabited by seal-like native creatures who live in haphazard, dome-shaped mud dwellings built from packed earth and river weed. The environment is both physically challenging and biologically unique, with the natives exhibiting varying levels of intelligence and adaptability. The primary location of action is a crashed scout ship where the protagonist, Kaiser, is stranded, isolated from Earth and his mother ship, Soscites II. The planet’s extreme weather cycles—shifting from extreme moisture to arid conditions—add to the environmental complexity. The setting serves as a backdrop for both physical hardship and psychological transformation, as Kaiser's interaction with the alien environment and his symbiotic biological changes mirror the evolving dynamics between humanity and an alien life form. The story blends science fiction with existential themes, using the planet’s alien ecosystem to explore identity, consciousness, and the boundaries between human and non-human intelligence.
