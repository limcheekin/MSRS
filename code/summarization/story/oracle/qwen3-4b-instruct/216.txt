The mysterious blue beings, known as the Steel-Blues, have distinct physical features: they are six feet tall with egg-shaped, steel-blue heads extending from crown to tips of their walking appendages. They possess no traditional legs but instead have many-jointed, independent tentacles that stretch and shrink while maintaining balance. Their heads feature an elliptical lens covering half the head, with converging ends curving around the sides—indicating eyes located on the back of their heads, which are likely used for vision. These creatures are described as robot-like, with no visible mouths or lips, communicating telepathically through mental language. Their bodies are metal-based and vulnerable to corrosive liquids, which is central to their torture method. The most prominent physical trait is their use of tentacles for movement, manipulation, and interaction, such as holding objects or restraining the protagonist. Their overall appearance combines a metallic, mechanical form with alien, organic-like adaptability, emphasizing their otherworldly nature.
