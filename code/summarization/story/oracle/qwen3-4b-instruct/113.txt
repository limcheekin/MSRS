The setting of the story is a future Earth colonization scenario in which men and women from different solar systems face challenges in forming companionships and families due to the physical and cultural separation between planets. The narrative unfolds on Midplanet, a colony world in a distant solar system, where male colonists are paired with women from Earth through a government-mandated selection process. This setting emphasizes the tension between personal desire for family and the rigid structure of interplanetary marriage systems. The story contrasts the isolated, frontier life of the colonists on Midplanet with the perceived sophistication and cultural appeal of Earthwomen, who are portrayed as glamorous and desirable despite their alien origin. The environment is both rural and futuristic—marked by primitive farming, logging, and a communal rocket port—while the emotional undercurrents revolve around loneliness, societal pressure, and the longing for companionship. The setting thus serves as a backdrop for exploring themes of identity, gender roles, cultural differences, and the human desire to build families across space.
