The mysterious "snoll doper" is a specialized device used by the women of the planet Fieu Dayol to select suitable mates from other worlds. It functions as a tool for interstellar matchmaking, designed to ensure compatibility by filtering men who refuse to conform to the sexual mores of their own societies. The term "snoll doper" is not a literal or physical object but a coded designation within the secret society's communication system, which operates in secrecy due to interstellar laws banning public appearances and local communication services. In the story, Kay Smith reveals that she is a "ship's stock girl" responsible for managing these matchmaking operations. The "snoll doper" is a small metallic device that extends into a tubular form resembling a shotgun, symbolizing both its function and its imposing nature. It is used to physically initiate the selection process, with the user (Kay) instructing the protagonist to "march" while the device is pressed against his back. Ultimately, the "snoll doper" represents a sophisticated, organized, and culturally aware secret society that navigates interstellar social dynamics through discreet, coded communication and selective mating practices.
