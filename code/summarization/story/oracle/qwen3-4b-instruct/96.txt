The United Universe introduced a variety of cultural rules and taboos across planets to ensure harmony and prevent conflict, rooted in the principle of mutual respect and universal understanding. These rules were not arbitrary but evolved from the unique societal norms of each planet, enforced through the Universal Kinship system. For example, on Electra, appearing bare-handed was forbidden due to the presence of eight-fingered beings with green-scaled fingernails, making bare hands a violation of aesthetic and cultural norms. On Saturn, yellow was considered a color of death, so wearing yellow was taboo. On Theemim, speaking publicly about eating was deemed vulgar, reflecting their sensitivity to bodily functions. Additionally, the word "history" was forbidden among Meropians, who had no historical past and viewed it as an insult. Public walking was restricted on Fomalhautian grounds, and even the term "hotel" was banned due to connotations of entertainment. Marriage was outlawed, as it was seen as a form of slavery on Talitha, and instead, individuals were expected to share companionship in a communal, non-exclusive manner. These rules emphasized the golden rule: "Do unto extraterrestrials as you would be done by," ensuring that visitors respected local customs and avoided cultural offense. The complexity arose from the need to balance individual differences with universal peace, resulting in a diverse, yet harmonious interplanetary society where adaptation and sensitivity were essential for survival and acceptance.
