The setting of the story is the distant planet Alphard Six, a once-untouched world located two hundred twenty-six light-years from Sol, in the outer limits of Terran expansion. This planet was never colonized by humans before the Hymenop alien invasion in 3025, and it remained uninhabited until the arrival of a hidden human colony. The environment is described as a cool, green world with a single dun-colored moon, featuring a central city and twenty-seven agrarian hamlets surrounded by cultivated fields. The dominant visual element is the massive, grounded spaceship at the heart of the city—crude, rusted, and powered by continuous atomic fission—indicating a technological anachronism. The planet's atmosphere and surface suggest a primitive, isolated society that has developed in relative isolation over centuries. This setting is crucial because it contrasts sharply with the advanced, post-war Terran technology of the crew, highlighting the paradox of a human civilization that has existed for over a thousand years without space travel or modern communication. The story unfolds against the backdrop of this forgotten, reclusive colony, where the presence of an ancient ship and the society built around it reveal a complex alien experiment by the Bees, who used human subjects in a long-term study of psychological and cultural development. The setting thus serves as both a physical and thematic stage for exploring themes of identity, conditioning, and the limits of human understanding.
