The story explores a group of humans who land on Alphard Six, a distant world previously surveyed but never colonized, to search for abandoned Terran colonies. The crew, consisting of navigator Farrell, captain Stryker, and engineer Gibson, initially hesitate due to the dangers posed by unknown threats, particularly after a near-catastrophic explosion caused by an unexplained atomic projectile. As they investigate, they discover a large, grounded spaceship at the center of a city, which appears to be a Terran artifact built centuries ago and now powered by continuous atomic fission—an impossibility under known technology. The group debates whether the ship could have arrived through interstellar travel or if it’s a product of a Hymenop experiment involving human subjects. Ultimately, they learn the ship was part of an early 21st-century expedition that failed during its journey to Sirius. The Hymenops, who invaded and occupied the region, captured the ship and conditioned its crew into believing they were descendants of the original crew, grounding it on Alphard Six. Over centuries, these humans grew up unaware of their true origin, living in a self-sustaining, isolated society with primitive technology. The Bees, who had previously controlled the region, created this colony as a control group to study human behavior under alien influence. The story's plot centers on the mystery of how such a long-standing, isolated human society could exist and the profound implications of their existence—revealing that the Bees' failure to understand human resilience and adaptability may have been the key reason they eventually withdrew.
