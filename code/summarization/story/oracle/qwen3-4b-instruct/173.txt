The members of the Explorer follow strict health and safety protocols to prevent the spread of disease in the potentially dangerous environment of Minos. These criteria include mandatory decontamination procedures for all individuals before boarding the ship, such as showering with disinfectants, exposure to germicidal ultraviolet radiation, and treatment with sonic blasts and sprays of germicidal mists. Each person undergoes a series of medical tests, including blood and spinal fluid sampling, to detect any traces of the "melting sickness" — a contagious disease that previously wiped out most of the planet’s population. Even if an individual appears healthy, they must be thoroughly tested and treated with immunizing solutions before being allowed on board. The safety protocol is further reinforced by the use of a universal chemical agent, Nucleocat Cureall, which destroys non-human cells and prevents any alien pathogens from surviving in the human body. These measures ensure that no carrier of disease can introduce a plague to the ship or its crew, adhering to interplanetary health laws that prioritize absolute safety over convenience. The process is mechanical, rapid, and fully automated, minimizing human error and guaranteeing consistent protection against biological threats.
