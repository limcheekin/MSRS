Cassal is on his way to Tunney 21 because he has been assigned a critical mission by Neuronics, Inc.—to persuade a prominent researcher on Tunney 21 to join their staff. This researcher specializes in developing instantaneous radio technology, which could revolutionize galactic communication and give Neuronics a monopoly over interstellar trade and transport. Cassal’s unique advantage is his possession of Dimanche, an advanced instrument capable of interpreting human thoughts and emotions, which he believes will help him convince the Tunnesian scientist that Earth’s scientists are not primitive but highly sophisticated. His journey, however, is complicated by the fact that he has been stranded on Godolph for three weeks due to a delayed ship schedule, and he must now navigate both physical danger—such as the attack by a man who wants his identification tab—and bureaucratic hurdles, including the requirement of an identification tab to board any vessel bound for the center of the Galaxy. Thus, his mission to Tunney 21 is both a personal and professional imperative, driven by the potential for immense financial gain and technological breakthrough.
