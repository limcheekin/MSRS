The setting of *Saboteur of Space* is a near-future Earth in the year 819, centered on the sprawling, once-great city of Dynamopolis, which now lies in a state of technological decay and economic stagnation. The city is depicted as a desolate, dimly lit metropolis atop a vast plateau, with its power systems nearly depleted due to a prolonged energy embargo. The atmosphere is tense and oppressive, marked by flickering lights, the hum of distant machinery, and the cold, thin air of high altitude. The primary location of action is Pi Mesa, the former spaceport that once served as Earth’s gateway to space, now abandoned and overgrown with weeds, its runways deserted and its facilities dormant. This desolate industrial landscape—metallic, vast, and eerily silent—provides a stark contrast to the looming threat of a Martian power cylinder meant to revive Earth's industries. The story unfolds in both the city’s underground bars and the crumbling spaceport infrastructure, emphasizing isolation, vulnerability, and the fragile balance between human survival and corporate or Martian domination. The setting is not just physical but symbolic: a dying world on the brink of transformation, where the power source could save or destroy, and where the line between hope and sabotage blurs in the shadow of impending conflict.
