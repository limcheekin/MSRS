The webfooted character in the story is Maota, an old native of Alpha Centaurus II who serves as the keeper of the ancient city. He is described as a wise, deeply spiritual, and emotionally complex individual. Maota is older, with weathered skin and a life marked by decades of solitude and service to the city's spiritual essence. His personality is rooted in tradition, reverence for the spirits that inhabit the ruins, and a profound belief in preserving the city in its original, untouched state. He resists modern intrusion, especially scientific exploration, fearing that alien voices and physical contact disrupt the balance between the living and the spirit world. Despite his rigid stance, Maota displays moments of vulnerability—particularly when he expresses emotional attachment to the talking book, which he believes "loved" him. His actions, such as fighting Michaelson and later mourning the loss of the book, reveal a deep sense of personal investment and sorrow. Though he appears stern and uncompromising, his character is ultimately defined by a longing for connection, continuity, and the preservation of a lost culture's soul. He embodies both a guardian of tradition and a human being with emotions, fears, and regrets.
