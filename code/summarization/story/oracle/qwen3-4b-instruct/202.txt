Professor Venetti’s invention, known as the "Expendable," is a device that destroys matter by reducing its mass to zero, effectively eliminating it without producing energy or leaving physical traces. It operates by seemingly violating the law of conservation of energy, as it destroys matter without creating energy. Instead, the energy released during the destruction is converted into heat, which contributes to rising global temperatures. The device is capable of disintegrating objects—including human bodies—into non-existent matter, with no visible signs of combustion or fragmentation. Tony Carmen uses the Expendable to dispose of unwanted bodies by feeding them into the machine, which then vanishes them, leaving no trace. The invention is marketed as a revolutionary waste disposal and garbage disposal solution, capable of replacing incinerators and traditional disposal methods. However, the device’s use leads to environmental concerns, as the government discovers that its operation increases planetary heat due to the conversion of destroyed matter into thermal energy. Despite this, the invention remains highly effective for clandestine disposal and is later commercialized by Arcivox as part of a new line of appliances.
