The mysterious musician, John Smith, is a central figure in the story set at The Space Room on Mars. He is introduced as a strange, enigmatic man who plays a bizarre, ancient instrument called a Zloomph—described as a five-thousand-year-old bass fiddle that is actually a time-traveling device. John’s presence transforms the band’s performance and captivates the audience, proving to be an exceptional musician whose music evokes deep emotional and spiritual resonance. Though initially reluctant to join the band, John eventually agrees to play with them after being offered a steady job and housing. His connection to the band is rooted in both his musical talent and his personal struggle to return to his own time dimension by finding a specific "hole" in the universe. Despite the band's efforts to keep him, John vanishes mysteriously after a reporter confronts him, leading to the realization that he had fallen through a hole into another time dimension. Thus, the relationship between John Smith and the band is one of mutual admiration and dependence—musically profound, emotionally significant, and ultimately tragic, as John’s disappearance leaves the band forever bereft of their most gifted musician.
